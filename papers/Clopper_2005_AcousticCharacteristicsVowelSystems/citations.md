## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Peterson & Barney (1952)** - the foundational American English vowel formant benchmark this paper explicitly responds to and regionally disaggregates; essential background for understanding what "General American" formant baselines have historically meant.
- **Hillenbrand, Getty, Clark & Wheeler (1995)** - replication of Peterson & Barney with northern-Midwest speakers; found NCCS evidence; the direct methodological precedent for this paper's approach.
- **Clopper (2004)** Ph.D. dissertation - source of the Nationwide Speech Project corpus and additional per-talker vowel-space data (including formant trajectories) not published in this JASA article.
- **Adank, Smits & van Hout (2004)** - comparative study of vowel normalization procedures that justified this paper's choice of Lobanov normalization.
- **Labov, Ash & Boberg (Atlas of North American English, forthcoming at time of writing)** - source of the six-region dialect classification and predicted shift patterns used as this paper's a priori hypotheses.
