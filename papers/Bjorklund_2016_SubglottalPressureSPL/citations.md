## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Sundberg J. (2017/2018) "Flow Glottogram and Subglottal Pressure Relationship in Singers and Untrained Voices."** Direct follow-up to this paper, extends the Ps analysis to voice source parameters (MFDR, H1-H2, closed quotient) and compares trained singers to untrained voices.
- **Titze IR. (1994) "Principles of Voice Production."** Theoretical framework establishing the expected ~8-9 dB SPL increase per doubling of Ps for an ideal source.
- **Smitheran JR, Hixon TJ. (1981)** Foundational method for estimating subglottal pressure from oral pressure during /p/ occlusion -- the technique used in this study.
