## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **[34] Kodera, Gendrin, de Villedary (1986)** — the original phase-derivative formulation of reassignment that this paper reformulates. Read it to understand exactly what implementation problem the STFT-ratio form solves, and to see Appendix A's identification of the operators with group delay and instantaneous frequency.
- **[35] Rihaczek (1968)** — the Rihaczek distribution is the substrate for the spectrogram, Margenau-Hill, and scalogram operator derivations (eqs. 25, 26, 52). Needed to follow the Appendix.
- **[36] Friedman (1985)** — the only explicitly speech-focused reference here, on interpreting the phase structure of speech via instantaneous frequency. Most directly relevant to using reassignment for formant and glottal analysis.
- **[18] Rioul and Flandrin (1992)** — defines the affine class used in Sections IV.A-IV.C; appendix D supplies the smoothing-equivalence relation used to match the scalogram against the ASPWVD in Fig. 13.
- **[38] Peyrin and Prost (1986)** and **[39] Jeong and Williams (1992)** — discrete-time Wigner definitions and alias-free generalized discrete distributions. Necessary before implementing any WVD-family reassignment on sampled speech, since the paper's Fig. 2 algorithm assumes the analytic signal precisely to sidestep aliasing.
