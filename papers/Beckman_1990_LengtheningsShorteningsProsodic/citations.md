## Key Citations for Qlatt

### Already in Collection
- **Klatt 1975** — Vowel lengthening in connected discourse (cited as foundational work on final lengthening)
- **Oller 1973** — Position-in-utterance effects on segment duration
- **Pierrehumbert 1980** — English intonation phonology (the ToBI framework foundation)
- **Wightman et al. 1992** — Quantitative follow-up measuring boundary lengthening across ToBI break indices (not cited here but directly extends this work)
- **Edwards et al. 1988** — Articulatory timing and prosodic interpretation (same research group)

### New Leads
- **Cooper & Paccia-Cooper 1980** — *Syntax and Speech*: catalog of syntactic effects on duration including final lengthening
- **Huggins 1975** — On isochrony and syntax: stress-timing effects
- **Fowler 1977** — Timing control in speech production (dissertation on rhythmic timing)
- **Selkirk 1984** — *Phonology and Syntax*: metrical grid theory used as framework in this paper
- **Beckman & Pierrehumbert 1986** — Intonational structure in English and Japanese
