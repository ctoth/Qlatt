## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Peterson, G. E., & Barney, H. L. (1952)** - Already in collection. The canonical vowel formant reference that Hao's data should be compared against.

2. **Hillenbrand, J., et al. (1995)** - Updated American English vowel formant data with 45 men and 48 women. Direct comparison target for Hao's formant tables.

3. **Fant, G. (1966)** - Vocal tract size factors and non-uniform F-pattern scalings. The theoretical foundation for understanding how tract dimensions relate to formant frequencies.

4. **Story, B. H., Titze, I. R., & Hoffman, E. A. (2001)** - Vocal tract shape and voice quality relationship using MRI. More detailed area function data than Hao's gross dimensional measures.

5. **Moore, C. A. (1992)** - MRI-based vocal tract volumes and formant resonance correspondence. The most direct predecessor to Hao's volume-formant analysis, finding that oral volume predicts F1.
