## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Fant, G. (1960)** - Acoustic Theory of Speech Production. The foundational source-filter theory that Rothenberg extends. Already in our collection.

2. **Rothenberg, M. (1973)** - Inverse filtering technique for glottal airflow. The companion paper establishing the measurement methodology. Important for understanding how the interactive model was validated.

3. **Titze, I.R. & Talkin, D.T. (1979)** - Effect of laryngeal configurations on phonation acoustics. Detailed physical-acoustic model that provides complementary computational perspective.

4. **Flanagan, J.L. & Ishizaka, K. (1978)** - Air volume displaced by vibrating vocal cords. Quantifies the vocal fold displacement airflow component that Rothenberg identifies as a minor parameter.

5. **Fant, G. & Martony, J. (1963)** - Formant amplitude measurements. Experimental observation of f1-to-higher-formant interaction that Rothenberg's model explains.
