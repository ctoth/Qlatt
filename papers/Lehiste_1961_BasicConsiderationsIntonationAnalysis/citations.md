## Key Citations (Most Relevant to Qlatt)

- **Peterson & Barney 1952** — Canonical vowel formant and F0 data used as comparison baseline. Already in collection.
- **House & Fairbanks 1953** — Consonant environment effects on vowel acoustics, including prior observation of intrinsic F0. Already in collection.
- **Peterson & Lehiste 1960** — Duration of syllable nuclei, the parent study providing the corpus used here.
- **Pike 1945** — Foundational American English intonation model (4-level system).
- **Trager & Smith 1957** — Alternative 4-level intonation analysis for American English.
