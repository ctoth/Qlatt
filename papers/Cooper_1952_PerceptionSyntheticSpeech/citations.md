## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Delattre, Liberman, and Cooper (1951) / Le Maitre Phonetique No. 96** -- Contains the vowel formant frequency choices used in these experiments; relevant for understanding the acoustic targets underlying Cooper et al.'s two-formant vowels.
- **Jakobson, Fant, and Halle (1952) / MIT Technical Report No. 13** -- The binary distinctive feature framework referenced in the paper's discussion of binary burst/transition decisions. Foundational for phonological feature theory.
- **Liberman, Delattre, and Cooper (Am. J. Psychol., forthcoming at time of publication)** -- The detailed write-up of the burst perception experiment (Fig. 3 data). Important for understanding the full PTK burst dataset.
- **Cooper, Liberman, and Borst (1951) / PNAS 37** -- The preceding conference paper that introduced the pattern playback approach and early results. Provides additional context for the synthesis-by-rule approach.
