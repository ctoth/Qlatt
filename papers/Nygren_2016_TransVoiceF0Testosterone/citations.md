## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Cosyns et al. 2014** (ref 20) -- Cross-sectional study of 38 trans men after long-term androgen therapy. Includes F0 and F0 variation data; found voice change was insufficient in 10% despite adequate hormone levels. Relevant for understanding long-term outcomes and individual variation in voice masculinization.

2. **Klatt & Klatt 1990** (ref 14) -- Analysis, synthesis, and perception of voice quality variations among female and male talkers. Directly relevant to Qlatt's voice quality modeling, reports sex differences in breathiness, aspiration noise, and spectral tilt. Already in collection.

3. **Fitch & Giedd 1999** (ref 40) -- MRI study of vocal tract morphology and development. Provides data on how vocal tract length develops and differs between sexes. Critical for parameterizing formant frequencies independent of F0 in speaker personality models.

4. **Holmberg et al. 1988** (ref 13) -- Glottal airflow and transglottal pressure measurements for male and female speakers. Provides quantitative source parameters for both sexes. Already in collection.

5. **Damrose 2009** (ref 19) -- Quantifying androgen therapy impact on the female larynx with longitudinal measurements every 2 weeks. Case study but with very fine temporal resolution of F0 change during testosterone treatment.
