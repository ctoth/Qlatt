Hayes develops constituent-level extrametricality as a way to derive English lexical stress using binary feet, cyclic structure preservation, distinct destressing rules, and word-tree visibility. The analysis distinguishes nouns, verbs, and suffixed adjectives and explains secondary-stress inheritance while retaining lexical exceptions and dialectal variation. For Qlatt it supplies explicit rule ordering and domain contracts for replacing syllable-count stress assignment, with important prerequisites concerning morphology and underlying vowel/glide representations.
