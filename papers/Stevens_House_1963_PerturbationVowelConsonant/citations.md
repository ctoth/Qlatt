## Key Citations

- **Peterson & Barney 1952** [16]: Canonical vowel formant data used as baseline comparison
- **Fant 1960** [5]: Acoustic theory of speech production -- theoretical framework
- **Stevens & House 1955** [18]: Their own prior quantitative vowel articulation description
- **Stevens & House 1961** [17]: Acoustical theory of vowel production (predecessor to this paper)
- **Lindblom 1962** [14]: Personal communication on Swedish vowel undershoot -- parallel independent discovery of undershoot phenomenon
- **Chiba & Kajiyama 1941** [2]: Vocal tract structure and vowel theory
- **House 1961** [8]: Vowel duration data relevant to undershoot hypothesis
