# Abstract

We describe several ways of measuring spectral mismatch at diphone joins. We report on the effect for synthetic speech of choosing diphone boundaries so as to minimise the various different measures.

## Interpretation

The paper addresses a fundamental quality problem in concatenative diphone synthesis: spectral discontinuities at segment boundaries. By evaluating multiple mismatch metrics and demonstrating that optimized cutpoints significantly improve perceptual quality (84% vs 64% word identification), the work provides both a practical technique and a principled framework for thinking about segment boundary optimization. The variable-duration approach (measure 2.4) is particularly interesting as it shows that spectral and temporal optimization are coupled problems.
