## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **[1] Fitt & Isard (1998), "Representing the environments for phonological processes..."** — Already in the collection as [[Fitt_Isard_1998_RepresentingEnvironmentsPhonologicalProcesses]]; the theoretical companion paper whose Principle-I/Principle-II framework this paper applies concretely.
- **[3] Williams & Isard (1997), "A keyvowel approach to the synthesis of regional accents of English"** — Source of the original split-symbol (`|rr|`/`|r|`) approach this paper reconsiders and simplifies; the earliest concrete rule-mechanics paper in this line of work, not yet in the collection.
- **[2] Wells (1982), Accents of English** — Foundational lexical-set source for every keyword discussed (NURSE, NEAR, SQUARE, START, NORTH, FORCE, CURE, LETTER, PRICE, CHOICE, MOUTH).
- **[5] Brown (1988), "Linking, intrusive and rhotic /r/ in pronunciation models"** — Relevant if intrusive /r/ handling is ever added to a declarative frontend's rule set.
- **[6] Maclagan & Gordon (1996), "Out of the AIR and into the EAR"** — Source for the New Zealand SQUARE-NEAR merger cited in Section 3.2.3; useful for handling that specific accent.
