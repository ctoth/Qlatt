## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Babel, M., McGuire, G., & King, J. (2014)** — The original study being replicated. Contains the PCA methodology, acoustic measurement procedures, and the finding that voice quality measures outpredict F0/formants for attractiveness. Already in collection as `Babel_2014_VocalAttractiveness`.

2. **Fraile, R., & Godino-Llorente, J. I. (2014)** — Comprehensive analysis of CPP measurement, relevant for implementing breathiness/voice quality assessment in synthesized speech.

3. **Shue, Y.-L., Keating, P., Vicenik, C., & Yu K. (2011)** — VoiceSauce tool documentation for spectral tilt, CPP, and energy measurement. Defines the acoustic measures used.

4. **Pisanski, K. et al. (2016)** — Voice parameters predicting sex-specific body morphology, directly relevant to speaker profile parameterization.

5. **Feinberg, D. R. et al. (2005)** — Experimental manipulation of F0 and formant frequencies affecting attractiveness, relevant for understanding what synthesis parameter changes are perceptually salient.
