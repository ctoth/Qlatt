## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Taal et al. (2011) [14]** -- The original STOI paper, already in our collection as Taal_2011_STOI. ESTOI directly extends this.
- **Rhebergen et al. (2006) [6]** -- Extended SII (ESII) for fluctuating noise, a Class-1 method that could provide complementary intelligibility assessment when clean/noise separation is available.
- **Kates and Arehart (2014) [20]** -- HASPI hearing-aid speech perception index, a more elaborate model that may capture additional quality aspects.
- **Jensen and Taal (2014) [17]** -- SIMI: mutual information-based intelligibility prediction, by the same authors, potentially offering information-theoretic insights.
- **Cooke (2006) [8]** -- Glimpse Model, a fundamentally different approach to intelligibility prediction that performs well alongside ESTOI on modulated noise.
