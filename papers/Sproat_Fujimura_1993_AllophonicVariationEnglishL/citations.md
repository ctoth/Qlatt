## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Giles & Moll (1975)** - "Cinefluorographic study of selected allophones of English /l/" - Provides foundational articulatory data on /l/ allophones using cinefluorography, directly relevant to understanding tongue body movement in dark vs light /l/

2. **Browman & Goldstein (1992)** - "Articulatory phonology: an overview" - The theoretical framework underlying Sproat & Fujimura's two-gesture model; essential for understanding gestural overlap and timing

3. **Catford (1977)** - "Fundamental problems in phonetics" - Introduces the vocoid/contoid distinction that informs the dual-gesture nature of /l/

4. **Lehiste (1964)** - "Acoustical characteristics of selected English consonants" - Comprehensive acoustic reference for consonant formant data, including /l/

5. **Halle & Mohanan (1985)** - "Segmental phonology of modern English" - Provides the phonological framework for syllable structure that determines /l/ allophony contexts
