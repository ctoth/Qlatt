## Key Citations (most relevant to Qlatt)

- **Klatt 1972** — The synthesizer used in this study; direct predecessor to Klatt 1980
- **Lisker & Abramson 1964** — Cross-language VOT measurements; canonical VOT data
- **Liberman, Delattre & Cooper 1958** — F1 cutback as voicing cue (the finding this paper extends)
- **Klatt 1973a** — VOT mechanism in consonant clusters; reflex glottal closure model
- **Klatt 1973b** — Durational characteristics of consonant clusters; sonorant lengthening data
- **Fant 1956** — Formant level predictability; referenced for F1 transition intensity effects
