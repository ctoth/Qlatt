## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Titze & Winholtz (1993)** - Foundational study on microphone type and placement effects on voice perturbation measurements. Directly relevant as the key prior work this paper builds upon.
2. **Svec & Granqvist (2010)** - Guidelines for selecting microphones for voice production research. Provides the methodological baseline for device comparison studies.
3. **Parsa & Jamieson (2001)** - Acoustic discrimination of pathological voice comparing sustained vowels vs continuous speech with different microphones. Relevant for understanding how device effects interact with different speech types.
4. **Vogel & Morgan (2009)** - Factors affecting sound recording quality for speech and voice analysis. Broader context on recording quality factors beyond just device type.
5. **Hildebrand et al. (2020)** - Voice analytics in business research with conceptual foundations and acoustic feature extraction. Provides the theoretical framework this paper operates within.
