## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Allen, Jonathan, Sheri Hunnicutt, and Dennis Klatt. 1987. *From text to speech: The MITalk system.*** -- The foundational reference for the complete MITalk TTS pipeline including Klatt formant synthesis, letter-to-sound rules, duration rules, and prosody generation. Already in the Qlatt paper collection.

2. **Elovitz, H.S., R. Johnson, A. McHugh, and J.E. Shore. 1976. Letter-to-sound rules for automatic translation of English text to phonetics.** -- The NRL 329-rule letter-to-sound system that established practical G2P conversion for TTS. Already in the Qlatt paper collection.

3. **Klatt, Dennis. 1980. Scriber and Lafs: two new approaches to speech analysis.** -- Klatt's speech perception model with diphone-based acoustic-phonetic decoding, relevant to coarticulation modeling and phonological rule encoding in synthesis.

4. **Riley, Michael, and Andrej Ljolje. 1996. Automatic generation of detailed pronunciation lexicons.** -- Describes methods for generating multiple allophonic pronunciations from decision trees trained on TIMIT and automatic transcriptions, directly relevant to Qlatt's postlexical rule design (flapping, deletion, glottalization).

5. **Pierrehumbert, Janet B., and Stefan Frisch. 1997. Synthesizing allophonic glottalization.** -- Provides rules for synthesizing glottalization as an allophonic process in TTS, directly applicable to Qlatt's postlexical glottalization rules for word-initial vowels and phrase boundaries.
