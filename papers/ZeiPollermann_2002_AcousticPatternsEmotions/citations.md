## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Williams & Stevens (1972)** — Original model of physiological activation → vocal expression relationship. Foundational for understanding the arousal axis of emotion-speech mapping.
2. **Scherer, Ladd, & Silverman (1984)** — Tests two competing models of vocal affect cues using JASA methodology. Connects intonation research (Ladd) to emotion expression.
3. **Pittam (1987)** — Voice quality discrimination and perceptual ratings. Relevant for understanding how listeners perceive voice quality differences that may carry valence information.
4. **Simonov et al. (1980)** — Spectral analysis of emotional stress in astronauts' voices. Early evidence that spectral distribution carries emotional valence, which motivated the LTAS analysis in this paper.
