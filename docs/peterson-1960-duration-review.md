# Duration table corpus review

Peterson & Lehiste (1960), Table I, p.702, all-CNC-list column supplies the 15 strong vowel inventory baselines. Centiseconds are converted to milliseconds. The original table's /i/ and /ɪ/ map to IY (beat) and IH (bit); the extracted notes reverse those English glosses. Unstressed inventory targets remain unchanged.

These stressed CNC measurements serve as intrinsic references for the existing contextual rules, not connected-speech means. Inventory citations flow into selection and materialization provenance. The existing Hertz nucleus budget retains priority through a separate committed floor rule; unchanged nucleus-timing tests verify that contract.

This final comparison uses Crystal commit `995b9867`, including #61's per-phone floors. The neutral-rate corpus is identical to its Chen parent. Both default and fast summary goldens are regenerated. Four English compatibility hashes change; Beauty and DECtalk remain controls. Companion fast-rate and render reports list their changed segments.
