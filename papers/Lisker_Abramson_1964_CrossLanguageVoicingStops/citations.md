## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Liberman, Delattre & Cooper (1958)** "Some Cues for the Distinction between Voiced and Voiceless Stops in Initial Position," *Language and Speech* I, 153-166 -- Directly relevant perceptual study of VOT cues using synthetic speech. Establishes the perceptual boundary between voiced and voiceless stops along the VOT continuum.

2. **Lisker & Abramson (in preparation, published ~1967)** "A Cross-Language Study of Voicing in Initial Stops: Experiments in Perception" -- The perceptual validation sequel to this production study. Tests whether VOT category boundaries vary across languages as predicted by the production data.

3. **van den Berg (1960)** "Voice Production: the Vibrating Larynx" (film, U. Groningen) -- Demonstrates laryngeal vibration patterns relevant to understanding the glottal mechanisms underlying VOT, including edge vibrations during voiceless stops.

4. **Abramson (1962)** *The Vowels and Tones of Standard Thai: Acoustical Measurements and Experiments* (Bloomington) -- Companion work by co-author providing detailed Thai acoustic data.

5. **Han (1963)** *Acoustic Phonetics of Korean*, Technical Report No. 1 (University of California) -- Detailed Korean stop consonant acoustics, relevant to understanding the three-way contrast that VOT alone does not fully separate.
