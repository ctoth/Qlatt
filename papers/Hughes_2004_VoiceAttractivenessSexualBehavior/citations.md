## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Hughes, Harrison, & Gallup (2002)** — Predecessor study linking voice attractiveness to bilateral symmetry; establishes the voice-as-fitness-indicator framework this paper extends.
- **Abitbol, Abitbol, & Abitbol (1999)** — Hormonal mechanisms of female voice development; provides the biological link between sex hormones, vocal structure, and body morphology.
- **Collins & Missing (2003)** — Vocal and visual attractiveness correlation in women; relevant for understanding multimodal attractiveness cues.
- **Krauss, Freyberg, & Morsella (2002)** — Listeners can infer physical attributes from voice; relevant for speaker profiling from acoustic parameters.
- **Zuckerman & Driver (1989)** — The vocal attractiveness stereotype; foundational work on how voice quality shapes social perception.
