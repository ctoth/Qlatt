## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Gauffin & Sundberg (1989)** - The foundational paper establishing the single-line SPL-dpeak relationship that this paper refines. Essential context for understanding intensity regulation.
2. **Fant et al. (1985)** - The LF-model paper; dpeak is equivalent to Ee in LF notation. Already in collection as `Fant_1985_LFModel`.
3. **Fant (1995)** - LF-model revisited with frequency domain analysis. Already in collection as `Fant_1995_LFModelRevisited`.
4. **Holmberg et al. (1988)** - Comprehensive glottal airflow and pressure measurements at soft/normal/loud intensities for both genders. Provides the baseline data this paper extends.
5. **Alku et al. (1997)** - Introduces the PSP (parabolic spectral parameter) used here to quantify spectral decay of the glottal source. Potentially useful for voice quality parameterization.
