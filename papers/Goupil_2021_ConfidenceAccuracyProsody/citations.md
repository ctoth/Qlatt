## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Jiang & Pell (2017)** - "The sound of confidence and doubt" - Detailed acoustic analysis of confident vs. uncertain speech from perception perspective; complements this production study

2. **Kimble & Seidel (1991)** - "Vocal signs of confidence" - Foundational work showing prosodic markers present without audience; only measured loudness and speech rate, not intonation

3. **Brennan & Williams (1995)** - "Prosody and filled pauses as cues to listeners about metacognitive states" - Classic paper on how prosody conveys epistemic states; relevant for TTS implementation

4. **Ponsot et al. (2018)** - "Cracking the social code of speech prosody using reverse correlation" - Data-driven method for uncovering prosodic signatures; from same lab, complementary methodology

5. **Goupil et al. (2021)** - "Listeners' perceptions of certainty and honesty" - Companion paper showing listeners can decode these prosodic cues; validates that synthesized prosody would be perceived as intended
