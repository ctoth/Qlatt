Measures how glottal-pulse rate and vocal-tract length jointly determine listeners' judgements of speaker height and speaker category (man, woman, boy, girl), using STRAIGHT-resynthesized isolated vowels scaled on two overlapping 7x7 logarithmic grids that reach far beyond the natural human range. Vocal-tract length dominates size judgements (regression slopes on log VTL of 1.50 to 3.57 versus 0.01 to -1.04 on log GPR), the two cues contribute about equally to sex/age judgements inside the normal population range, and vocal-tract length decides the category for unnatural low-pitch/short-tract combinations, which listeners consistently label "boy". For a formant synthesizer this supplies the formant-scaling law (uniform multiplicative scaling with a 15.5 cm adult-male anchor), calibrated GPR-VTL target regions for man, woman, boy, and girl voice presets, and perceptual resolution limits of about 6-10% for vocal-tract length against 2% for pitch.
