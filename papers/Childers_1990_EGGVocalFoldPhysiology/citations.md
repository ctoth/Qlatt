## Key Citations (Most Relevant to Qlatt)

- **Childers et al. 1986** — The EGG model paper (EGG = k/[A(t)+C]), vocal fold vibratory motion model
- **Pinto, Childers & Lalwani 1989** — Formant speech synthesis quality improvement using EGG features
- **Childers & Krishnamurthy 1985** — Comprehensive EGG review
- **Hirano 1981** — RAP jitter measure definition, clinical voice examination
- **Scherer, Drucker & Titze 1988** — Direct measurement of vocal fold contact area with excised larynges
- **Krishnamurthy & Childers 1986** — EGG-based algorithms for measuring opening/closing instants
