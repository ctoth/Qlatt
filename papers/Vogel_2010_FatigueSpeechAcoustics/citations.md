## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Klatt & Klatt (1990)** - "Analysis, synthesis, and perception of voice quality variations among female and male talkers" - Directly relevant to Qlatt; discusses how aspiration noise affects formant trajectories and voice quality synthesis.

2. **Gauffin & Sundberg (1980, 1989)** - Glottal source waveform characteristics and spectral correlates - Fundamental for understanding how source modifications (like those during fatigue) affect the output spectrum.

3. **Laukkanen et al. (2008)** - Vocal fatigue acoustic measures in teachers - Provides additional data on spectral tilt (alpha ratio) changes due to vocal/physical fatigue, complementary to sleep deprivation.

4. **Nilsonne et al. (1988)** - F0 variation during depression - Relevant because depression and fatigue show similar acoustic patterns; useful for implementing affective speech states.

5. **Hillenbrand et al. (1994, 1996)** - Acoustic correlates of breathy voice - Discusses how breathiness (relevant to fatigue) affects higher formants and spectral characteristics.
