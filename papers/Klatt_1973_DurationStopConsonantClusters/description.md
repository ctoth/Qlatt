Spectrographic study of durational patterns in 25 different prestressed word-initial consonant clusters in English, measured from 3 speakers. Establishes baseline singleton consonant durations and derives 5 additive percentage-based rules for predicting how durations change in clusters: general shortening, sonorant lengthening after aspirated stops, ballistic shortening before stops, labial incompressibility, and dental-retroflex restructuring. Directly applicable to speech synthesis by rule for setting consonant durations in onset clusters.
