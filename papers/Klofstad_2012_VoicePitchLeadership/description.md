Experimental study showing that both men and women prefer leaders with lower-pitched voices, regardless of candidate gender. Stimuli: "I urge you to vote for me this November" manipulated +/-0.5 ERB (~20 Hz perceived shift). Lower-pitched female voices perceived as more competent, stronger, and more trustworthy. Lower-pitched male voices perceived as stronger and more competent by male listeners. Suggests voice pitch as biological factor in leadership selection.
