## Key Citations (most relevant to Qlatt)

- **[1] Klatt 1972** — The Klatt terminal-analog synthesizer model used as the vocal-tract backend. Direct ancestor of Klatt 1980.
- **[3] Coker 1968** — Articulatory vocal-tract model, alternative to terminal-analog approach.
- **[10] Hunnicutt 1974** — Letter-to-sound rules (several hundred rules), became core of MITalk G2P.
- **[11] Chomsky & Halle 1968** — SPE lexical stress rules, basis for the stress assignment algorithm.
- **[13] Halliday 1970** — Functional model of language (ideational/interpersonal/textual) used for F0 contour theory.
- **[15] Coker, Umeda & Browman 1973** — Bell Labs parallel TTS system for comparison.
- **[16] O'Shaughnessy 1974** — Consonant duration in clusters, directly relevant to Qlatt duration rules.
- **[17] Barnwell 1971** — Segment duration algorithm for reading machine context.
