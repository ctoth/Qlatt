## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Titze (1991)** - "A model of neurologic sources of aperiodicity in vocal fold vibration" (JSHR 34, 460-472) -- Directly models the source of aperiodicity; essential for understanding how to implement jitter vs. deterministic modulation in the synthesizer.

2. **Ishizaka & Flanagan (1972)** - "Synthesis of voiced sounds from a two-mass model" -- The foundational two-mass vocal fold model that produces the nonlinear phenomena described in this paper; already in collection.

3. **Klatt & Klatt (1990)** - "Analysis, synthesis, and perception of voice quality" (JASA 87, 820-841) -- Connects voice quality perception to synthesis parameters; already in collection.

4. **Steinecke & Herzel (1994)** - "Bifurcations in an asymmetric vocal fold model" -- Provides the mathematical model showing how left-right asymmetry produces the bifurcations observed clinically; directly relevant to asymmetric vocal fold modeling.

5. **Berry, Herzel, Titze, & Krischer (in press)** - "Interpretation of biomechanical simulations of normal and chaotic vocal fold oscillations" (JASA) -- Connects eigenmodes of vocal fold vibration to the bifurcation patterns, providing the physical basis for the desynchronization mechanisms.
