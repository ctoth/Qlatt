Hughes and Halle measured energy density spectra of gated fricative consonant segments from multiple speakers, establishing that fricatives can be classified into three spectral classes (labial, alveolar, palatal) based on the frequency location and prominence of their spectral peaks. They developed a three-measurement objective identification procedure using energy ratios across frequency bands that achieved 86-93% accuracy, and validated these criteria against human listener responses to 50-ms gated fricative segments. This is a foundational study for understanding how frication noise spectra encode place of articulation, directly relevant to setting parallel branch filter parameters in a Klatt synthesizer.
