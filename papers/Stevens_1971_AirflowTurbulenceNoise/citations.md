## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Stevens (1971 companion, ref 1)** — "Aerodynamic and Acoustic Events at the Release of Stop and Fricative Consonants" — covers the dynamic/transient aspects that complement this static analysis. Essential for stop burst modeling.

2. **Heinz & Stevens (1961, ref 15)** — "On the Properties of Voiceless Fricative Consonants" — direct acoustic measurements of fricative spectra that complement this aerodynamic analysis. Relevant for setting parallel branch amplitudes.

3. **Fant (1960, ref 13)** — *Acoustic Theory of Speech Production* — provides the transfer function theory used here for vocal tract filtering of noise sources. Already in our collection.

4. **Klatt, Stevens & Mead (1966, ref 10)** — "Studies of Articulatory Activity and Airflow During Speech" — experimental airflow measurements during speech that ground this paper's theoretical predictions.

5. **Rothenberg (1968, ref 23)** — *The Breath-Stream Dynamics of Simple-Released-Plosive Production* — comprehensive aerodynamic analysis of stop consonant production relevant to PLSTEP burst modeling.
