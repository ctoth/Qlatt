## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Stehr et al. 2021 (ref 12)** - Examining vocal attractiveness through articulatory working space. Directly relevant: quantifies the link between articulation quality and attractiveness.
2. **Kempe, Puts & Cardenas 2013 (ref 62)** - Masculine men articulate less clearly. Establishes the inverse relationship between masculinity and articulatory precision.
3. **Babel, McGuire & King 2014 (ref 112)** - A more nuanced view of vocal attractiveness. Already in our collection. Multi-dimensional attractiveness model.
4. **Pietraszewski & Schwartz 2014 (ref 87)** - Accent as a dedicated social categorization dimension. Relevant to how articulatory features are processed for social evaluation.
5. **Podesva et al. 2015 (ref 16)** - Social meaning of released /t/ in US politicians. Production and perception data on the same articulatory variable studied here.
