## Key Citations for Follow-up

- Liberman and Prince: predecessor's metrical representation and the stress rules Hayes revises.
- Kiparsky: cyclic preservation and secondary prominence.
- Selkirk: feet and prosodic categories, lexical productivity alternative.
- Nanni: adjectival -ative and the need for constituent-level exclusion.
- Trammell: evidence about readers' preferred stress for unfamiliar words.
