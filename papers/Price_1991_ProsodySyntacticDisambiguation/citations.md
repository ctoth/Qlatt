## Key Citations (Most Relevant to Qlatt)

- **Klatt (1975)** — Vowel lengthening at syntactic boundaries in connected discourse
- **Klatt (1987)** — Review of TTS conversion, prosody as major requirement
- **Pierrehumbert (1980)** — Intonation phonology framework (basis for boundary tone labels used here)
- **Crystal & House (1988)** — Segmental durations in connected speech
- **Cooper & Paccia-Cooper (1980)** — Syntax and speech production, duration at syntactic boundaries
- **Lehiste (1973)** — Original prosodic disambiguation study that this paper extends
- **Wightman et al. (1992)** — Companion paper on segmental durations near prosodic phrase boundaries
- **Selkirk (1984)** — Prosodic phonology theory (metrical structure determines intonation)
- **Beckman & Pierrehumbert (1986)** — Intonational structure conventions used for labeling
