## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Campbell & Mokhtari (2003)** — Establishes voice quality as an independent prosodic dimension in Japanese, directly relevant to our voice quality parameter controls
- **Ito (2003, 2004)** — Quantifies the relationship between breathiness and politeness in Japanese, providing aspiration noise measurements relevant to Klatt AH parameter
- **Teshigawara (2003)** — Phonetic study of anime vocal stereotypes including F0 targets for heroic characters (320–400 Hz), useful for voice preset design
- **Podesva (2007)** — Phonation type (falsetto) as stylistic variable in constructing persona, methodologically relevant for voice quality preset work
- **Stevens & Hanson (1995)** — Classification of glottal vibration from acoustic measurements, already in our collection (foundational for voice quality measures used here)
