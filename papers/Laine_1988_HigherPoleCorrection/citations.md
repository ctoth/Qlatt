## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Fant, G. (1959)** -- Original derivation of the HPC formula (eq. 4 in Laine). The foundation that this paper validates and extends.
2. **Gold, B. and Rabiner, L.R. (1968)** -- The digital vs. analog formant synthesis comparison. Claims digital systems don't need HPC; Laine shows this is incorrect for variable vocal tract lengths.
3. **Laine, U.K. (1982)** -- The PARCAS (PARallel-CAScade) terminal analog model. Structurally implements HPC through its parallel-cascade topology.
4. **Maeda, S. (1982)** -- Digital vocal tract simulation using T-network approximation. Relevant for understanding the relationship between section count and HPC accuracy.
5. **Flanagan, J.L. (1972)** -- Transmission Line model equations used as the reference system throughout this paper.
