## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Scherer et al. 1998 (ref 13)** - "Just Noticeable Differences for glottal flow waveform characteristics" - J Voice 12(1):21-30. Predecessor study measuring JNDs for Oq and Sq at single target values. Essential for comparing methodologies and understanding how JNDs vary across the full parameter space.

2. **Rao, van Dinther, Veldhuis, Kohlrausch 2001 (ref 11)** - "A measure for predicting audibility discrimination thresholds for spectral envelope distortions in vowel sounds" - JASA 109:2085-2097. Provides a computational model for predicting when spectral changes are audible, potentially useful for determining perceptibility of synthesis parameter changes.

3. **Veldhuis 1998 (ref 10)** - "The spectral relevance of glottal-pulse parameters" - IEEE ICASSP. Theoretical framework for understanding which glottal pulse parameter variations produce perceptually relevant spectral changes.

4. **Fant 1979 (ref 23)** - "Glottal source and excitation analysis" - STL-QPSR 1:85-107. Early analysis of excitation parameter E and its relationship to voice quality.

5. **Henrich 2001 (ref 18)** - Doctoral thesis on glottal source in speech and singing. Full methodology details and additional data not in this paper.
