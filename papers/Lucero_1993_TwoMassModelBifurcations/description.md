Analyzes the large-amplitude dynamics of the Ishizaka-Flanagan two-mass vocal fold model, locating all three equilibrium positions analytically for a rectangular prephonatory glottis and deriving a bifurcation diagram in normalized subglottal pressure and coupling coefficient. Shows the oscillation region is bounded by two Hopf bifurcation curves and is strictly smaller than the instability region of the rest position, so instability of the rest position alone does not produce oscillation; a convergent prephonatory glottis raises and a divergent one lowers the phonation threshold pressure, and the negative-differential-resistance oscillation theory is disproved because its weak-coupling region lies outside the oscillation region. For a formant synthesizer with a self-oscillating glottal source it supplies the full parameter set, closed-form oscillation-region limits, and a warning that the standard linearized phonation-onset criterion overpredicts where phonation occurs.
