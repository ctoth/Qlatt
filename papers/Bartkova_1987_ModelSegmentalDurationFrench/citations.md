## Key Citations for Implementation

- **Klatt 1976**: Intrinsic duration concept, segmentation criteria, English duration model framework
- **Klatt 1979**: English synthesis-by-rule duration model (primary comparison target)
- **O'Shaughnessy 1984**: French duration model (direct competitor/comparison)
- **Crystal & House 1982**: Segmental duration measurement methodology
- **Huggins 1971**: JND for segment duration (20 ms threshold used for validation)
- **Delattre 1939**: Vowel lengthening before voiced consonants in French (historical basis for m_c factor)
