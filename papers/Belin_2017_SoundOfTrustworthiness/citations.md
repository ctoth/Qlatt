## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **McAleer et al. (2014) [ref 4]** - The prior study that generated the original trustworthiness ratings for the 32 voices. Essential for understanding the full methodology and seeing the original "social voice space" dimensions.

2. **Scherer (1972, 1978) [refs 2, 6]** - Foundational work on voice-personality relationships. Important for understanding the broader context of what acoustic parameters correlate with which personality dimensions.

3. **Feinberg et al. (2005, 2011) [refs 11, 17]** - Studies on how f0 and formant frequencies affect voice attractiveness. Relevant for understanding the interaction between pitch and formant parameters in voice perception.

4. **Kawahara & Matsui (2003) [ref 16]** - The STRAIGHT analysis/resynthesis method used for voice morphing. Important if implementing similar voice manipulation techniques.

5. **Shue et al. (2011) [ref 21]** - VoiceSauce voice analysis software. Useful tool reference for extracting acoustic parameters from voice recordings.
