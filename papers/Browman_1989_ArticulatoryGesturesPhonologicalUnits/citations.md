## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Saltzman & Kelso (1987)** - "Skilled actions: a task dynamic approach" - The formal dynamical model underlying the gesture definitions. Essential for understanding the equations and stiffness/damping parameters.
- **Sagey (1986)** - "The representation of features and relations in non-linear phonology" - The primary feature geometry framework being compared. Important for understanding the articulatory geometry vs. feature geometry debate.
- **Stevens (1972, 1989)** - "The quantal nature of speech" - Provides the theoretical basis for why CD categorical ranges (closed, critical, narrow, mid, wide) emerge from articulatory-acoustic nonlinearities.
- **Browman & Goldstein (1986)** - "Towards an articulatory phonology" - The earlier formulation that introduced gestural scores and the GEST computational model.
- **Fowler (1980)** - "Coarticulation and theories of extrinsic timing" - Foundational work on coarticulation as inherent in gestural overlap rather than imposed by external timing rules.
