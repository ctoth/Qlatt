# Coker, Umeda, Browman (1973) — Automatic Synthesis from Ordinary English Text

Describes the Bell Laboratories text-to-speech system (1969–1972) built around Coker's articulatory vocal tract model, using a hierarchical pipeline from syntax analysis through stress/pause assignment to articulatory parameter control. Presents a vowel duration model T = K1 + S(K2 + K3*C) combining stress-situation and following-consonant factors, a 9-level stress scale with pitch and consonant-elongation correlates, and detailed rules for allophonic variation at word boundaries including VOT differences for initial vs. final stops. The paper demonstrates that articulatory synthesis, despite being newer than formant synthesis, benefits from natural prosody rules due to its inherent handling of coarticulation.
