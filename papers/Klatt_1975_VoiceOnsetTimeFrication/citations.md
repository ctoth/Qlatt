- **Lisker & Abramson 1964, 1967** - Foundational VOT measurements and cross-language comparisons
- **Halle, Hughes & Radley 1957** - Acoustic properties of stop consonants (burst spectra)
- **Fant 1960** - Acoustic theory of speech production
- **Stevens 1971** - Airflow and turbulence noise for fricatives and stops
- **House & Fairbanks 1953** - Consonantal environment effects on vowel characteristics
- **Klatt 1973a** - Durational characteristics of prestressed word-initial consonant clusters
