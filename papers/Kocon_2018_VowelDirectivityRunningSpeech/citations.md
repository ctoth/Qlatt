## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Monson et al. (2012a)** - Already in collection as Monson_2012_SpeechDirectivityHFE. The parent dataset for this study; contains voiceless fricative directivity and the original corpus description.
- **Chu & Warnock (2002)** - The most comprehensive prior reference data for speech directivity, with third-octave weighting functions up to 8 kHz. Would provide comparison data for validating any directivity filter implementation.
- **Flanagan (1960)** - Foundational analog measurements of mouth radiation. Already referenced in the Chalker 1985 paper in the collection. Important for understanding the theoretical basis of directivity.
- **Blandin et al. (2016)** - Models higher-order acoustic propagation modes in vowel directivity. Could inform a physics-based directivity model rather than the empirical lookup table approach.
- **Katz & d'Alessandro (2007)** - Finer angular resolution singing voice directivity. Useful for understanding the limits of 15-degree resolution data.
