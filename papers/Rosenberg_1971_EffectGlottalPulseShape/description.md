# Rosenberg 1971 - Effect of Glottal Pulse Shape on the Quality of Natural Vowels

Investigates which simplified glottal pulse shapes produce the most natural-sounding synthetic speech through pitch-synchronous analysis-resynthesis and A-B listening tests. Demonstrates that pulses with a single slope discontinuity at glottal closure (the "Rosenberg B/C" shapes) with ~12 dB/oct spectral decay are strongly preferred, and establishes that opening time should exceed closing time (T_P/T ~0.40, T_N/T ~0.16). This paper is a foundational reference for all subsequent glottal source models including the Klatt synthesizer source.
