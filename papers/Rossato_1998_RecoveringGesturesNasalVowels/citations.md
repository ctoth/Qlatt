## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Feng & Castelli 1996** (ref 5): Source of the area ratio parameter d and nasal tract area functions used in this study. Directly relevant for understanding acoustic correlates of nasalization in vowel synthesis.
- **Maeda 1988** (ref 7): The articulatory model underlying the simulation. Relevant if implementing articulatory-based vocal tract models.
- **Dang, Honda & Suzuki 1994** (ref 3): Morphological data on nasal and paranasal cavities. Useful for parameterizing nasal tract resonances in synthesis.
- **Chistovich & Lublinskaya 1979** (ref 2): Large-scale spectral integration (3-Bark smoothing). Relevant for perceptual modeling of formant proximity effects.
