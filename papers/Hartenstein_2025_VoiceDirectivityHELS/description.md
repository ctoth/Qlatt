This paper applies the Helmholtz Equation Least Squares (HELS) method to reconstruct full 3D far-field directivity of a human singer using a 588-microphone spherical array, proposing a mixed criterion combining cross-validation error and condition number stability for optimal spherical wave decomposition truncation order selection. Key findings include that voice directivity requires spherical harmonic orders >9 above 2 kHz, radiation is non-frontal at certain frequency bands (600-900 Hz, 1500-2000 Hz), and 3D measurements reveal significant off-axis lobes invisible in traditional 2D directivity studies. Relevant to acoustic radiation modeling in synthesis — the frequency-dependent directivity data could inform radiation filter design if position-dependent output is modeled.
