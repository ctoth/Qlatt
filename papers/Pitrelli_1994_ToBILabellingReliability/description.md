# Pitrelli, Beckman & Hirschberg 1994 -- Evaluation of Prosodic Transcription Labeling Reliability in the ToBI Framework

Reports the first large-scale inter-transcriber reliability study of the ToBI prosodic labeling system, using 26 transcribers at 14 sites labeling 34 utterances (489 words) from four speech databases (WSJ, ATIS, TRAINS, SAILOR). Establishes benchmark agreement rates: 88% on presence/absence of tonal elements, 81% on exact tonal category, and 92% on break indices within +/-1 level using a stringent transcriber-pair-word metric. Concludes that the ToBI convention and training materials are sufficiently mature for large-scale prosodic annotation of speech databases.
