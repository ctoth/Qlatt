## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Banse & Scherer (1996)** - "Acoustic profiles in vocal emotion expression" - Foundational methodology paper for acoustic analysis of paralinguistic prosody. Uses similar discriminant analysis approach. Essential for understanding the emotion-vs-intention distinction.

2. **Morlec, Bailly, & Aubergé (2001)** - "Generating prosodic attitudes in French" - Directly relevant to synthesis: describes a computational model for generating attitude-specific prosody in French TTS.

3. **Blanc & Dominey (2003)** - "Identification of prosodic attitudes by temporal recurrent network" - Neural network approach to classifying prosodic attitudes, could inform machine learning approaches to intention-prosody mapping.

4. **Pierrehumbert (1980)** - "English intonation" (cited indirectly via other papers) - Foundation for ToBI annotation system; relevant for mapping the findings to standard prosodic representations.

5. **Tanenhaus, Kurumada, & Brown (2015)** - "Prosody and intention recognition" - Recent review on how context modulates prosodic interpretation; relevant for understanding when prosody alone is sufficient vs. when context is needed.
