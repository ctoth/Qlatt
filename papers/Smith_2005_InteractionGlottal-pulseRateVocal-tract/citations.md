## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Smith DRR, Patterson RD, Turner R, Kawahara H, Irino T (2005), JASA 117:305-318.** The companion discrimination study. It establishes the 6-10% vocal-tract-length just-noticeable difference and the constraint that STRAIGHT resynthesis holds up only while glottal-pulse rate stays below the first formant. Both numbers are direct engineering constraints on a synthesizer's size control.
- **Fitch WT, Giedd J (1999), JASA 106:1511-1522.** Magnetic resonance imaging morphology of the human vocal tract across development. Source of the 15.5 cm adult-male vocal-tract-length anchor used to convert a spectral envelope ratio into a physical length, and of the length-versus-height curve used to name simulated speaker heights.
- **Kawahara H, Irino T (2004), in Speech Separation by Humans and Machines, pp. 167-180.** The STRAIGHT reference describing how a pitch-independent spectral envelope is extracted and independently rescaled. Read alongside Kawahara, Masuda-Kasuse and de Cheveigne (1999) for the original algorithm.
- **Peterson GE, Barney HL (1952), JASA 24:175-184** together with **Turner RE, Patterson RD (2003), JASA 33:585-589.** The classic vowel formant database and its size-focused reanalysis. These supply the man, woman, boy, and girl regions in pitch-by-tract-length space that this paper's ellipses encode.
- **Fant G (1970), Acoustic Theory of Speech Production, 2nd ed.** The linear scaling relation between formant frequency and vocal-tract length that the whole spectral-envelope-ratio conversion rests on.
