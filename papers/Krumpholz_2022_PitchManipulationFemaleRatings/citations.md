# Citations

## Reference List

Full reference list available in the paper (pages 14-16). Key references include works on voice pitch perception, cross-modal attractiveness, and sexual dimorphism from Collins and Missing (2003), Feinberg et al. (2008), Vukovic et al. (2010a, 2010b), Mook and Mitchel (2019), and others.

## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Feinberg et al. (2008)** - The role of femininity and averageness of voice pitch in aesthetic judgments of women's voices. Key prior finding that higher pitch = more attractive.
2. **Mook and Mitchel (2019)** - Used 6 manipulation levels (20 Hz increments, 160-260 Hz) for cross-modal effects. Larger manipulations than this study.
3. **Collins and Missing (2003)** - Vocal and visual attractiveness are related in women. Already in collection.
4. **Pisanski et al. (2018)** - Voice pitch as a stable acoustic-phonetic parameter for femininity.
5. **Vukovic et al. (2010a)** - Women's voice pitch is negatively correlated with health risk factors. Contradicted by this study's null health finding.
