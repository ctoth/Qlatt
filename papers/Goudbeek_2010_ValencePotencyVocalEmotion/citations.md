## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Fontaine et al. (2007)** — Establishes the four-dimensional emotion space including potency/control as a necessary third dimension beyond arousal and valence. Provides the theoretical grounding for this paper's dimensional framework.
- **Juslin & Laukka (2003a)** — Comprehensive review of vocal emotion expression research. Would provide broader context on acoustic correlates of emotion.
- **Yanushevskaya et al. (2007)** — Time and frequency domain analysis of voice source correlates of affect. Would complement this paper's spectral analysis with direct glottal source measurements.
- **Scherer (2003)** — Vocal communication of emotion review. Provides the theoretical framework (Component Process Model) underlying GEMEP design.
- **Tamarit, Goudbeek & Scherer (2008)** — Spectral slope measurements in emotional speech using the same corpus. More detailed spectral analysis that would complement these findings.
