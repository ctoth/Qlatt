## Key Follow-Ups

- **Heman-Ackah et al., 2003 (ref 22)** -- Standard CPP methodology with linear regression normalization, which this study did NOT use. Important for understanding the measurement difference.
- **Hillenbrand, 1987 (ref 21)** -- Foundational cepstral analysis methodology for voice signals. Defines the harmonic organization measure used here.
- **Hughes et al., 2004 (ref 2)** -- Establishes that voice attractiveness predicts sexual behavior and body configuration. Motivates this study's hypothesis.
