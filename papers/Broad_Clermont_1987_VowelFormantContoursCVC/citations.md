## Key Citations

- **Lindblom 1963** — Foundational work on vowel reduction and exponential duration effects that this paper extends
- **Ohman 1967** — Numerical coarticulation model with similar superposition concept
- **Delattre et al. 1955** — Original consonant locus concept used throughout
- **Stevens & House 1963** — Perturbation of vowels by consonants, additivity findings
- **Broad & Fertig 1970** — Earlier work by first author on formant trajectories in CVC, precursor to this paper
- **Schouten & Pols 1979a,b, 1981** — Spectral coarticulation studies, locus measurements via extrapolation
