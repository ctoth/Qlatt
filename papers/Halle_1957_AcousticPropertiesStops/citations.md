# Citations

## Full Reference

Halle, M., Hughes, G. W., & Radley, J.-P. A. (1957). Acoustic Properties of Stop Consonants. *The Journal of the Acoustical Society of America*, 29(1), 107-116. doi:10.1121/1.1908634

## Key Citations Referenced

1. Jakobson, R., Fant, G., & Halle, M. (1952). *Preliminaries to Speech Analysis*. Technical Report 13, Acoustics Laboratory, MIT. — Framework for distinctive features (acute/grave, tense/lax terminology)

2. Delattre, P., Liberman, A. M., & Cooper, F. S. (1955). Acoustic loci and transitional cues for consonants. *JASA*, 27, 769-773. — Haskins pattern playback experiments on synthetic transitions

3. Liberman, A. M., Delattre, P., Cooper, F. S., & Gerstman, L. J. (1954). The role of consonant-vowel transitions in the perception of stop and nasal consonants. *Psychological Monographs*, 379. — Perception of synthetic CV transitions

4. Hughes, G. W., & Halle, M. (1956). Spectral properties of fricative consonants. *JASA*, 28(2), 303-310. — Companion study on fricative spectra

5. Stevens, K. N., & House, A. S. (1956). Studies of formant transitions using a vocal tract analog. *JASA*, 28, 578-585. — Vocal tract analog formant transition studies

6. Radley, J.-P. A. (1955). The role of transitions in the identification of English stops. S.M. thesis, Dept. of Electrical Engineering, MIT. — Extended transition analysis

7. Beranek, L. L. (1949). *Acoustic Measurements*. John Wiley and Sons, pp. 538-542. — Measurement methodology

8. Stevens, K. N. (1952). The perception of sounds shaped by resonance circuits. Sc.D. thesis, MIT. — Critical band width for formant perception
