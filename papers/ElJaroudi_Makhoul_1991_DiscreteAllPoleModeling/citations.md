## Key Citations for Qlatt Project

- **[6, 7] Itakura & Saito 1968, 1970** — Origin of the I-S distortion measure and its use for spectral estimation/formant extraction. Foundation of DAP error criterion.
- **[11, 12] Makhoul 1975** — LP tutorial and spectral LP properties. DAP is presented as an improvement over LP.
- **[14] McAulay 1984** — Maximum likelihood spectral estimation approach that DAP relates to.
- **[13] Markel & Gray 1976** — Standard reference for LP of speech; DAP improves upon methods described here.
