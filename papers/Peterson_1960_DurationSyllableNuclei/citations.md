## Key Citations (Most Relevant to Qlatt)

- **House & Fairbanks 1953** — Direct predecessor studying consonant influence on vowel duration; this paper extends and quantifies their findings
- **Lehiste & Peterson 1959** — Companion paper with the detailed CNC word list used in this study
- **Fant 1958** — Provided the spectrographic equipment (Mingograph, 48-channel spectrograph at KTH Stockholm) used for data analysis
- **Classe 1939** — Referenced for the relationship between stress groups and syllable timing
