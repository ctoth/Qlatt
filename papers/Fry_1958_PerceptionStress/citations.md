## Key Citations

- **Fry (1955)** — The companion production study showing that duration and intensity ratios distinguish noun from verb forms in English. The analytical basis for the synthesis parameters used in this paper.
- **Borst & Cooper (1957)** — The Voback (channel Vocoder) used for the F0 experiments in this paper.
- **Liberman (1952)** — Pattern playback equipment at Haskins Laboratories used for the duration/intensity experiments (cited in text but not in reference list).
