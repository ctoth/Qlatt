## Key Citations (most relevant to Qlatt)

- **Pierrehumbert (1981)** — Synthesizing Intonation (already in collection)
- **Liberman & Pierrehumbert (1984)** — Intonational invariance under pitch range/length changes; basis for the F0 range formula
- **Scherer, Ladd, & Silverman (1984)** — Predecessor study establishing continuous vs. categorical vocal cue distinction
- **Laver (1980)** — Voice quality description framework
- **Crystal (1969)** — Assumed independence of contour, range, and voice quality
- **'t Hart & Collier (1975)** — Phonology of intonation; anchor point approach
