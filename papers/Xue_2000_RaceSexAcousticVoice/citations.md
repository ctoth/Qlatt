## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Flanagan (1958)** — Glottal source properties including the -12 dB/octave harmonic rolloff that explains the SPI-F0 relationship. Already in the collection as Flanagan 1958.
2. **Titze (1989)** — Physiologic and acoustic sex differences in voice; provides the anatomical/physiological basis for the acoustic differences found here. Fundamental reference for sex-differentiated synthesis.
3. **Xue & Deliyski (in press)** — Companion paper establishing that elderly speakers show poorer acoustic outputs than young/middle-aged adults; provides the aging baseline this paper builds on.
4. **Mueller (1985)** — Characterization of the senescent voice; relevant for age-based voice modeling.
5. **Awan & Mueller (1996)** — F0 characteristics across racial groups in children; useful for understanding whether the race-independence finding extends across age groups.
