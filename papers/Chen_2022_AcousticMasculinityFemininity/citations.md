## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Cartei et al. [1] (2014)** — Direct predecessor: F0 and ΔF correlates of women's ratings of men's vocal masculinity. Provides complementary manipulation-based evidence for the correlational findings here.
- **Pisanski & Rendall [4] (2011)** — Tests whether listeners prioritize F0 or formants for speaker size/masculinity/attractiveness assessments. Directly relevant to the F0 vs VTL relative importance question.
- **Munson [8] (2007)** — Acoustic correlates of perceived masculinity, femininity, AND sexual orientation. Extends the parameter space beyond binary gender perception.
- **Feinberg et al. [3] (2005)** — Manipulation study showing F0 and formant frequency changes independently affect attractiveness. Key evidence for F0/VTL independence.
- **Biemans [10] (2000)** — Comprehensive thesis on gender variation in voice quality. Likely contains detailed acoustic measurements we don't have elsewhere.
