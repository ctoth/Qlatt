## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Keating (1990b)** "The window model of coarticulation" — Primary theoretical framework for grammar-based coarticulation modeling; provides the window width concept useful for parameterizing coarticulation in synthesis
- **Lindblom (1990)** "Explaining phonetic variation: H&H theory" — The hyper/hypo speech framework directly relevant to implementing speaking-style variation in synthesis
- **Browman & Goldstein (1992)** "Articulatory phonology: An overview" — Foundational reference for gestural overlap approach to coarticulation
- **Sussman et al. (1993)** "Locus equations" — Quantitative method for measuring C-to-V coarticulation; potentially useful for deriving formant transition parameters from data
- **Hardcastle & Hewlett (eds.) (1999)** *Coarticulation: theory, data and techniques* — The definitive edited volume on coarticulation, comprehensive reference
