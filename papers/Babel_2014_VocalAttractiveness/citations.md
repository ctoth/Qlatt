## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Klatt and Klatt (1990)** [ref 43] - Voice quality variations in male and female talkers; already in our collection, foundational for understanding the spectral tilt measures used here
2. **Puts et al. (2006)** [ref 23] - Dominance and sexual dimorphism in voice pitch; connects f0 and formant dispersion to perceived dominance/attractiveness
3. **Bruckert et al. (2010)** [ref 38] - Vocal attractiveness increases by averaging; introduces HNR as a voice smoothness measure predicting attractiveness
4. **Titze (1989)** [ref 42] - Physiological and acoustic differences between male and female voices; quantitative gender-specific acoustic parameters
5. **Feinberg et al. (2008)** [ref 24] - Femininity and averageness of voice pitch in aesthetic judgments; f0 manipulation study for female attractiveness
