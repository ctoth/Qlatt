## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Peterson & Barney (1952)** JASA 24:175-184 -- Canonical F1-F2 vowel data for men, women, and children. Foundational for vowel formant targets. Used directly in this paper to derive vowel articulation contours.
- **Stevens, Kasowski, & Fant (1953)** JASA 25:734-742 -- Describes the 35-section electrical analog used in this study. Important for understanding the experimental apparatus.
- **Fant (1952)** MIT Tech. Rept. No. 12 -- Early Fant work on vocal tract analog theory, predecessor to the Acoustic Theory of Speech Production.
- **Chiba & Kajiyama (1941)** "The Vowel, Its Nature and Structure" -- X-ray-based vocal tract area functions for vowels. Source of the tract profiles that motivated the parabolic model.
- **Delattre (1951)** PMLA 66:864-875 -- Relations between formant frequencies and cavity configurations in natural speech. Independently confirms the articulatory-acoustic trends found with the analog.
