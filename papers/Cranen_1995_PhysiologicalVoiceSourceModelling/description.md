Cranen and Schroeter present a parametric glottal geometry model that extends Titze's (1984) parameterization to distinguish two acoustically distinct types of glottal leakage: a "linked leak" (incomplete closure due to abduction) that steepens the overall spectral tilt, and a "parallel chink" (independent cartilaginous opening) that selectively attenuates lower frequencies while preserving the high-frequency spectral slope. The model decomposes the glottal area derivative into four components (dc-offset, core cosine, opening correction, closing correction) and derives the synthesis equations for computing glottal flow through the split membranous/chink paths. The work is directly relevant to synthesizing breathy and female voice qualities, where the type of leakage determines whether the voice sounds "dull-breathy" (linked leak) or "bright-breathy" (parallel chink).
