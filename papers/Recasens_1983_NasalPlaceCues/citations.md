## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Fujimura (1962)** — Foundational acoustic analysis of nasal consonants; provides pole/zero measurements that complement Recasens' perceptual data. Already in our papers collection.
2. **Hecker (1962)** — Nasal consonant synthesis using an articulatory speech synthesizer; directly relevant to how we set formant synth parameters for nasals.
3. **Nakata (1959)** — Early synthesis and perception study of nasals; one of the few studies combining both.
4. **Mártony (1964)** — Role of formant amplitudes in nasal synthesis; relevant to setting parallel branch gains for nasal murmurs in Klatt synthesizer.
5. **Fant (1960)** — Acoustic theory underlying the nasal formant/antiformant structure. Already in our papers collection.
