## Key Citations

- **Rabiner 1968 [1]**: Digital formant synthesizer with mixed excitation including fricative noise modulated by glottal pulse
- **Rosenberg 1971 [2]**: Pitch-synchronous analysis and resynthesis with various source waveforms; cosine-segment pulse shape; found subjects sometimes preferred idealized pulses over natural
- **Flanagan 1972 [5]**: Reference for subglottal coupling effects on formant frequencies
- **Fant et al. 1972 [6]**: Subglottal formant measurements showing pole shifts during glottal opening
- **Mathes & Miller 1947 [9]**: Phase effects in monaural perception; importance of earphones for detecting phase differences; source waveforms used in Holmes's phase experiment
