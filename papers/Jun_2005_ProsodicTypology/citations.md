## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Pierrehumbert, J. (1980)** - *The Phonology and Phonetics of English Intonation*. The foundational AM analysis that all ToBI systems build upon. Essential for understanding the theoretical basis of the pitch accent and boundary tone inventory.

2. **Ladd, D. R. (1996)** - *Intonational Phonology*. The comprehensive theoretical treatment of AM intonational phonology, including the criteria for what counts as an intonational phonology. Already in the collection as Ladd_2008 (2nd edition).

3. **Wightman, C. et al. (1992)** - Segmental Durations in the Vicinity of Prosodic Phrase Boundaries. Provides the quantitative duration data that maps break indices to duration lengthening effects, directly relevant for Qlatt's duration rules.

4. **Beckman, M. E. (1986)** - *Stress and Non-Stress Accent*. Defines the stress-accent vs pitch-accent distinction that underlies the typology model and the realization of postlexical prominence in English.

5. **Hirst, D. and Di Cristo, A. (1998)** - *Intonation Systems: Survey of Twenty Languages*. The complementary cross-linguistic survey using the INTSINT framework rather than ToBI, useful for comparison and for languages not covered in this volume.
