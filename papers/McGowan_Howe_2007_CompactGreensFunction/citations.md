## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Howe & McGowan (2005)** — Direct application of this theory to sibilant [s] production, with experimental validation. Most relevant follow-up for understanding fricative source mechanisms.
- **Krane (2005)** — Aeroacoustic production of low-frequency unvoiced speech sounds, complementary analysis of non-sibilant sources.
- **Stevens (1971)** — Foundational work on airflow and turbulence noise that this paper extends; directly cited in our collection.
- **Shadle (1985)** — Experimental fricative acoustics that the compact Green's function theory helps explain; already in our collection.
- **McGowan (1988)** — The aeroacoustic approach applied to phonation (voicing) rather than frication.
