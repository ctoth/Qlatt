## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **[1] Fitt, Susan (1997), "The generation of regional pronunciations of English for speech synthesis"** — Earlier standalone work by the lead author on the same general problem; likely the direct predecessor of the keyword-lexicon architecture this paper (and the companion 1999 Eurospeech paper) build on.
- **[2] Williams, Briony J., and Isard, Stephen (1997), "A keyvowel approach to the synthesis of regional accents of English"** — Source of the original `rr`/`r` split-symbol notation for post-vocalic /r/, referenced in Section 3.3's discussion of cross-word phenomena; the companion ICPhS 99 paper explicitly builds on and revises this approach.
- **[3] Wells, John C. (1982), Accents of English** — Foundational source for the Wells lexical-set/keyword system this entire discussion presupposes.
- **[4] Reid, Euan (1978), "Social and stylistic variation in the speech of children"** — Cited re: research on speaking-rate/style effects in Section 3.2's discussion of accent-specific allophones.
- **[5] Romaine, Suzanne (1978), "Postvocalic /r/ in Scottish English: sound change in progress?"** — Cited re: Edinburgh English glottalization scope in Section 3.1's t-glottaling discussion.
