## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **[1] Ananthapadmanabha & Fant (1982)** — "Calculation of true glottal flow and its components" — The foundational theory of source/vocal tract interaction used throughout Plumpe's paper. Essential for understanding formant modulation within pitch periods.
- **[20] Krishnamurthy (1983)** — "Glottal source estimation using a model of formant frequency modulation" — PhD thesis on the formant modulation approach that Plumpe extends; may contain more detail on the closed-phase detection algorithm.
- **[34] Wong, Markel & Gray (1979)** — "Least-squares glottal inverse filtering" — The baseline closed-phase inverse filtering method that Plumpe improves upon with formant stationarity detection.
- **[10/11] Dennis, Gay & Welsch (1981)** — NL2SOL algorithm — The specific nonlinear least-squares optimizer used for LF model fitting; understanding its convergence properties matters for implementation.
- **[24] Fant, Liljencrants & Lin (1985)** — "A four-parameter model of glottal flow" — The original LF model paper (already in collection as Fant_1985_LFModelGlottalFlow).
