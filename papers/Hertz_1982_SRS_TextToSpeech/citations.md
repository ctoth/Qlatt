## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Klatt (1980)** - "Software for a cascade/parallel formant synthesizer" - The foundational Klatt synthesizer paper that Qlatt implements. Essential reference for understanding the target synthesizer architecture.

2. **Hertz (1979b)** - Ph.D. dissertation - Contains more detailed information about the SRS rule language, contour definition system, and implementation details not fully covered in this JASA paper.

3. **Carlson & Granström (1976)** - "A text-to-speech system based entirely on rules" - Parallel development of rule-based TTS in Sweden, useful for comparing approaches to the same problem.

4. **Lovins, Macchi & Fujimura (1979)** - "A demisyllable inventory for speech synthesis" - Alternative to phoneme-based synthesis that might inform coarticulation modeling.

5. **Allen (1976)** - "Synthesis of speech from unrestricted text" - MIT approach to unrestricted text synthesis, important context for the field at the time.
