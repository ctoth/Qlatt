Gauffin and Sundberg analyze the relationships between flow glottogram waveform characteristics and their spectral correlates in singers and nonsingers, using inverse filtering. They establish that the peak-to-peak amplitude of the flow glottogram correlates with the source spectrum fundamental amplitude, while the negative peak of the differentiated glottogram (E_e in the LF model) correlates with overall SPL. The paper validates predictions from Fant's LF model and defines phonation types (pressed, normal, flow, breathy) in terms of measurable glottogram parameters.
