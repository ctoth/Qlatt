# Citations

## Reference List

[1] Wells, John C. (1982). *Accents of English*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.

[2] Fitt, Susan, and Isard, Stephen (1998). Representing the environments for phonological processes in an accent-independent lexicon for synthesis of English. *Proceedings: ICSLP 98.*

[3] Fitt, Susan (1999). The treatment of vowels preceding 'r' in a keyword lexicon of English. *Proceedings: ICPhS 99.*

## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **[1] Wells (1982), Accents of English** — Foundational source for the lexical-set keyword framework (KIT, DRESS, TRAP, NURSE, etc.) this paper's keysymbol/keyword mapping is built directly on. Essential background for understanding the "Well's Keyword" column in Table 1.
- **[2] Fitt & Isard (1998), ICSLP 98** — Almost certainly defines the formalism for post-lexical rule "environments" only informally illustrated in this paper (the `$` boundary marker, bracket/capital resolution mechanics). High priority for anyone implementing the rule engine itself.
- **[3] Fitt (1999), ICPhS 99** — Focused treatment of the NEAR/SQUARE/NORTH/FORCE/CURE/NURSE-split vowel-before-/r/ keysymbols discussed in Section 4.1-4.2 of this paper; useful for the rhotic-accent vowel design specifically.
