## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **van den Berg 1956a** - Direct and indirect determination of mean subglottic pressure. Foundational methodology paper for subglottic pressure measurement. Isshiki builds directly on this work's findings about the 4th power relationship between intensity and flow rate.

2. **Ladefoged & McKinney 1963** - Loudness, sound pressure, and subglottic pressure in speech. Emphasized the relation between subglottic power and loudness. Directly relevant to understanding the SPL-pressure relationship in running speech rather than sustained vowels.

3. **Flanagan 1958** - Some properties of the glottal sound source. Properties of the glottal source that inform efficiency calculations. Referenced for opening quotient (OQ) effects on intensity, which Isshiki uses to explain the efficiency increase at higher intensities.

4. **Fant 1960** - Acoustic theory of speech production. The foundational source-filter theory text. Isshiki draws on Fant's analysis of the subglottal system's impedance characteristics to explain why flow rate decreases at low pitch for louder voice.

5. **Stevens & House 1961** - Acoustical theory of vowel production. Discusses how vocal tract transfer function affects radiated intensity. Relevant to understanding the E_tr component of Isshiki's intensity equation.
