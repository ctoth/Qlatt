## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Saltzman & Munhall (1989)** - "A dynamical approach to gestural patterning in speech production" - The core computational model underlying articulatory phonology; defines the task dynamic equations and coordinative structures. Essential for understanding the mathematical formalism.

2. **Browman & Goldstein (1990a)** - "Gestural specification using dynamically-defined articulatory structures" - The detailed specification of gestural scores and tract variable dynamics. Contains the computational implementation details.

3. **Nolan (1992)** - "The descriptive role of segments: Evidence from assimilation" - Demonstrates with EPG data that assimilated final consonants retain residual articulatory gestures, directly relevant for modeling natural connected speech.

4. **Cooper (1991)** - "An articulatory account of aspiration in English" - Transillumination data showing aspiration is gradient, not categorical. Critical for implementing VOT/aspiration rules.

5. **Byrd (1992)** - "Perception of assimilation in consonant clusters: a gestural model" - Links gestural overlap to perceptual assimilation with acoustic modeling; directly relevant for how overlap affects formant transitions at word boundaries.
