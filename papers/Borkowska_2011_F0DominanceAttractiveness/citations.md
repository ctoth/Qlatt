## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Hodges-Simeon et al. (2010)** — Already in collection; complementary study on male voice F0/dominance/attractiveness showing linear relationships for both
2. **Feinberg et al. (2008a)** — Already in collection; tested femininity vs averageness in voice pitch attractiveness, found linear relationship (contrasts with this paper's nonlinear finding)
3. **Collins & Missing (2003)** — Already in collection; vocal and visual attractiveness correlation in women, provides F0 and formant data
4. **Jones et al. (2010)** — Voice pitch and dominance perception with raised/lowered pitch pairs; methodological comparison
5. **Puts et al. (2006)** — Dominance and male voice pitch evolution; provides the male counterpart to this paper's female dominance findings
