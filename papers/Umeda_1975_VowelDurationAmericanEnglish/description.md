# Umeda 1975 — Vowel Duration in American English

Large-corpus empirical study deriving multiplicative duration rules for American English vowels from continuous speech readings by three speakers. Provides a compact three-parameter model (T = T_0 + S(K_1 + K_2 * C)) where vowel duration depends on inherent vowel length (T_0), a stress-situation factor (S) encoding position, stress, and word type, and a consonant factor (C) encoding the manner/voicing of the following consonant. Includes detailed tables of constants per vowel, consonant factors by consonant class, stress-situation factors by positional condition and speaker, plus analysis of unstressed vowels and variability.
