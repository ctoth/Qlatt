## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **ITU-T P.810 (1996), Modulated Noise Reference Unit** — defines Q and the MNRU reference degradation that P.800 makes mandatory as an anchor in DCR, CCR and the threshold method. Without it the reference-condition requirement cannot be implemented.
2. **ITU-T P.85 (1994), subjective assessment of speech voice output devices** — the direct analogue of P.800 for synthesized rather than transmitted speech; the right companion when the system under test generates the signal.
3. **ITU-T P.830 (1996), subjective assessment of digital codecs** — the application-specific procedure this revision was aligned with; supplies the modified IRS, SNR(p) definition and codec-test panel sizes P.800 leaves open.
4. **HOTH (D.F.), JASA 12, 1941** — the empirical source of the Table A.1 room-noise spectrum used as the standard background-noise condition.
5. **COMBESCURE (P.) et al, ICASSP 82** — the origin of the DCR method, giving the empirical case that ACR lacks sensitivity among good-quality circuits.
6. **ITU-T P.56 (1993), active speech level** — required for every level-normalization step in Annex B; the definition of "active speech level" that the −26 dB target refers to.
