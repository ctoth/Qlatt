This chapter investigates how manipulations of voice pitch (±1.5 semitones) and speech tempo (factors 0.85/1.00/1.15) affect female listeners' attractiveness ratings of male speakers, using Dutch spontaneous speech and cumulative link mixed models. The key finding is an asymmetric effect: higher pitch and slower tempo independently reduce attractiveness, but lower pitch and faster tempo do not increase it, with pitch having a larger effect than tempo. Relevant to TTS voice parameter selection — suggests default F0 and speaking rate should be at or near population means for natural-sounding male voices, since deviations primarily cause negative perceptual effects.
