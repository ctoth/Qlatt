Describes the design and full process of the first Blizzard Challenge, in which six groups built voices from the same freely released CMU ARCTIC single-speaker databases and synthesized an identical 250-sentence test set spanning five text genres. The evaluation protocol pairs MOS (1-5) ratings on novels, news and conversational text with forced typed transcription on phonetically confusable carrier phrases and semantically unpredictable sentences, delivered over the web to three separate listener populations (speech experts, volunteers, paid undergraduates) with 20 stimuli per test, 10 or more listeners per sample, and a natural-speech topline. This is the canonical protocol template for evaluating a formant or source-filter synthesizer defensibly, since it separates naturalness from intelligibility, documents why MOS alone cannot validate speaker-similarity claims, and catalogues the failure modes of web-delivered listening tests.
