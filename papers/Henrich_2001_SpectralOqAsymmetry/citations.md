## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **[9] Veldhuis 1998 - R++ model**: Defines the R++ glottal flow model analyzed in this paper. Not yet in collection. Relevant for understanding alternative LF-like models with different computational properties.

2. **[11] Doval & d'Alessandro 1999 - Full spectral derivations**: The 22-page technical report containing the complete derivations summarized in this 4-page conference paper. Would provide the missing mathematical detail for implementation. Unpublished LIMSI report -- may be difficult to obtain.

3. **[10] Sundberg et al. 1999 - Baritone voice sources**: Reported high correlation between Oq and H1*-H2* in baritone voices, which this paper argues may indicate parameter covariation rather than a true one-to-one mapping. Not yet in collection.

4. **[5] Doval & d'Alessandro 1997 - ICASSP spectral correlates**: Predecessor study with more detailed spectral analysis of individual GFM models. Provides the analytical foundation for the formulas in this paper.

5. **[12] Henrich et al. 2000 - EGG Oq measurements**: Workshop paper on open quotient measurement methodology using EGG, which underpins the experimental validation in this paper.
