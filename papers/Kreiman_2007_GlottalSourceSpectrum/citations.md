## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Alku, Strik, & Vilkman (1997)** -- Parabolic spectral parameter (PSP) for glottal flow quantification. An alternative to H1-H2 that captures overall spectral shape in a single parameter. Not yet in collection.

2. **Iseli & Alwan (2004)** -- Improved correction formula for harmonic magnitude estimation, reducing formant influence on source spectral measures. Essential for accurate H1*-A1 and H1*-A3 computation. Not yet in collection.

3. **Ni Chasaide & Gobl (1997)** -- Voice source variation chapter from the Handbook of Phonetic Sciences, providing comprehensive R parameter definitions and voice quality mappings. Related content partially covered by Gobl_2003_VoiceQualityEmotion.

4. **Fant (1995/1997)** -- R parameter definitions and the voice quality continuum from pressed to breathy. The theoretical basis for the pulse shape analysis in this paper. Partially covered by Fant_1985_LFModelGlottalFlow in collection.

5. **de Krom (1993)** -- Cepstral-domain harmonics-to-noise ratio technique used for separating harmonic and noise components of the glottal source. Not yet in collection.
