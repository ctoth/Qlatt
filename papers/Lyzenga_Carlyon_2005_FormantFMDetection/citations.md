## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Lyzenga & Carlyon (1999)** - Foundation paper for FMDI in vowel formants. Contains the original hybrid model and demonstrates that off-frequency modulated formants interfere with FM detection. Essential predecessor to this paper.

2. **Lyzenga & Horst (1997)** - Source of the modified place model and beating detector used in the hybrid model. Contains the Roex filter bank approach and excitation pattern comparison for frequency discrimination.

3. **Mermelstein (1978)** - Formant frequency difference limens for both steady-state and consonant-bound vowels. Relevant for establishing perceptual limits on formant transitions in synthesis.

4. **Hawks (1994)** - Shows that parallel changes across multiple formants lower detection thresholds, suggesting coordinated formant movement is perceptually advantageous.

5. **Hillenbrand & Houde (2003)** - Narrow-band pattern matching vowel perception model that addresses the missing-formant problem. Directly relevant to high-F0 voice synthesis.
