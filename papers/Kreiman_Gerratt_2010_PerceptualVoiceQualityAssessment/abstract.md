# Abstract

## Original Text (Verbatim)

Despite many years of research, we still do not know how to measure vocal quality. This paper reviews the history of quality assessment, describes some reasons why current approaches are unlikely to be fruitful, and proposes an alternative approach that addresses the primary difficulties with existing protocols.

---

## Our Interpretation

The paper argues that voice quality measurement is fundamentally broken: rating scales like GRBAS and CAPE-V have no theoretical foundation and can't be validated because "quality" itself is undefined. The authors propose using speech synthesis as a measurement tool, where listeners adjust acoustic parameters until the synthesis matches a target voice, achieving 96% agreement versus near-chance agreement with traditional ratings. For Qlatt, this validates the synthesis approach and suggests perceptual testing methodology.
