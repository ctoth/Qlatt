## Key Citations (Most Relevant to Qlatt)

- **Ishizaka & Flanagan 1972** - Two-mass vocal cord model; aerodynamic equations used in glottal flow computation
- **Titze 1976** - Prior viscoelastic vocal fold vibration model; basis for this paper's approach
- **Titze & Strong 1975** - Normal modes of vocal fold tissue; first attempt at distributed parameter model
- **Hirano 1975** - Body-cover theory of vocal fold structure; stress-strain data used for tissue parameters
- **von Gierke et al. 1952** - Tissue viscosity measurements; basis for nominal viscosity values
