## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Ramus, F., & Mehler, J. (1999). Language identification with suprasegmental cues: A study based on speech resynthesis. *JASA, 105*(1), 512-521.** The companion paper. Source of the delexicalizing resynthesis recipe (all consonants to /s/, all vowels to /a/, F0 fixed at 230 Hz) and the only adult experiment testing discrimination on rhythm alone. Directly relevant as a prosody-evaluation rig for a synthesizer.
- **Nazzi, T., Bertoncini, J., & Mehler, J. (1998). Language discrimination by newborns. *JEP:HPP, 24*(3), 756-766.** Supplies the corpus this paper measures and the behavioral results the simulations reproduce, including the negative English/Dutch result.
- **Dauer, R. M. (1983). Stress-timing and syllable-timing reanalyzed. *Journal of Phonetics, 11*, 51-62.** The phonological account of rhythm as a by-product of syllable structure and vowel reduction that this paper turns into measurements.
- **Mehler, J., Dupoux, E., Nazzi, T., & Dehaene-Lambertz, G. (1996). Coping with linguistic diversity: The infant's viewpoint.** Source of the Time-Intensity Grid Representation (TIGRE), the vowel-centered signal representation the whole method assumes.
- **Levelt, C., & van de Vijver, R. (1998). Syllable types in cross-linguistic and developmental grammars.** The five-class syllable-markedness typology that predicts rhythm classes beyond the standard three, including a strictly-CV class beyond Japanese.
