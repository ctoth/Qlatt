## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Pierrehumbert 1980** — Already in collection. The thesis that resolved the levels-vs-configurations debate by positing H/L tones with pitch accents as combinations. Pike's 1989 letter shows he anticipated several of its key ideas.

2. **Ladd 2008 *Intonational Phonology*** — Already in collection. Ladd's comprehensive treatment that contextualizes both the historical debate and modern autosegmental-metrical theory. This 2014 paper is a supplement to its Chapter 2.

3. **Bolinger 1951 "Intonation: Levels versus configurations"** — The foundational paper opposing the four-level analysis. Understanding this debate is essential for understanding why modern systems (including ToBI) use the approach they do.

4. **Pike 1945 *The Intonation of American English*** — The original monograph. Contains Pike's full system including contour inventories, complex intonation patterns, and voice quality features (pp. 93-95) that anticipate modern paralinguistic modeling.

5. **Murray 1994 *Theory Groups and the Study of Language in North America*** — Documents the institutional politics of Bloomfieldian linguistics. Relevant for understanding how intellectual gatekeeping shaped which theoretical frameworks survived into the generative era.
