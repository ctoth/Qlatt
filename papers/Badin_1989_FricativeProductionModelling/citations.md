## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Shadle (1985)** - The definitive PhD thesis on fricative acoustics. Primary source for obstacle vs non-obstacle configurations, flow exponents, and mechanical model comparisons. Essential reading for anyone implementing fricative synthesis.

2. **Stevens (1971)** - Theoretical foundation for relating airflow and turbulence to fricative/stop acoustics. Proposes SPL∝V_c³√A_c relationship that this paper tests against.

3. **Fant (1960)** - Foundational acoustic theory of speech production. Proposes simpler SPL∝V_c² relationship. Core reference for source-filter theory.

4. **Badin & Fant (1984)** - Technical details on vocal tract computation using electric quadrupole representation. Relevant for implementing acoustic transfer function calculations.

5. **Hixon et al. (1967)** - Original experimental work on turbulent noise correlates that this paper replicates and extends. Useful for understanding experimental methodology.
