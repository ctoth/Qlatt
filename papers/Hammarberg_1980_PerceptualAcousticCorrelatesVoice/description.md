Factor-analytic study of 28 perceptual voice quality terms rated by expert listeners on 17 pathological voices, yielding five bipolar factors (Unstable-Steady, Breathy-Overtight, Hyper-Hypofunctional, Coarse-Light, Head-Chest Register) that account for 85% of variance. Four of the five factors were significantly correlated with acoustic measures derived from long-time-average spectra (LTAS) and fundamental frequency analysis, with spectral slope between frequency bands being the primary acoustic correlate of the breathy-overtight dimension. Establishes a foundational perceptual framework for voice quality description with quantified acoustic-perceptual mappings.
