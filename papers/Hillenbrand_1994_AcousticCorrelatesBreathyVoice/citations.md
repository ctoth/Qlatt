## Key Citations (most relevant to Klatt synthesis)

1. **Klatt & Klatt (1990)** — Direct predecessor; established aspiration noise as primary breathiness cue; this paper automates and extends their periodicity measure
2. **Hillenbrand (1988a)** — Synthesis study showing aspiration noise dominates breathiness perception over spectral tilt
3. **de Krom (1993)** — Related cepstrum-based harmonic-to-noise ratio technique; CPP is simpler
4. **Fukazawa et al. (1988)** — Spectral tilt index (BRI); this paper found it weak, contradicting Fukazawa's results
5. **Bickley (1982)** — Found H1 amplitude (not aspiration noise) drove breathiness in Gujarati; contradicted by this paper
