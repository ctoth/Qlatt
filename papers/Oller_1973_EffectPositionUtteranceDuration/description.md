Systematic investigation of how position within an utterance (initial, medial, final) affects the duration of speech segments in English. Through eight experiments with nonsense syllable inventories, Oller demonstrates that final-syllable vowels are lengthened by approximately 100 msec, word-initial consonants by 20-30 msec, and absolute final fricatives by dramatically larger amounts. These effects persist across intonation types (declarative, interrogative, imperative) and boundary levels (word-final, phrase-final, utterance-final), establishing the empirical foundation for positional duration rules in speech synthesis systems such as Klatt 1976.
