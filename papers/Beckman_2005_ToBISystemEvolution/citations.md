## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Pierrehumbert (1980)** — The foundational PhD thesis defining the autosegmental-metrical model of English intonation. All ToBI pitch accent and boundary tone labels derive from this work. Essential for understanding the theoretical basis of F0 target generation.

2. **Silverman et al. (1992)** — The original ToBI standard definition paper. Already in collection as `Silverman_1992_ToBILabelingProsody`.

3. **Wightman et al. (1992)** — Quantitative measurements of segmental durations near prosodic boundaries. Directly relevant to duration rules for phrase-final lengthening.

4. **Pitrelli, Beckman and Hirschberg (1994)** — Inter-transcriber reliability evaluation of ToBI. Important for understanding which distinctions are robust vs problematic (H* vs L+H* is the weakest).

5. **Ladd (1996)** — *Intonational Phonology* textbook. The comprehensive theoretical treatment of the AM framework underlying ToBI. Already in collection as `Ladd_2008_IntonationalPhonology` (2nd edition).
