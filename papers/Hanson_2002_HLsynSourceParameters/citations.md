## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Stevens and Bickley (1991)** - The original HL parameter proposal. Already in collection as Stevens_1991_HL_Parameters. Essential companion to this paper.
2. **Rothenberg (1968, 1993)** - The equivalent-circuit model that HLsyn is built on. Rothenberg_1981_InteractiveVoiceSource partially covers this. The 1968 thesis (breath-stream dynamics) may be worth acquiring for the complete circuit derivation.
3. **Hanson et al. (2001)** - "Towards models of phonation" - extends the phonation model with more detailed vocal-fold mechanics. Not yet in collection.
4. **Titze (1992)** - Phonation threshold pressure, the key aerodynamic parameter that governs voicing onset/offset in HLsyn. Not yet in collection.
5. **Klatt and Klatt (1990)** - The KLSYN88 synthesizer and KLGLOTT88 source model that HLsyn maps to. Already in collection as Klatt_1990_VoiceQualityVariations.
