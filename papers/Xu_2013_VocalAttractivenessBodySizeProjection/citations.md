## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Morton (1977)** [ref 8] - Motivation-structural rules in animal sounds; theoretical foundation for the body size projection framework used throughout this paper
2. **Chuenwattanapranithi et al. (2008)** [ref 11] - Directly tests the size code hypothesis for emotional speech encoding using systematic acoustic manipulation
3. **Birkholz et al. (2006, 2007)** [refs 14-15] - VocalTractLab articulatory synthesis system used to generate stimuli; relevant for understanding stimulus generation methodology
4. **Klatt and Klatt (1990)** [ref 9] - Voice quality analysis framework; already in collection
5. **Hanson et al. (2001)** [ref 7] - Models of phonation; spectral tilt measures H1-H2, H1-A1, H1-A3 used for voice quality verification
