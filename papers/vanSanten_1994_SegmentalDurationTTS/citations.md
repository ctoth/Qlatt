## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Klatt (1976)** - The baseline multiplicative duration rules that this paper supersedes. Already in collection.
2. **Allen et al. (1987)** - MITalk system with complete duration module specification. Already in collection.
3. **Umeda (1977)** - Comprehensive consonant duration data for American English. Not yet in collection.
4. **van Santen (1993a)** - Mathematical foundations of sums-of-products models (the theoretical underpinning). Already in collection as vanSanten_1993_SegmentalDuration.
5. **Riley (1992)** - CART-based duration modeling that this paper compares against unfavorably. Relevant for understanding the limitations of tree-based approaches.
