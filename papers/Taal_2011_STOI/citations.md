## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **[3] ANSI S3.5-1997 (SII standard)** - The Speech Intelligibility Index standard that STOI aims to improve upon; provides the foundational framework for frequency-weighted intelligibility prediction
- **[5] Kates & Arehart 2005 (CSII)** - The coherence-based speech intelligibility index that is STOI's closest competitor for single-channel noise reduction conditions; relevant for understanding alternative intelligibility metrics
- **[28-29] Dau et al. 1996 (DAU model)** - The auditory processing model that STOI competes against; provides a more physiologically-grounded but computationally expensive alternative approach
- **[19] Kjems et al. 2009** - The ITFS listening experiment data used to validate STOI; contains the Dantale II corpus intelligibility scores that serve as ground truth
- **[26] Hu & Loizou 2008** - Comprehensive evaluation of objective quality measures for speech enhancement; provides context for how STOI fits among existing metrics
