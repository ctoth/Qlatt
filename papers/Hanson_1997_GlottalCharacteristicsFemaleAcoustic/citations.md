## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Holmberg et al. (1995)** - "Comparisons among aerodynamic, EGG, and acoustic spectral measures of female voice" - J. Speech Hear. Res. 38, 1212-1223. Directly validates the acoustic measures in this paper against physiological data. Essential for confirming Klatt parameter mappings.

2. **Karlsson (1992a)** - PhD thesis on analysis and synthesis of different voices with emphasis on female speech. Comprehensive female voice modeling with inverse filtering, voice quality classification.

3. **Klatt & Klatt (1990)** - Already in collection. Core reference for KLGLOTT88 source model and voice quality synthesis.

4. **Stevens & Hanson (1995)** - "Classification of glottal vibration from acoustic measurements" - Develops the theoretical framework behind the two-group speaker classification.

5. **Sodersten & Lindestad (1990)** - "Glottal closure and perceived breathiness" - Physiological (fiberscopic) validation of the relationship between glottal closure patterns and perceived breathiness.
