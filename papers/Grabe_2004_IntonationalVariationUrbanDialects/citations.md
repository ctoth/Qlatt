## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Grabe, Post, Nolan & Farrar (2000), Journal of Phonetics 28, 161-185** — the companion paper providing the detailed methodology and additional dialect data for the truncation/compression finding summarized in Section 5.1 of this chapter. Retrieval was attempted for this collection but not obtained (paywalled, no open-access copy located, sci-hub blocked by CAPTCHA).
- **Haan, J. (2002), "Speaking of questions," PhD thesis, LOT** — source of the question-marker/final-rise trade-off framework this paper extends to English; worth reading for the original Dutch statistical methodology.
- **Ladd, D.R. (1996), "Intonational phonology"** — for the Glasgow rise-plateau transcription problem and the language-specific truncation/compression hypothesis this paper's data revises.
- **Grabe (1998a), "Comparative intonational phonology: English and German," MPI Series 7** — fuller treatment of the AM phonological framework and comparative labelling method underlying the IViE system.
- **Rahilly, J. (1991), PhD thesis, Queen's University Belfast** — foundational source on Belfast's rising declarative/question non-distinction; would give more phonetic detail on the Belfast rise-plateau family.
