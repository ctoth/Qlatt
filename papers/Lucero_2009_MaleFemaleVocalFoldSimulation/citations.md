## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **[2] Kob 2002** — The VOX multiple-mass vocal fold model used in this paper. Contains the full model specification with equations and parameters. Relevant if implementing a physical vocal fold model beyond the LF source.
- **[4] Jiang & Titze 1994** — Experimental measurements of intraglottal pressure and impact stress, providing validation data for vocal fold collision models.
- **[1] Dejonckere 2001** — Source of the gender-specific vocal fold shape measurements (n=10 female, n=9 male) used as simulation inputs. Contains the anatomical data for male/female vocal fold geometry.
