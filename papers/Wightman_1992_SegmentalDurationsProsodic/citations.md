## Key Citations (most relevant to Qlatt)

- **Crystal & House 1982, 1988, 1990**: Segmental durations in connected speech — provides the baseline duration statistics and gamma distribution model used in this paper
- **Klatt 1975, 1976**: Foundational work on linguistic uses of segmental duration, including pre-boundary lengthening
- **Beckman & Pierrehumbert 1986**: Intonational structure framework (ToBI precursor) used for the prosodic hierarchy
- **Lehiste 1970, 1973, 1979**: Suprasegmentals and perception of phrase boundaries
- **Price, Ostendorf, Shattuck-Hufnagel & Fong 1991**: Prosody in syntactic disambiguation — companion paper using same corpus
- **Pierrehumbert 1980**: Phonology and phonetics of English intonation — theoretical framework
- **Selkirk 1984**: Prosodic categories and phonological structure
- **Nespor & Vogel 1986**: Prosodic phonology hierarchy
