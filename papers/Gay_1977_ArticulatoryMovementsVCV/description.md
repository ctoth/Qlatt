Cinefluorographic study of articulatory movements in VCV utterances with vowels /i,a,u/ and consonants /p,t,k/, examining both timing and positional properties of coarticulation. Demonstrates that anticipatory movements toward the second vowel always begin during consonant closure, that carryover effects depend on the phonetic identity of the consonant, and that both types of coarticulation are limited to immediately adjacent segments. Provides quantitative articulatory and acoustic data relevant to modeling formant transitions and coarticulatory rules in synthesis.
