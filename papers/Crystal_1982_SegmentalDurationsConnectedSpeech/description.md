Crystal and House present a comprehensive database of segmental durations measured from connected speech readings by 14 talkers, divided into FAST and SLOW tempo groups. They provide mean durations, standard deviations, and counts for all major phonetic categories (vowels, sonorants, stops, fricatives, affricates), along with contextual analyses of vowel duration before voiced/voiceless consonants. Duration distributions are modeled with two-parameter gamma functions, providing shape and scale parameters useful for generating natural durational variation in synthesis.
