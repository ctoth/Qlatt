## Key Citations for Qlatt Project

- **van den Berg et al. 1957 (ref 15)**: Air resistance and Bernoulli effect — the primary empirical source for glottal resistance formulas used throughout this paper
- **van den Berg 1956 (ref 14)**: Subglottic pressure measurements — Table 1 data source
- **Fletcher 1950 (ref 6)**: High-speed glottal area measurements — the primary data source for volume velocity waveform derivation
- **Stevens, Kasowski & Fant 1953 (ref 13)**: Electrical analog of vocal tract — context for the source-filter framework
- **Flanagan & House 1956 (ref 5)**: Formant coding system — the synthesis application motivating this work
