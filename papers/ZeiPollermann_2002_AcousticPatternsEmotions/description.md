This book chapter presents empirical acoustic measurements (F0, energy, delivery rate, long-term average spectra) for anger, joy, and sadness in 66 French speakers, using the covariance model to link emotionally-induced physiological arousal to vocal parameter patterns. The key finding is that F0, energy, and rate parameters differentiate emotions by arousal level (anger/joy vs sadness) but cannot distinguish hedonic valence (anger vs joy), while Bark-based LTAS analysis reveals that anger produces significantly higher spectral energy than joy in the 300–3400 Hz range, suggesting voice quality carries valence information. For speech synthesis, these results provide concrete numerical targets for emotion presets and support using spectral tilt and voice quality parameters rather than F0 alone to differentiate positive from negative high-arousal emotions.
