Fry (1958) systematically tests how duration, intensity, and fundamental frequency affect listeners' perception of lexical stress in English noun/verb pairs (e.g., SUBject vs. subJECT) using synthesized speech. The paper establishes that duration ratio is the strongest graded cue for stress, intensity is secondary, and F0 operates as an all-or-none cue where direction matters but magnitude does not. A third experiment shows that within-syllable F0 contour patterns (sentence intonation) can completely override duration cues, making F0 contour shape the most powerful factor when it is present.
