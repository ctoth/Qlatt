A theoretical/methodological companion to the Unisyn keyword-lexicon papers, working through the problem of where to draw the line between what an accent-independent lexicon should encode as keysymbols versus what should be derived by accent-dependent post-lexical rule. Surveys hard cases (phonemic-vs-allophonic ambiguity, full/reduced vowel alternation, cross-word phenomena, stress and syllabification divergence across accents) and proposes three candidate architectures without settling on one, deferring the concrete rule notation to companion work. Useful for the design vocabulary and decision procedure it supplies, less so as a source of a ready-made rule syntax.
