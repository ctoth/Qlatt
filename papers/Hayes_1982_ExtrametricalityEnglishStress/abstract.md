## Our Interpretation

Hayes argues that excluding peripheral constituents from metrical operations simplifies English stress rules and explains differences among grammatical and morphological classes. The account separates foot construction, cyclic preservation, destressing and word-tree labeling, while acknowledging lexical exceptions. Its relevance to Qlatt is the explicit structure and rule ordering needed beyond a syllable-count heuristic. *(pp.227-228,274-275)*
