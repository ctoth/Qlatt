# Citations

Key references cited in Steinecke & Herzel (1995).

## Foundational vocal fold models

- **Ishizaka & Flanagan (1972)** — "Synthesis of voiced sounds from a two-mass model of the vocal cords," Bell Syst. Tech. J. 51, 1233–1268. *The original two-mass model that this paper simplifies.*
- **Titze (1973)** — "The human vocal cords: A mathematical model. Part I," Phonetica 28, 129–170. *Multi-mass vocal fold model.*
- **Story & Titze (1995)** — "Voice simulation with a body-cover model of the vocal folds," JASA 97, 1249–1260. *Body-cover distinction not included in Steinecke's simplified model.*
- **Titze & Talkin (1979)** — "A theoretical study of the effects of various laryngeal configurations on the acoustics of phonation," JASA 66, 60–74. *PDE-based vocal fold simulation.*
- **Titze & Alipour-Haghighi (1994)** — *Myoelastic Aerodynamic Theory of Phonation* (in press). *Comprehensive theory text.*

## Phonation onset and stability analysis

- **Titze (1988)** — "The physics of small amplitude oscillation of the vocal folds," JASA 83, 1536–1552. *Phonation threshold pressure theory; linear dependence on glottal rest area confirmed by this paper.*
- **Lucero (1993)** — "Dynamics of the two-mass model of the vocal folds: Equilibria, bifurcations, and oscillation region," JASA 94, 3104–3111. *Prior bifurcation analysis of the two-mass model; compared and extended here.*
- **Pelorson et al. (1994)** — "Theoretical and experimental study of quasi-steady flow separation within the glottis during phonation. Application to a modified two-mass model," JASA 96, 3416–3431.

## Nonlinear dynamics of voice

- **Herzel, Berry, Titze & Saleh (1994)** — "Analysis of vocal disorders with methods from nonlinear dynamics," J. Speech Hear. Res. 37, 1008–1019. *Clinical voice signal analysis using nonlinear dynamics; period tripling and modulation in laryngeal paralysis.*
- **Herzel (1993)** — "Bifurcations and chaos in voice signals," Appl. Mech. Rev. 46, 399–413.
- **Herzel, Berry, Titze & Steinecke (1995)** — "Nonlinear dynamics of the voice: Signal analysis and biomechanical modeling," Chaos 5 (January 1995). *Companion paper.*
- **Herzel & Knudsen (1994)** — "Bifurcations in a vocal fold model," Nonlinear Dyn. (in press).
- **Berry, Titze, Herzel & Krischer (1994)** — "Interpretation of biomechanical simulations of normal and chaotic vocal fold oscillations with empirical eigenfunctions," JASA 95, 3595–3604. *Desynchronization of principal modes hypothesis.*
- **Titze, Baken & Herzel (1993)** — "Evidence of chaos in vocal fold vibration," in *Vocal Fold Physiology: New Frontiers in Basic Science*, pp. 143–188.

## Asymmetric vocal folds and pathology

- **Ishizaka & Isshiki (1976)** — "Computer simulation of pathological vocal-cord vibration," JASA 60, 1193–1198.
- **Isshiki, Tanabe, Ishizaka & Broad (1977)** — "Clinical significance of asymmetrical vocal cord tension," Am. Otol. 86, 58–66. *Excised canine larynx experiments showing three oscillatory patterns depending on glottal gap.*
- **Smith, Berke, Gerratt & Kreiman (1992)** — "Laryngeal paralyses: Theoretical considerations and effects on laryngeal vibration," J. Speech Hear. Res. 35, 545–554.
- **Wong, Ito, Cox & Titze (1991)** — "Observation of perturbation in a lumped-element model of the vocal folds with application to some pathological cases," JASA 89, 383–394.

## Clinical voice observations

- **Herzel, Steinecke, Mende & Wermke (1991)** — "Chaos and bifurcations during voiced speech," in *Complexity, Chaos and Biological Evolution* (Plenum), pp. 41–50.
- **Mende, Herzel & Wermke (1990)** — "Bifurcations and chaos in newborn infant cries," Phys. Lett. A 145, 418–424.
- **Kelman (1981)** — "Vibratory pattern of the vocal folds," Folia Phoniatr. 33, 73–99.
- **Hammarberg, Fritzell, Gauffin & Sundberg (1986)** — "Acoustic and perceptual analysis of vocal dysfunction," J. Phon. 14, 533–547.

## Aerodynamics

- **van den Berg, Zantewa & Doornenbal (1957)** — "On the air resistance and the Bernoulli effect of the human larynx," JASA 29, 626–631.
- **Stevens (1977)** — "Physics of laryngeal behavior and larynx modes," Phonetica 34, 264–279.
- **Broad (1979)** — "The new theories of vocal fold vibration," in *Speech and Language*, Vol. 2.

## Nonlinear dynamics (general)

- **Doedel (1986)** — AUTO: Software for Continuation and Bifurcation Problems in ODEs. *Used for computing bifurcation curves.*
- **Lauterborn & Parlitz (1988)** — "Methods of chaos physics and their application to acoustics," JASA 84, 1975–1993.
- **Glass & Mackey (1988)** — *From Clocks to Chaos* (Princeton UP).

## Anatomy
