## Key Citations

- **Isshiki & Takeuchi 1970** — Prior factor analysis of hoarseness that this study builds upon
- **Jansson & Sundberg 1976** — LTAS method used for spectral analysis
- **Wendahl 1963, 1966** — Laryngeal analog synthesis of harsh voice, directly relevant to Klatt-type synthesis of voice quality
- **Takahashi & Koike 1975** — Prior perceptual dimensions study using sustained vowels (this study extends to connected speech)
