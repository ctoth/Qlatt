## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Fant G (1966)** — "A note on vocal tract size factors and non-uniform F-pattern scalings" — foundational for understanding how VTL maps to formant scaling, directly relevant to implementing speaker-specific formant transforms
2. **Story BH, Titze IR, Hoffman EA (1996)** — "Vocal tract area functions from magnetic resonance imaging" — provides MRI-derived area functions that complement this paper's AR-based volume data; essential for realistic tube model parameterization
3. **Andrianopoulos MV et al (2001)** — "Multimodal standardization of voice among four multicultural populations formant structures" — provides the formant frequency data across races that this paper lacks, completing the dimension-to-acoustics picture
4. **Xue A, Hao J (2003)** — "Gender differences in senile changes of human vocal tract" — extends these findings to aging speakers, relevant for age-dependent vocal tract scaling
5. **Nordstrom PE (1975)** — "Attempts to simulate female and infant vocal tracts from male area functions" — directly relevant for implementing female/child voice scaling from male vocal tract parameters
