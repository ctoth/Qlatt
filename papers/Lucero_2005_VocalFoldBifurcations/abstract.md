# Abstract

## Original Text (Verbatim)

This article presents an analysis of the dynamics of a bidimensional oscillator, which has been proposed as a simple model for the vocal fold motion at phonation. The model is capable of producing an oscillation with physiologically realistic values for the parameters. A simple extension of the model using even-powered polynomials in the damping factor is also proposed, which allows controlling the regime of the oscillation (sub- or supercritical). This extension may be used to represent the oscillation hysteresis phenomenon observed at the phonation onset-offset.

---

## Our Interpretation

Lucero analyzes a simplified vocal fold oscillator (based on Titze's 1988 mucosal wave model) to determine whether phonation onset is gradual (supercritical) or abrupt with hysteresis (subcritical), finding that for typical physiological parameters onset is always supercritical. An extended model with quartic nonlinear damping is proposed to reproduce the experimentally observed hysteresis between onset and offset subglottal pressures. This provides mathematical grounding for modeling voice onset quality (breathy vs. hard attack) and register transitions in a formant synthesizer.
