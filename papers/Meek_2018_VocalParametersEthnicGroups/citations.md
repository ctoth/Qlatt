## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Walton & Orlikoff (1994)** - Speaker race identification from acoustic cues in the vocal signal. Most directly relevant prior work: 50 Caucasian and 50 AA males, found frequency perturbation and shimmer differed by race, listeners identified race above chance from vowel samples.

2. **Andrianopoulos, Darrow, & Chen (2001)** - Multimodal standardization of voice among four multicultural populations. Larger study (N not specified in thesis) examining F0 and spectral characteristics across populations.

3. **Xue & Hao (2006)** - Vocal tract dimensions by race. Physical measurements of vocal tract volume and length across Caucasian, African American, and Chinese speakers (n=120). Found significant racial differences in oral volume and total tract volume.

4. **Cox & Mueller (2004)** - Ethnographic factors in voice: comprehensive literature review that concluded data was inconclusive but pointed toward possible F0 differences between African American and Caucasian speakers.

5. **Vargo (2012)** - Acoustic and perceptual analysis of F0 in African American and Caucasian speakers. Directly related dissertation examining the same core question.
