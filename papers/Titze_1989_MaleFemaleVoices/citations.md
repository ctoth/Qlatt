## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Holmberg, E., Hillman, R., and Perkell, J. (1988)** - Primary source of male/female airflow and pressure data used to derive the aerodynamic scale factors. Essential for validating airflow parameters in speaker profiles.

2. **Hirano, M. (1983)** - Cadaveric vocal fold structure data that underlies the scale factors. Provides the anatomical measurements for length, thickness, and tissue composition differences.

3. **Titze, I. (1988a)** - The four-parameter glottis model (Q_a, Q_s, Q_b, Q_p) referenced in this paper's simulation. Needed for implementing the prephonatory glottal shape parameterization.

4. **Titze, I. (1988c)** - Regulation of vocal power and efficiency by subglottal pressure. Complements this paper's efficiency analysis and provides the framework for effort-dependent voice variation.

5. **Bickley, C. and Brown, K. (1987)** - Bending-beam model proposed as improvement over string model for small vocal fold lengths (children, females). Relevant for more accurate F0 prediction at the extremes.
