This chapter by the creators of the original MAE_ToBI system documents the complete design of the Tones and Break Indices annotation conventions for Mainstream American English, including the pitch accent inventory (H*, L*, L+H*, L*+H, H+!H*), phrase accents, boundary tones, the 0-4 break index scale, and alignment rules for tone labels. The key contributions are the articulation of five consensus claims underlying the system (two tone levels, separate tonal and metrical tiers, two levels of phrasing), the four design principles that emerged from the 1991-1994 workshops (grounding in research, efficiency, community consensus, iterative development), and comparison with competing approaches (INTSINT, IViE) that clarifies ToBI labels as phonological tags rather than narrow phonetic transcriptions. For speech synthesis, this paper provides the authoritative specification of the prosodic labeling system that maps phonological categories to F0 targets, phrase boundaries, and break-index-driven duration rules, making it essential reference for any ToBI-based TTS prosody implementation.
