## Key Citations

- **Fant 1960** (ref 2): Acoustic theory providing the theoretical basis for nasal tract modeling with poles and zeros
- **Liberman et al. 1954** (ref 3): Prior Haskins study on nasal perception using pattern playback — Nakata's study extends this with analog synthesis
- **Delattre et al. 1955** (ref 12): Formant locus concept that Nakata uses to characterize nasal place cues
- **Fant 1956** (ref 9): Predictability of formant levels in cascade synthesis — justifies why cascade connection naturally produces correct amplitude relations
