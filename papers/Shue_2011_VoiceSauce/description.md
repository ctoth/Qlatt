VoiceSauce is a free Matlab program that computes an automated battery of voice-quality measures over directories of audio, including pitch-synchronous harmonic amplitudes (H1, H2, H4, A1-A3), spectral-tilt measures corrected for formant frequencies and bandwidths, cepstral peak prominence, three-band harmonic-to-noise ratio, subharmonic-to-harmonic ratio, and F0-normalized RMS energy, with F0 and formants from STRAIGHT, Snack, or Praat. Comparing H1-H2 against by-hand FFT measurement and a Praat script on Georgian stop-vowel data, VoiceSauce discarded no files and gave roughly half the within-category variability of the Praat script for non-modal phonation, which the authors attribute to dense 1 ms F0 tracking and to a maximum-search harmonic estimator equivalent to a very long FFT window. For a formant/source-filter synthesizer this defines both the validation battery for synthesized voice quality and the exact window conventions (3 pitch cycles for harmonics, 5 pitch periods for energy/CPP/HNR, 10%-of-F0 search range, 20-frame smoothing) needed for measurements comparable with published values.
