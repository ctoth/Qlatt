## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Fant (1995) - LF model revisited** - Defines the R-parameters (Rd, Ra, Rk, Rg) used in Table II of this paper; essential for implementing LF source control
2. **Story and Titze (1995) - Body-cover model** - The vocal fold model architecture that Zhang extends to 3D; foundational for understanding body/cover stiffness parameters
3. **Titze (1988) - Small-amplitude oscillation physics** - Derives phonation threshold pressure theory validated by Zhang's results; critical for voice onset modeling
4. **Holmberg et al. (1988) - Glottal airflow measurements** - Empirical data on male/female speakers at different loudness levels; validation targets for synthesis
5. **Kreiman and Gerratt (2012) - Perceptual interaction of harmonic source and noise** - Important for understanding how spectral tilt and noise interact perceptually in voice quality
