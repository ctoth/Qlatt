## Key Citations (most relevant to Qlatt)

- **Ohman (1966, 1967)** — VCV coarticulation data and numerical model; foundational for coproduction view. Already in collection as Ohman_1966_CoarticulationVCV.
- **Perkell (1969)** — Vocal tract physiology; distinct vowel/consonant muscle systems
- **Feldman (1966)** — Spring-like (equilibrium point) model of muscle control: F = ae^(k(l-l₀))
- **Bell-Berti & Harris (1979)** — Fixed temporal lead of anticipatory lip rounding regardless of consonant context
- **Gay & Hirose (1973), Gay & Ushijima (1974)** — Rate effects on consonant vs vowel production
- **Draper et al. (1958)** — Respiratory coordination during speech
- **Kent & Minifie (1977)** — Critical review of coarticulation theories
- **Daniloff & Hammarberg (1973), Hammarberg (1976)** — Primary exemplar of extrinsic timing view critiqued here
- **Sternberg et al. (1978)** — Self-executing plans; latency increases linearly with number of stressed syllables
- **Lashley (1951)** — Serial order problem; origin of extrinsic timing assumptions
