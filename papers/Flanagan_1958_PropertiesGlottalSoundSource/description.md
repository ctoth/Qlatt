Flanagan derives quantitative properties of the glottal sound source from high-speed motion picture data and steady-flow resistance measurements, establishing that the glottis acts as a high-impedance current source with a volume velocity spectrum falling at approximately -12 dB/octave. The paper derives a small-signal equivalent circuit for the glottal source, analyzes the relationship between glottal duty cycle and spectral richness, and shows that quasi-static flow assumptions are valid below about 300 Hz. These results provide foundational physical justification for the source-filter model used in formant synthesizers.
