## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Henrich et al. (2004)** - DECOM method paper. Essential for understanding the open quotient measurement technique used throughout this study.

2. **Klatt and Klatt (1990)** - Voice quality analysis and KLSYN88 synthesis. Already in collection. Provides the synthesis framework that these Oq measurements parameterize.

3. **Holmberg et al. (1988, 1989)** - Glottal airflow measurements for speech (not singing). Important for understanding how these singing results transfer to speech phonation parameters.

4. **Sundberg et al. (1993)** - Phonatory control in male singing with subglottal pressure and voice source analysis. Provides complementary aerodynamic data to the EGG-based measurements in this paper.

5. **Childers et al. (1990)** - EGG and vocal fold physiology. Foundational for understanding the relationship between EGG signals and the glottal parameters measured here.
