# Abstract

## Original Text (Verbatim)

[Book-level abstract from Chapter 1 metadata:]

This book comprises contributions on vocal aspects of attractiveness, social likability, and charisma. Despite some apparent distinct characteristics of these three concepts, there are not only similarities, but even interdependencies to be considered. This chapter introduces and regards the concepts studied, methods applied, and material selected in the contributions. Based on this structured summary, we argue to increase interdisciplinary and even holistic efforts in order to better understand the concepts for voice and speech in humans and machines.

---

## Our Interpretation

This edited volume brings together 17 chapters from researchers across phonetics, evolutionary biology, social psychology, and speech technology to consolidate the state of the art on how voice properties create social impressions of attractiveness, likeability, and charisma. The book spans production acoustics, perceptual evaluation, and technological applications (robot/synthetic voice design). For Qlatt, it serves as a comprehensive reference for the speaker personality research effort, with several chapter authors (Kreiman, Babel, Quene, Burkhardt) already represented in the paper collection.
