## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Hunnicutt (1980) [3]** - The ~500 letter-to-sound rules used in Klattalk come from this paper. Essential for understanding G2P rule design and the linguistic principles behind context-dependent grapheme conversion.

2. **Klatt (1979) [4]** - The segmental duration rules referenced throughout. Critical for implementing natural-sounding prosody with percentage-based duration modification.

3. **Klatt (1980) [5]** - The foundational Klatt synthesizer paper. Already in our collection as `Klatt_1980_CascadeParallelFormantSynthesizer`.

4. **Maeda (1974) [6]** - Source of the "hat-pattern" F0 contour strategy. Important for implementing prosodic F0 rules.

5. **Allen et al. (1979) [1]** - MITalk system documentation. Useful for comparison and the morpheme dictionary approach mentioned in Future Plans.
