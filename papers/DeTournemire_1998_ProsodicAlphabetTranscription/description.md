Presents a method for rapidly adapting prosodic models to new voices in French TTS by automatically identifying a prosodic alphabet at the acoustic level. The alphabet consists of break labels (pause durations, syllable lengthening), F0 shape labels (rise, fall, flat, and compound shapes classified by semitone thresholds), and accent labels (secondary and emphatic). A syllable elasticity factor (z-score normalization of segment durations) models duration, while F0 is stylised using 4 points per syllable with linear interpolation. The automatically transcribed corpus trains two-layer neural networks for F0 and duration prediction. Integrated into the CNET French TTS system, the automatically trained prosody achieves subjective quality equivalent to the hand-crafted CNETVOX system as rated by 16 naive listeners.
