## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Hanson (1997)** - "Glottal characteristics of female speakers: Acoustic correlates" - the direct companion paper with female speaker data; essential for complete gender comparison data
2. **Klatt and Klatt (1990)** - "Analysis, synthesis, and perception of voice quality variations" - KLGLOTT88 model used for predictions, voice quality synthesis framework directly relevant to our synthesizer
3. **Holmberg et al. (1988)** - Glottal airflow measurements providing the empirical basis for chink area estimates and minimum airflow values used in Table II predictions
4. **Hanson (1995a)** - Ph.D. thesis containing full theoretical derivations for all five acoustic measures and correction factors
5. **Stevens (1998)** - Acoustic Phonetics textbook with theoretical framework for spectral effects of glottal configuration
