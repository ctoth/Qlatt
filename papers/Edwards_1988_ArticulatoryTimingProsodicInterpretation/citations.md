## Key Citations (most relevant to Qlatt project)

- **Klatt 1976** — Linguistic uses of segment duration in English. The primary duration model this paper critiques and extends.
- **Beckman & Edwards 1987** — Phonological and phonetic basis of final lengthening. Companion paper with more detail on phrase-final lengthening mechanisms.
- **Fant 1987** — Interactive phenomena in speech production. Connection to F1 synthesis and articulatory timing.
- **Lindblom 1963** — Spectrographic study of vowel reduction. Foundational work on how duration affects formant targets.
- **Pierrehumbert & Beckman (in press)** — Japanese tone structure. Framework for prosodic hierarchy used in this paper.
- **Cooper & Eady 1986** — Metrical phonology in speech production. Stress clash and metrical grid predictions tested in this paper.
