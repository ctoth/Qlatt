Holmberg et al. (1995) compare aerodynamic measures (glottal airflow waveform, DC flow, maximum flow declination rate), electroglottographic adduction quotient, and acoustic spectral slope measures (H1-H2, H1-F1, H1-F3, F1-F3) for 20 normal female speakers in comfortable and loud voice conditions. The study establishes normative relationships between these domains, finding that flow-adduction quotient correlates strongly with H1-H2 (r = -0.69) and that H1-H2 can substitute for adduction quotient when inverse filtering is unreliable. The Appendix provides mean and SD values for all measures across speech tasks and loudness conditions, serving as a reference for female voice synthesis parameters.
