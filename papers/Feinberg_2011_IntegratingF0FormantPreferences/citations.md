## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Feinberg et al. 2005** — Original F0+formant manipulation method and attractiveness data; foundational for this project's voice preset manipulation approach
- **Fitch & Giedd 1999** — MRI-based vocal tract length measurements; height explains >70% VTL variance; essential for physiologically grounded VTL modeling
- **Smith et al. 2005, 2007** — F0 × VTL interactions in size/sex/age perception; provides the perceptual framework underlying the attractiveness interaction
- **Puts et al. 2007** — F0 and formant frequencies as dominance signals; relevant for voice quality presets targeting perceived authority
- **Candolin 2003** — Theoretical framework for cue amplification vs backup signaling in multi-cue assessment; conceptual foundation for understanding how vocal parameters interact
