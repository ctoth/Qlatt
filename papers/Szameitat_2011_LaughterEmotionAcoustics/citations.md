## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Laukka & Juslin (2005)** — Dimensional approach to vocal expression of emotion in speech; frequently cited as primary comparison point for speech–laughter convergence
- **Williams & Stevens (1972)** — Foundational JASA paper on emotions and speech acoustics; one of the earliest systematic acoustic analyses
- **Szameitat et al. (2009b)** — Companion JASA paper with detailed acoustic profiles of distinct emotional expressions in laughter (more granular than the dimensional approach here)
- **Schroeder et al. (2001)** — Acoustic correlates of emotion dimensions specifically oriented toward speech synthesis
- **Hammerschmidt & Jürgens (2007)** — Acoustical correlates of affective prosody; cited for prosodic structure characterization
