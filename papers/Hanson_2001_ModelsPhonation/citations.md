## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Hanson & Stevens (submitted)** — Full HLsyn mapping equations from quasi-articulatory to Klatt parameters. This is the most important missing piece for implementing the higher-level control described in this paper.
- **Kuo (1998)** — Two-mass model with vocal-fold nodule modifications. Contains the detailed aerodynamic equations and parameter values for the modified model.
- **Slifka (2000)** — Respiratory constraints at prosodic boundaries. Links breathing mechanics to phrase-final voice quality changes (glottalization, breathiness), directly relevant to prosodic voice quality rules.
- **Redi & Shattuck-Hufnagel (2001)** — Companion paper in same journal issue documenting variation in glottal events across normal speakers. Provides the empirical basis for when glottalization occurs in American English.
- **Stevens & Bickley (1991)** — Earlier version of higher-level Klatt parameter control (already in collection). The precursor to HLsyn with 10 parameters instead of 13.
