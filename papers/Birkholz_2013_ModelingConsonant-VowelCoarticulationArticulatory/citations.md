## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **[16] Öhman SEG (1967) Numerical model of coarticulation.** The foundational superposition model with a context-independent coarticulation function. Birkholz positions his method as the alternative, so this is required background for understanding what problem the corner-vowel interpolation is solving.
- **[42] Lindblom B (1963) Spectrographic study of vowel reduction.** The undershoot model that justifies treating vowel targets as invariant while only consonants get context-sensitive targets. This asymmetry is the load-bearing assumption of the whole approach.
- **[53] Birkholz, Kröger, Neuschaefer-Rube (2011) Model-based reproduction of articulatory trajectories for consonant-vowel sequences.** Source of the critically damped sixth-order transition model with per-consonant time constants, which is directly reusable in a formant synthesizer's parameter trajectory generator.
- **[47] Birkholz, Kröger, Neuschaefer-Rube (2010) Articulatory synthesis and perception of plosive-vowel syllables with virtual consonant targets.** The virtual-target concept (overshooting the reachable articulator position so release lands mid-transition), one of the more portable tricks in the paper.
- **[15] Birkholz, Jackèl, Kröger (2007) Simulation of losses due to turbulence in the time-varying vocal tract.** The noise source model that the paper repeatedly identifies as the single biggest limitation on plosive and fricative recognition.
- **[36] Birkholz P (2005) 3D-Artikulatorische Sprachsynthese.** The complete vocal tract, area function and acoustic tube model that this paper only summarizes. Needed for anyone reimplementing the area function computation.
