## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Banse & Scherer 1996 [5]** — Acoustic profiles of 14 emotions; the most comprehensive multi-emotion acoustic dataset. Would provide quantitative F0/amplitude targets per emotion for synthesis.
- **Scherer 1986 [50]** — The component process model of vocal affect; foundational theory predicting emotion-specific acoustic patterns including the "coping potential" dimension not tested in this paper.
- **Gobl & Chasaide 2003 [17]** — Voice quality (breathy/creaky) in emotion — complements this paper's pitch/amplitude focus with spectral slope and jitter/shimmer data.
- **Juslin & Laukka 2003 [1]** — Comprehensive review of emotion in voice and music; would provide a broader synthesis of acoustic cue utilization.
- **Ohala 1984 [45]** — Frequency code hypothesis for F0 cross-linguistically; the ethological foundation for why low pitch signals dominance/aggression.
