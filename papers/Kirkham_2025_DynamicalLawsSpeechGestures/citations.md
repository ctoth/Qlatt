## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Sorensen & Gafos (2016)** — "The gesture as an autonomous nonlinear dynamical system" — The nonlinear cubic model that Kirkham validates with data-driven discovery. Essential for understanding the theoretical framework.

2. **Turk & Shattuck-Hufnagel (2020)** — *Speech timing* book — General Tau model for articulatory movements, an alternative to task dynamics that Kirkham discusses as fitting position/velocity better.

3. **Saltzman & Munhall (1989)** — Already in collection. The foundational task-dynamic paper that Kirkham's findings modify (under-damping vs critical damping).

4. **Brunton, Proctor, & Kutz (2016)** — The SINDy method paper. Essential for understanding the computational methodology.

5. **Iskarous, Cole, & Steffman (2024)** — Minimal dynamical model of intonation with tone contrast and scaling as emergent properties. Relevant for prosody modeling in Qlatt.
