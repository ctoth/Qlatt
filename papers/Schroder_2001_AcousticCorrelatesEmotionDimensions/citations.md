## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **[14] Schröder, "Emotional Speech Synthesis: A Review", Eurospeech 2001** —
  this is the paper the directory name claims to hold and which was actually
  requested. DOI `10.21437/Eurospeech.2001-150`. Still needs retrieval.
- **[10] Murray & Arnott (1993), JASA 93(2), 1097-1108** — the canonical review of
  human vocal emotion correlates, and the source of the per-emotion acoustic
  profiles this paper's rules are validated against. The primary reference for
  building emotion rules into a formant synthesizer.
- **[1] Banse & Scherer (1996)** — acted-corpus acoustic profiles including
  spectral-band energy measures; this paper contradicts it on the
  power/spectral-slope relation, so both are needed to adjudicate.
- **[6] Cowie, Sawey & Douglas-Cowie (1995), ASSESS** — required to pin down the
  operational definitions of the nine acoustic variables in Tables 1 and 2,
  especially 'spectral slope non-fricatives' and 'tune duration'.
- **[9] Hammarberg et al. (1980)** — the three-spectral-band voice-quality
  measures approximated here; relevant to mapping emotion onto glottal source
  parameters.
