## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Henrich, D'Alessandro, and Doval (2001)** - Theoretical analysis of how OQ and pulse asymmetry jointly determine H1-H2 in the LF model. Shows that asymmetry's influence increases with OQ. Essential for understanding the theoretical underpinning of this paper's empirical findings.

2. **Klatt & Klatt (1990)** - The KLGLOTT88 source model where H1-H2 is perfectly correlated with OQ. This paper directly challenges that simplification. Already in papers collection.

3. **Iseli, Shue, and Alwan (2007)** - The formula used to correct H1*-H2* for vocal tract effects. Important for any analysis pipeline that measures voice source characteristics from audio.

4. **Hawks & Miller (1995)** - Formant frequency-to-bandwidth mapping function. Used as baseline for bandwidth estimation in synthesis when OQ <= 0.7.

5. **Doval, D'Alessandro, and Henrich (2006)** - Comprehensive treatment of glottal flow model spectra. Provides frequency-domain representations that complement this paper's empirical findings. Already in papers collection.
