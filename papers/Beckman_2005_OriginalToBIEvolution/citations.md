## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Pierrehumbert (1980)** — The foundational AM model that MAE_ToBI encodes. Essential for understanding what the tone labels mean phonologically.

2. **Beckman & Hirschberg (1994), *The ToBI Annotation Conventions*** — The actual labeling guide document; this chapter explains the "why", that document specifies the "how" in operational detail.

3. **Silverman et al. (1992)** — The original ICSLP paper introducing ToBI to the speech technology community. Contains the first inter-transcriber reliability results.

4. **Wightman et al. (1992)** — Durational correlates of prosodic phrase boundaries (BI=3 and BI=4). Directly relevant for duration rules in synthesis.

5. **Pierrehumbert & Hirschberg (1990)** — The meaning of intonation contours in discourse. Maps ToBI sequences to pragmatic/discourse functions, which is needed for choosing appropriate tune patterns in TTS.
