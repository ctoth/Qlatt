## Key Citations (Most Relevant to Qlatt)

- **Coleman 1960**: Acoustic correlates of hoarseness — early measurement of cycle-to-cycle variation in pathological voices
- **Moore 1962**: Physiology of hoarseness — vocal fold behavior underlying harsh quality
- **Thompson 1962**: Wavelength perturbation measurement in pathological larynges — quantitative jitter data from real voices

## Companion Paper

- **Wendahl, R. W.** (1966). Laryngeal analog synthesis of jitter and shimmer: Auditory parameters of harshness. *Folia Phoniatrica*, 18(2), 98-108. DOI: 10.1159/000263059 — Follow-up study explicitly examining both jitter and shimmer contributions to harshness perception.
