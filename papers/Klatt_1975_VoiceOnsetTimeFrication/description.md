Systematic spectrographic measurements of voice onset time, burst frication duration, and aspiration duration for English word-initial consonant clusters involving plosive consonants. Klatt decomposes VOT into burst and aspiration components, showing aspiration duration is nearly constant across places of articulation while burst duration increases from labial to velar. The paper provides detailed VOT prediction rules for connected speech, organized by stress position, preceding segment voicing, and cluster composition, along with a discussion of six perceptual cues to the voiced/voiceless distinction.
