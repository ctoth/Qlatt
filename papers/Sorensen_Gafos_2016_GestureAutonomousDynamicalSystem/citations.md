## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Saltzman & Munhall (1989)** — The foundational task-dynamic model that this paper revises. Essential for understanding gestural dynamics in speech production. Already in collection.

2. **Browman & Goldstein (1990)** — Gestural specification using dynamically-defined articulatory structures. The phonological framework that pairs with the dynamical model. Not in collection.

3. **Byrd & Saltzman (2003)** — "The elastic phrase" — models boundary-adjacent lengthening using gestural dynamics, directly relevant for prosodic timing in synthesis.

4. **Kröger et al. (1995)** — The continuous activation function proposal that this paper argues against. Important for understanding the nonautonomous alternative.

5. **Munhall et al. (1985)** — Original empirical data on speech movement velocity profiles that motivated the kinematic predictions tested in this paper.
