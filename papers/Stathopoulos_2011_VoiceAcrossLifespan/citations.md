## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Abitbol, Abitbol, & Abitbol (1999)** — Sex hormones and the female voice; directly relevant to understanding hormonal effects on voice parameters for female speaker profiles
2. **Linville (2001)** — Comprehensive book on vocal aging; likely contains detailed acoustic parameter tables for elderly voice synthesis
3. **Hollien (1987)** — Review of old voices; foundational for elderly voice quality modeling
4. **Huber et al. (1999)** — Formant data for children and adults from this same research group; extends beyond F0/SPL/SNR to formant frequencies
5. **Kent (1976)** — Anatomical and neuromuscular maturation of speech; relevant for child voice parameter development
