## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Story, Titze, and Hoffman (1996)** - Vocal tract area functions from MRI. JASA 100, 537-554. Provides the cross-sectional area data that complements this paper's midsagittal length data. Essential for tube-model formant synthesis.

2. **Lee, Potamianos, and Narayanan (1999)** - Acoustics of children's speech. JASA 105, 1455-1468. Provides formant frequency measurements for 436 children ages 5-17, directly complementing this paper's anatomical data and enabling validation of VTL-to-formant predictions.

3. **Fant (1966)** - Note on vocal tract size factors and non-uniform F-pattern scalings. STL-QPSR 4, 22-30. The theoretical basis for non-uniform formant scaling that this paper's segment-specific growth data validates.

4. **Baer, Gore, et al. (1991)** - Analysis of vocal tract shape and dimensions using MRI: Vowels. JASA 90, 799-828. Earlier MRI study of adult vocal tract shape/dimensions during vowel production, providing complementary articulation-dependent data.

5. **Titze (1989)** - Physiologic and acoustic differences between male and female voices. JASA 85, 1699-1707. Acoustic correlates of the anatomical sex differences documented in this paper.
