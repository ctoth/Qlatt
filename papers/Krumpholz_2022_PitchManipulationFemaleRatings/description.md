This paper investigates how increasing female voice pitch by approximately 20 Hz affects male listeners' ratings of attractiveness, femininity, health, and age in both audio-only and audiovisual conditions, using 20 speakers and 104 male raters in a within-subject design with naturalistic German sentences. The key finding is that increased voice pitch significantly boosts perceived femininity and reduces perceived age by ~2 years, but does not significantly affect perceived attractiveness or health, contradicting prior studies that used less naturalistic stimuli. For speech synthesis voice profiles, this establishes that modest F0 adjustments primarily affect femininity and age perception rather than attractiveness, and that cross-modal effects of voice pitch on face perception are limited to age judgments only.
