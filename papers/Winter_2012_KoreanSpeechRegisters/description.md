Sociophonetic study of 16 Korean speakers comparing formal (contaymal) and informal (panmal) speech registers across 16 acoustic measures. Formal speech shows lower F0 (-17 Hz), reduced pitch range (-1.43 semitones), decreased perturbation (jitter/shimmer), increased HNR, slower rate, and register-specific breath patterns. Provides quantitative evidence that formality affects the entire phonetic profile, not just individual features.
