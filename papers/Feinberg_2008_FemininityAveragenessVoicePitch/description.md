This paper tests whether men prefer feminine (high) F0 or average F0 in women's voices, using both correlational analysis (N=123) and PSOLA pitch manipulation experiments (±20 Hz at three starting pitch levels). The key finding is a strong linear pitch–attractiveness relationship (r=0.341, linear model 3145× more likely than quadratic by AIC), with men preferring raised pitch even in already-high-pitched voices, and the effect being stronger for lower starting pitches. For a Klatt synthesizer, this provides empirical justification for targeting female voice presets at F0 well above the population average (~220 Hz), in the 240–260 Hz range, and confirms that source F0 and vocal tract formants are perceptually independent dimensions of attractiveness.
