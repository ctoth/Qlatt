## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Hart, J. 't, Collier, R., and Cohen, A. (1990). A perceptual study of intonation. Cambridge University Press, Cambridge.**
   - Fundamental reference for the IPO intonation model used throughout the thesis for F0 contour annotation and analysis

2. **Pierrehumbert, J. (1980). The phonology and phonetics of English intonation. Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge, Massachusetts.**
   - Foundational work on autosegmental-metrical intonation theory; provides the theoretical framework for intonation pattern categorization

3. **Murray, I. R., and Arnott, J. L. (1993). "Toward the simulation of emotion in synthetic speech: a review of the literature on human vocal emotion," Journal of the Acoustical Society of America, 93, 1097-1108.**
   - Comprehensive review of emotional speech synthesis literature; directly relevant to implementing emotion-specific prosodic parameter modifications in formant synthesis

4. **Cahn, J. E. (1990). Generating expression in synthesized speech. Technical report, MIT Media Lab., Boston.**
   - Practical implementation of emotional prosody generation in TTS systems; provides concrete parameter manipulation strategies for emotional expression

5. **Scherer, K. R. (1989). "Vocal measurement of emotion". In: R. Plutchik and H. Kellerman (Eds.) Emotion: theory, research, and experience, Volume 4, Academic Press, San Diego, 233-259.**
   - Fundamental reference for understanding acoustic correlates of emotional expression; establishes the bridge between voice source characteristics and perceived emotion
