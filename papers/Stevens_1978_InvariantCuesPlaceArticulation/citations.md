## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Blumstein & Stevens 1979 — "Perceptual invariance and onset spectra for stop consonants"**: The acoustic production companion study. Already in collection as `Blumstein_Stevens_1979_AcousticInvariance`.

2. **Zue 1976 — "Acoustic characteristics of stop consonants"**: Detailed acoustic measurements used as basis for many of the stimulus design decisions. Already in collection as `Zue_1976_StopConsonantAcoustics`.

3. **Fant 1960 — Acoustic Theory of Speech Production**: Theoretical foundation for the formant-shift predictions used to derive the three spectral templates. Already in collection.

4. **Halle, Hughes & Radley 1957 — "Acoustic properties of stop consonants"**: Earlier work establishing burst frequency properties that this paper builds upon.

5. **Cole & Scott 1974 — "Toward a theory of speech perception"**: Proposed that onset spectra join the burst to the vowel smoothly; Stevens & Blumstein build on this notion of spectral continuity.
