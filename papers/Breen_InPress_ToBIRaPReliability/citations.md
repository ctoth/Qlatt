## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Dilley & Brown 2005** - The RaP labeling system specification (Version 1.0). Essential for understanding the full RaP label inventory and annotation guidelines.
2. **Beckman & Ayers Elam 1997** - ToBI labeling guidelines Version 3. The authoritative specification of the ToBI system used in this study.
3. **Pierrehumbert 1980** - The autosegmental-metrical theory underlying both ToBI and RaP. Foundation for understanding the theoretical framework.
4. **Carletta 1996** - The kappa statistic methodology used for chance-corrected agreement. Important for understanding the statistical framework.
5. **Wightman et al. 1992** - Segmental durations near prosodic boundaries. Directly relevant to Qlatt's duration rules for boundary-adjacent segments.
