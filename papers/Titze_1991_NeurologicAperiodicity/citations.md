## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Baer 1979** - Foundation work establishing that single motor unit twitches in cricothyroid muscle produce measurable F0 perturbation. Directly motivated Titze's model.
2. **Larson, Kempster, & Kistler 1987** - Direct experimental evidence that discharge of single motor units in CT and TA muscles causes F0 changes. Validates the model's premise.
3. **Horii 1979** - Establishes measured baseline jitter values for sustained phonation in normal voices. Key comparison data for the model predictions.
4. **Pinto & Titze 1990** - Unification of perturbation measures (jitter, shimmer, CV). Defines the measurement framework used in this paper; important for implementing compatible perturbation metrics.
5. **Titze, Luschei, & Hirano 1989** - TA muscle's role in F0 regulation, including the ISI data (14% CV) used as a key model parameter.
