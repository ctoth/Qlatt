## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Pierrehumbert (1980)** — Foundational ToBI theory; already in collection. The H*+L tone removed from ToBI is discussed as a potential solution for non-final fall-rises.
- **Silverman et al. (1992)** — The ToBI standard definition paper; essential reference for anyone implementing ToBI-based prosodic annotation.
- **De Pijper and Sanderman (1993)** — Perceptual boundary strength methodology that Roach's experiment follows; relevant for validating break-index assignment.
- **'t Hart, Collier and Cohen (1990)** — IPO intonation system; an alternative perceptual approach to intonation that might inform F0 contour generation differently from ToBI.
- **Ladd and Campbell (1991)** — Prosodic structure and boundary hierarchies; relevant to the | vs || boundary question.
