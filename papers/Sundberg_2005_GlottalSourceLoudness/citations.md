## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Holmberg et al. (1988)** [#7] — Foundational data on glottal airflow vs. loudness in untrained voices; provides comparison baseline for soft/normal/loud conditions
- **Titze (1992)** [#16] — Defines phonation threshold pressure and the PSEN normalization used throughout this paper; essential for implementing effort-based control
- **Sundberg et al. (1999)** [#14] — Same methodology on professional baritone singers; useful for comparing trained vs. untrained voice source behavior
- **Hanson (1997)** [#3] — H1-H2 measurements in female speakers; we already have this paper (Hanson_1995_GlottalCharacteristicsFemale); validates the H1-H2 vs Qclosed relationship
- **Fant (1960)** [#1] — Establishes that MFDR determines first formant level and hence perceived loudness; we already have this paper
