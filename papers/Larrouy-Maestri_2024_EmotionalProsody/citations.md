## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Banse & Scherer (1996)** - Already in collection as `Banse_1996_VocalEmotionAcousticProfiles`. Foundational acoustic profiles for 14 emotions with 29 parameters.

2. **Gobl & Chasaide (2003)** - Already in collection as `Gobl_2003_VoiceQualityEmotion`. Voice quality parameter trajectories for seven voice qualities mapped to emotional activation.

3. **Cowen et al. (2019)** - Demonstrates 12 emotion categories are reliably recognized in speech prosody across cultures. Could provide finer-grained emotion targets beyond basic 6.

4. **Laukka et al. (2016)** - Lens model analysis of emotion expression/recognition across 5 nations with acoustic features. Would complement the cross-cultural perspective.

5. **van Rijn & Larrouy-Maestri (2023)** - Models individual and cross-cultural variation in emotion-to-prosody mapping. Lead author's own follow-up with concrete modeling approach.
