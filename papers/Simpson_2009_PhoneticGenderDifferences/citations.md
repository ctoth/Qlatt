## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Titze 1989** - "Physiologic and acoustic differences between male and female voices" JASA 85:1699-1707 - Computational model predicting that female vocal folds never fully close, producing inherent breathiness. Directly relevant to voice source modeling.
- **Henton 1989** - "Fact and fiction in the description of female and male pitch" - Demonstrates that the apparent larger female pitch range disappears when measured in semitones. Critical for F0 contour generation.
- **Diehl et al. 1996** - "On explaining certain male-female differences in phonetic realization of vowel categories" J. Phonetics 24:187-208 - Tests F0-vowel space interaction hypothesis. Relevant to understanding formant estimation at high F0.
- **Fant 1966** - "A note on vocal tract size factors and non-uniform F-pattern scalings" STL-QPSR 4:22-30 - Original analysis of non-uniform male-female formant scaling. Foundational for voice preset design.
- **Simpson 2001** - "Dynamic consequences of differences in male and female vocal tract dimensions" JASA 109:2153-2164 - Articulatory speed differences and their acoustic consequences. Relevant to formant transition modeling.
