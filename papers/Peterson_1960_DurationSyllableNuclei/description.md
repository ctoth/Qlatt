Systematic spectrographic study of how preceding and following consonants affect the duration of stressed vowels and diphthongs in 1263 American English CNC words. Establishes the foundational finding that vowels are approximately 50% longer before voiced consonants than voiceless ones (2:3 ratio), and provides intrinsic duration values for 15 syllable nuclei types. Also describes segmentation criteria for spectrographic measurements and documents that initial consonant voicing has negligible effect on nucleus duration.
