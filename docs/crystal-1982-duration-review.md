# Duration table corpus review

Crystal & House (1982), Table VI supplies paired FAST/SLOW means for vowels,
nonvocalic sonorants, stops, fricatives, and affricates. For category ratio `q`
and vowel ratio `qv = 93.4 / 111.6`, the rule uses exponent `log(q) / log(qv)`
and duration factor `rate ** -exponent`. Choosing the vowel ratio as the rate
anchor and interpolating with a power law are engineering adaptations.

This models relative tempo response, not conversion from isolated words to
connected speech. The default rate remains neutral. The paper's 0.79 pause
ratio is not applied to speech segments; pause policy is unchanged. Existing
duration floors, caps, and millisecond rounding still apply.

corpus golden remains unchanged; a separate fast-rate golden records the new
behavior. DECtalk is an unchanged control at the same requested rate.
