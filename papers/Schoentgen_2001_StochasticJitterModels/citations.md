## Full Reference

## Key Citations (most relevant to Qlatt)

- Titze, I. R. (1984, 1988): Ribbon model of vocal fold vibration — the physical basis for all five jitter models
- Titze, I. R. (1989): *Principles of Voice Production* — T_m values (1.0 ms male, 0.35 ms female)
- Wendahl, R. W. (1963): Laryngeal analog synthesis of harsh voice quality
- Klatt, D. H., and Klatt, L. C. (1990): Analysis of voice quality variations among speakers — flutter and aspiration noise
- Childers, D. G., and Wu, K. (1990): Quality of speech produced by analysis-synthesis
- De Gucchteneere, R. (1995, 1996): Stochastic models applied to jitter analysis
- Schoentgen, J., and De Guchteneere, R. (1995): Time series analysis of jitter
- Winkler and Raming (1992): Published microtremor data — microtremor frequency values 4.7-10.6 Hz
- Box, G. E. P., and Jenkins, G. M. (1970): *Time Series Analysis* — AR model methodology
- Baken, R. J., and Orlikoff, R. F. (1997): Correlation dimension of glottal cycle data
- Pinto and Titze (1990): Unification of perturbation measures
- Koike, Y. (1973): Application of inverse filtering for detecting laryngeal pathology
- Lieberman, P. (1963): Some acoustic measures of the fundamental periodicity of normal and pathological larynges
- Hollien, H., and Shipp, T. (1972): Speaking fundamental frequency and chronologic age
- Crystal, T. H., and House, A. S. (1988): Segmental duration — perturbation time series
- Herzel, H., Berry, D., Titze, I. R., and Saleh, M. (1994): Analysis of vocal disorders with methods from nonlinear dynamics
- Kelley, C. T. (1995): *Iterative Methods for Linear and Nonlinear Equations*
- Steiglitz, K. (1996): *A Digital Signal Processing Primer* — AR filter coefficients
- Chatfield, C. (1984): *The Analysis of Time Series* — statistical methods
