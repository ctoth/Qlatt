## Key Citations (for Qlatt cross-referencing)

- **Liberman et al. 1954** — Predecessor transition-cue experiment establishing that F2 transitions distinguish b/d/g
- **Cooper et al. 1952** — Original Haskins synthetic speech perception experiments
- **Potter, Kopp & Green 1947** — "Hub" concept (predecessor to "locus"), identifying fixed spectral positions for consonants via spectrographic analysis

## Papers in Collection That Cite or Relate to This Work

- **Stevens 1998** — *Acoustic Phonetics* textbook covers locus theory extensively
- **Liberman & Mattingly 1985** — Motor theory of speech perception, built partly on locus/transition findings
- **Blumstein & Stevens 1979** (in collection as reference) — Spectral invariance approach as alternative to locus theory
- **Recasens 1983** — Nasal place cues using formant transitions
- **Lisker & Abramson 1964** — VOT work complementary to place-of-articulation cues
