## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Zue 1976 [5]** - Already in the project collection as `Zue_1976_AcousticCharacteristicsStops`. The most detailed controlled study of stop consonant acoustics, and the source of many VOT measurements referenced in this paper.

- **Klatt 1975 [4]** - VOT and frication/aspiration timing measurements that are directly relevant to parameterizing the AF/AH transition in the Klatt synthesizer. Klatt himself noted the difficulty of separating the two phases.

- **Stevens 1993 [1]** - The source of the classical three-phase model (Fig. 1 in the current paper). Important for understanding the baseline model that this paper challenges.

- **Fant 1960 [2]** - Already in the project collection. Page 185 specifically discusses aspiration as a possible mix of fricative and aspirated sound, which this paper empirically investigates.

- **Stevens, Manuel & Matthies 1999 [6]** - Place of articulation measures using burst spectra. Complements this paper's focus on the post-burst aspiration phase.
