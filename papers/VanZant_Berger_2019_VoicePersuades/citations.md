## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Mehrabian & Williams (1969)** — Original study on perceived and intended persuasiveness paralinguistic cues; foundational reference for voice-persuasion link
- **Schroeder & Epley (2015, 2016)** — Voice vs. text evaluation studies showing speech reveals intellect and affects dehumanization; relevant for understanding how vocal quality shapes social perception in TTS
- **Weinstein, Zougkou, & Paulmann (2018)** — Voice tone and motivation; directly relevant to how prosodic features influence listener behavior
- **Aronovitch (1976)** — Voice quality and personality stereotypes; provides acoustic-personality mappings relevant to voice quality presets
- **Guyer, Fabrigar, & Vaughan-Johnston (2019)** — Speech rate, intonation, and pitch as bias and cue effects of vocal confidence; provides detailed acoustic analysis relevant to synthesis parameters
