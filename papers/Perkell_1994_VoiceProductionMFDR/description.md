# Perkell, Hillman, & Holmberg 1994

Short methodological paper comparing aerodynamic and acoustic voice measurements from 1988 and 1993 studies of normal speakers, identifying that lower 1988 values of maximum airflow declination rate (MFDR) were partly caused by excessive low-pass filtering in the inverse-filtering algorithm. Provides revised MFDR values and confirms that core conclusions about male-female differences and loudness effects remain valid despite the numerical corrections.
