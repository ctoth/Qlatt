## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Deterding, D. (1990)** Ph.D. thesis - the source of every "citation form" comparison value in this paper (Tables 3-6); needed to understand exactly how the earlier /hVd/ citation-word data were collected and measured.
- **Roach, Knowles, Varadi & Arnfield (1993)** - describes the MARSEC corpus this paper's connected-speech data is drawn from, including the "RP or close to it" characterization of its speakers.
- **Zwicker & Terhardt (1980)** - source of the Hz-to-Bark conversion formula used throughout this paper's Bark-scale tables and figures.
- **Ladefoged & Maddieson (1990)** - source of the alternative F2-F1 "backness" representation the paper discusses as an option for speaker-normalized vowel-frontness measures.
