Taxonomy of 6 subtypes of creaky voice with distinct acoustic profiles: prototypical creak, vocal fry, multiply pulsed voice, aperiodic voice, nonconstricted creak, and tense/pressed voice. Maps each type to specific acoustic measures (F0, HNR, H1-H2, jitter, SHR, formant bandwidths). Key reference for understanding that "creaky voice" is not monolithic - different acoustic measures characterize different types.
