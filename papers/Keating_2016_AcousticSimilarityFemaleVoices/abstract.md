# Abstract

## Original Text (Verbatim)

Voices can be similar or different in many ways: many acoustic properties characterize voices. Typical assumption: a few key parameters are critical in distinguishing individual speakers' voices; other parameters will tend to be similar across many speakers. Some candidates in the literature for key parameters: F0, formant frequencies, nasality, breathiness, jitter, shimmer (see references in [1]). Our research question: Are there a few acoustic parameters that do most of the work of characterizing speaker similarity/distinctiveness?

---

## Our Interpretation

This poster investigates which acoustic parameters are most important for making female voices sound distinct from each other, testing 26 parameters across 50 speakers. The surprising finding is that F0 and higher formants (F3, F4) plus voice periodicity measures (CPP, SHR) are more important for speaker discrimination than the spectral tilt measures typically emphasized in voice quality research. This has direct implications for designing speaker profiles in formant synthesizers -- prioritize F0 and formant tuning over source quality parameters for creating distinguishable voices.
