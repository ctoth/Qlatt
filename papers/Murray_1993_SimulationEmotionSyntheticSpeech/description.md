# Murray & Arnott 1993 — Toward the Simulation of Emotion in Synthetic Speech

A comprehensive review of the literature on human vocal emotion, synthesizing findings from decades of experimental studies into a unified summary of how five primary emotions (anger, happiness, sadness, fear, disgust) affect speech parameters. The paper identifies three main parameter categories affected by emotion: voice quality, utterance timing, and pitch contour. Table I provides a ready-to-implement summary mapping emotions to specific acoustic parameter changes (speech rate, pitch average/range, intensity, voice quality, pitch changes, articulation) relative to neutral speech, making this a key reference for implementing emotion in formant synthesizers.
