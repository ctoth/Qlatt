## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Fant, Liljencrants & Lin (1985) [ref 24]** - Original LF-model paper. Essential for understanding glottal source parameters (Rg, Rk, Ra) used in Qlatt.

2. **Klatt & Klatt (1990) [ref 22]** - Voice quality variations including male/female differences. Relevant for extending Qlatt to different voice types.

3. **Klatt (1987) [ref 73]** - Comprehensive review of TTS history. The definitive historical reference.

4. **Stevens & Bickley (1991) [ref 41]** - Higher-level parameters concept. Could inform how Qlatt's semantics layer abstracts synthesizer control.

5. **Gobl & Karlsson (1991) [ref 25]** - Source of Figure 2 showing LF parameter effects on spectrum. Useful for voice quality tuning.
