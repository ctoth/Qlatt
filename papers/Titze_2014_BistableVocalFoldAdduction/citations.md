# Key Citations

## Foundational / Directly Used

- **Scherer, R. C., Shinwari, D., De Witt, K. J., Zhang, C., Kucinschi, B. R., and Afolabi, A. A. (2010)**. "Intraglottal pressures in a 3D model with a non-rectangular glottal shape." J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 128(2), 828-838.
  - Source of empirical intraglottal pressure data used throughout the paper. Replaced ideal Bernoulli pressures with measured values including flow separation, viscous losses, and turbulence.

- **Titze, I. R. (1988b)**. "The physics of small-amplitude oscillation of the vocal folds." J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 83, 1536-1552.
  - Source of phonation threshold pressure equation (Eq. 1).

- **Titze, I. R. (1994)**. *Principles of Voice Production*. Prentice Hall / National Center for Voice and Speech.
  - General reference for vocal fold mechanics and Bernoulli integration approach.

- **Tokuda, I., Horacek, J., Svec, J. G., and Herzel, H. (2010)**. "Bifurcations and chaos in register transitions of excised larynx experiments." Chaos 18.
  - Body stiffness values (30 N/m) used in the two-spring model. Simulation of register shifts.

## Register Physiology

- **Hirano, M. (1974)**. "Morphological structure of the vocal cord as a vibrator and its variations." Folia Phoniatr. 26, 89-94.
  - Cover-body theory of vocal fold structure. Basis for understanding how CT and TA muscles change fold geometry.

- **Hirano, M., and Sato, K. (1993)**. *Histological Color Atlas of the Human Larynx*. Singular Publishing, San Diego.
  - Irregular compartmental arrangement between ligament and TA fibers; surface non-uniformity of vocal folds.

- **van den Berg, J. (1960)**. "Vocal ligaments versus registers." Curr. Probl. Phoniatr. Logop. 1, 19-34.
  - Classical register theory based on vocal ligament function.

- **Hollien, H. (1974)**. "On vocal registers." J. Phon. 2, 125-143.
  - Definition of modal voice, vocal register terminology.

- **Roubeau, B., Henrich, N., and Castellengo, M. (2009)**. "Laryngeal vibratory mechanisms: The notion of vocal register revisited." J. Voice 23(4), 425-438.
  - Direct evidence of lower vocal fold contact being associated with EGG knee. Electroglottographic confirmation.

## Intraglottal Pressure and Flow

- **Titze, I. R. (2006)**. *The Myoelastic Aerodynamic Theory of Phonation*. National Center for Voice and Speech.
  - Theoretical framework for phonation mechanics.

- **Scherer, R. C., and Guo, C. G. (1990)**. "Laryngeal modeling: Transglottal pressure, vocal intensity and the Bernoulli Effect." J. Voice 4(3), 172-178.
  - Flow separation and viscous losses in glottal flow.

## Muscle Activation and Adduction

- **Hirano, M. (1975)**. Demonstrated that LCA adducts vocal processes; TA stiffens body.
  - Referenced for muscle function descriptions.

- **Berke, G. S., Moore, D. M., Gerratt, B. R., Hanson, D. G., Bell, T. S., and Natividad, M. (1988)**. "The effect of recurrent laryngeal nerve section on prephonatory positioning." Laryngoscope 97(7), 1-7.
  - In vivo muscle stimulation results.

- **Choi, H. S., and Berke, G. S. (1996)**. "Identification of laryngeal framework muscle activation..." Arch. Otolaryngol.
  - CT dominates in adduction; LCA reflects TA dominance.

## Phonation Threshold

- **Titze, I. R., Schmidt, S. S., and Titze, M. R. (1995)**. "Phonation threshold pressure in a physical model of the vocal fold mucosa." J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 97, 3080-3084.
  - Physical model validation of threshold pressure equation.

- **Chan, R. W., Titze, I. R., and Titze, M. R. (1997)**. "Further studies of phonation threshold pressure in a physical model." J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 101, 3722-3727.
  - Extended threshold pressure measurements.

## EGG and Register Evidence

- **Titze, I. R. (1990)**. "Interpretation of the electroglottographic signal." J. Voice 4, 1-9.
  - EGG modeling showing knee represents rapid release of lower contact.

- **Hess, M., and Ludwigs, M. (2000)**. Visual observation of vocal fold contact confirming EGG interpretation.

- **Svec, J. G., Schutte, H. K., and Miller, D. G. (1996)**. "A subharmonic vibratory pattern in normal vocal folds." J. Speech Hear. Res. 39, 135-143.

- **Henrich, N., d'Alessandro, C., Doval, B., and Castellengo, M. (2005)**. "Glottal open quotient in singing." J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 117(3), 1417-1430.

## Register Modeling

- **Tokuda, I., Horacek, J., Svec, J. G., and Herzel, H. (2007)**. "Comparison of biomechanical modeling of register transitions and voice instabilities with excised larynx experiments." J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 122, 519-531.

- **Story, B. H., Titze, I. R., and Hoffman, E. A. (1996)**. "Vocal tract area functions from magnetic resonance imaging." J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 100, 537-554.

## Vocal Tract Interaction

- **Alipour, F., and Scherer, R. C. (2012)**. "Ventricular pressures in phonating excised larynges." J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 132(2), 1017-1026.
  - Steady ventricular pressure (pe) measured directly above vocal folds. Found pe/ps ratio ~0.2 for wide gap and 0.6 for narrow gap. Supports the role of supraglottal pressure in register stabilization.

- **Titze, I. R., and Verdolini Abbott, K. (2012)**. *Vocology: The Science and Practice of Voice Habilitation*. National Center for Voice and Speech.
