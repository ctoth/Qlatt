## Key Citations (for Qlatt project)

- **Fant 1960** - Source of the male vocal tract area functions used as input data. Already in collection.
- **Chiba & Kajiyama 1941** - Source of child (girl age 8, boy) vocal tract dimensions.
- **Fant 1975a** - Non-uniform vowel normalization, provides interpolated female-to-male formant difference data used for comparison in Figure 12.
- **Nordstrom & Lindblom 1975** - Companion normalization procedure paper.
- **Pols, Tromp & Plomp 1973** - Dutch vowel formant data showing inter-speaker variability range.
