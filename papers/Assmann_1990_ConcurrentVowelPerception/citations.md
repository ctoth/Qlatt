- **Meddis, R. (1988).** Simulation of auditory-neural transduction: Further studies. *J. Acoust. Soc. Am.*, 83, 1056-1063.
- **Patterson, R. D., & Moore, B. C. J. (1986).** Auditory filters and excitation patterns as representations of frequency resolution. In *Frequency Selectivity in Hearing*, ed. B. C. J. Moore (Academic, London), pp. 123-177. — Roex(p) filter function.
- **Brokx, J. P. L., & Nooteboom, S. G. (1982).** Intonation and the perception of simultaneous voices. *J. Phon.*, 19, 23-26.
- **Summerfield, A. Q., & Assmann, P. F. (1990).** Perception of concurrent vowels: effects of pitch-period asynchrony and harmonic misalignment. *J. Acoust. Soc. Am.* (submitted).
- **Gardner, R. B. (1989).** A algorithm for separating simultaneous vowels. *Br. J. Audiol.*, 23, 170-171.
- **Chalikia, M. H., & Bregman, A. S. (1989).** The perceptual segregation of simultaneous auditory signals: Pulse train segregation and vowel segregation. *Percept. Psychophys.*, 46, 487-496.
- **Weintraub, M. (1985).** A theory and computational model of monaural auditory sound separation. Unpublished Ph.D. thesis, Stanford University. — Related place-time model.
