## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Stilp & Lewicki 2013 [17]** — Direct predecessor: ICA applied to phonetic categories, establishing the β values for broad classes that Deloche refines at phoneme level. Relevant for comparing category-level vs phoneme-level statistical structure.

2. **Lewicki 2002 [11]** — Foundational paper establishing that ICA filters for natural sounds follow Q₁₀ ∝ f^β power law matching cochlear tuning. The starting point for the entire line of research.

3. **Fant 1972 [32]** — Vocal tract wall effects, losses, and resonance bandwidths. Directly relevant to Qlatt's bandwidth modeling; Deloche's vowel β results provide independent validation that bandwidths drive perceptual structure.

4. **Hanna et al. 2016 [28]** — Lip radiation measurements of vocal tract resonances. Connects to Deloche's finding that lip opening degree determines vowel β.

5. **Stevens 1998 [33]** — Acoustic Phonetics. Already in collection; provides the acoustic phonetic framework that Deloche's statistical results quantitatively validate.
