## Key Citations (Most Relevant to Qlatt)

- **Ishizaka & Flanagan 1972**: Two-mass vocal cord model — the "physiological approach" alternative to this paper's "behavioral" approach
- **Fujimura 1968**: "Acoustical approach" — aperiodicity approximation in frequency bands
- **Lindqvist 1972**: Descriptive model of laryngeal articulation — source of the glottal abduction/laryngeal closure dimensions that map to T parameter
- **Ladefoged 1973**: "Glottal stricture" dimension — similar to T parameter
- **Rothenberg 1973**: Inverse filtering technique used to derive the reference glottal waveforms for model development
- **Liljencrants 1971**: Smoothed step commands — the control paradigm used for synthesis rules
- **Fant & Liljencrants 1962**: Formant level definition for voiced sounds

## Papers in Collection That Cite or Relate to This Work

- `Fant_1985_LFModelGlottalFlow` — The LF model built on this behavioral modeling tradition, replacing the analog circuit with a mathematical (exponential + sinusoidal) waveform parameterization
- `Fant_1995_LFModelRevisited` — Further development of the LF parameterization
- `Doval_2003_VoiceSourceCALM` — CALM model also uses physiologically-motivated parameters (similar F/L/T concept)
- `Kreiman_2007_GlottalSourceSpectrum` — Perceptual voice quality assessment relates to the spectral effects of L and T variation
- `Gauffin_1989_SpectralCorrelatesGlottalVoice` — Gauffin (co-author Lindqvist-Gauffin) on spectral correlates of glottal voice source variation
- `Holmberg_1988_GlottalAirflowPressure` — Measurements of glottal airflow that validate the waveform shapes produced by this model
- `Hanson_1995_GlottalCharacteristicsFemale` — Glottal characteristics measurements that relate to the T parameter's effects
