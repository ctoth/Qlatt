## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Leben, William. 1973. *Suprasegmental Phonology*.** -- The direct precursor to autosegmental phonology; Goldsmith builds his theory as a response to problems Leben identified with tone stability and contour tones.

2. **Chomsky, Noam and Morris Halle. 1968. *The Sound Pattern of English*.** -- The standard theory of generative phonology that Goldsmith is revising; essential context for understanding what autosegmental phonology replaces.

3. **Liberman, Mark. 1975. *The Intonational System of English*.** -- Parallel PhD work on English intonation at MIT; directly relevant to our ToBI-based prosody system. Already partially covered by our Pierrehumbert (1980) paper.

4. **Haraguchi, Shosuke. 1975. *The Tone Pattern of Japanese*.** -- Autosegmental analysis of a well-known pitch-accent language; would provide cross-linguistic validation of the association algorithm.

5. **Clements, G.N. 1976. "Vowel Harmony in Non-linear Generative Phonology."** -- Extension of autosegmental theory beyond tone to vowel harmony; cited by Goldsmith as key supporting evidence for the generality of the framework.
