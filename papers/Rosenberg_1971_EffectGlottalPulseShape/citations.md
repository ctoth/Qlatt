## Key Citations (Most Relevant to Qlatt)

- **Miller 1959** (ref 2): Pioneer of inverse filtering; demonstrated pulse-like nature of glottal excitation and that slope at closure increases with vocal effort
- **Flanagan 1958** (ref 11): Properties of glottal sound source; source spectral decay characterization -- **IN COLLECTION** as `Flanagan_1958_PropertiesGlottalSoundSource`
- **Holmes 1962** (refs 6, 7): Found that simulating natural larynx behavior improves quality; source closely simulating inverse-filtered waveform preferred over simple pulse train -- Holmes related work **IN COLLECTION** as `Holmes_1973_GlottalWaveformParallelFormant` and `Holmes_1983_FormantSynthesizersCascadeParallel`
