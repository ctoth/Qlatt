Empirical study establishing vocal fry (pulse register) as a distinct phonational register by demonstrating that its F0 range (2-78 Hz) falls entirely below the modal register with no overlap in any of 22 subjects tested. Provides group and mean frequency ranges for vocal fry, modal, and falsetto registers for both male and female speakers. Key reference for defining F0 boundaries of the three phonational registers.
