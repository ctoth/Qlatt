# Abstract

## Original Text (Verbatim)

The document carries a "Summary" rather than an abstract:

> This Recommendation describes methods and procedures for conducting subjective evaluations of transmission quality. The main revision encompassed by this version of this Recommendation is the addition of an annex describing the Comparison Category Rating (CCR) procedure. Other modifications have been made to align this Recommendation with recent revision of Recommendation P.830.

Source clause:

> ITU-T Recommendation P.800 was revised by ITU-T Study Group 12 (1993-1996) and was approved under the WTSC Resolution No. 1 procedure on the 30th of August 1996.

Keywords (as printed):

## Our Interpretation

P.800 answers the question of how to obtain a subjective speech-quality number that is comparable across laboratories, languages and years. It specifies the Absolute Category Rating listening test and its five-point Excellent-to-Bad scale whose mean is the MOS, the Degradation and Comparison Category Rating variants for small impairments and for systems that may improve quality, and the quantal-response and threshold methods for detectability and equivalence work. Its substance is the surrounding constraints: acoustic conditions, noise spectra, speech-material construction, level normalization, listener eligibility, session length, mandatory MNRU reference anchors, and the prescribed statistical treatment. For a speech synthesis project it is the citable protocol that turns subjective quality assessment into a repeatable measurement.
