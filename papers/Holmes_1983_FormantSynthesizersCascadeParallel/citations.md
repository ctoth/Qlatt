## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Klatt [3] 1980** - "Software for a cascade/parallel formant synthesizer" - The paper Holmes is responding to; essential for understanding the cascade/parallel hybrid approach that Qlatt implements.

2. **Holmes [17] 1973** - "The influence of glottal waveform on the naturalness of speech from a parallel formant synthesizer" - Details on glottal source modeling and the original JSRU parallel synthesizer design.

3. **Fant [10] 1960** - "The Acoustic Theory of Speech Production" - Foundational text for all formant synthesis; theory of vocal tract transfer functions.

4. **Flanagan [15] 1972** - "Speech Analysis, Synthesis and Perception" - Comprehensive reference on speech synthesis including source-filter model details.

5. **Rosenberg [19] 1971** - "Effect of glottal pulse shape on the quality of natural vowels" - Glottal waveform modeling that informs the excitation signal design in Section 7.
