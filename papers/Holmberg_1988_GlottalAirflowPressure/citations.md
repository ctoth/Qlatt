## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Rothenberg (1973)** — Foundational inverse-filtering technique used in this study; essential for understanding glottal airflow waveform derivation
2. **Fant (1979, 1982)** — Voice source theory predicting 6 dB SPL increase per doubling of MFDR; links airflow parameters to acoustic output
3. **Stevens (1977, 1981)** — Physics of laryngeal behavior and vibration modes; theoretical basis for understanding vocal fold mechanics
4. **Klatt (1986)** — Detailed spectral analysis of female voice; directly connects to synthesizer parameter settings for female voices
5. **Monsen & Engebretson (1977)** — Male/female glottal wave variations; complements this paper with different methodology
