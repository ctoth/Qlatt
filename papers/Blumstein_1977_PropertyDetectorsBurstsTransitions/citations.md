## Key Citations (Most Relevant to Synthesis)

- **Zue 1976** (ref 4): Acoustic characteristics of stop consonants -- detailed measurements of burst and transition parameters
- **Klatt 1972** (ref 17): Terminal analog speech synthesizer theory -- the synthesizer model used to generate stimuli
- **Fant 1960** (ref 22): Acoustic theory of speech production -- fundamental framework
- **Stevens & Blumstein 1978** (ref 21): Direct follow-up establishing context-independent invariant properties
- **Halle, Hughes & Radley 1957** (ref 2): Foundational measurements of stop consonant acoustics
- **Delattre, Liberman & Cooper 1955** (ref 5): Acoustic loci and transitional cues for consonants
