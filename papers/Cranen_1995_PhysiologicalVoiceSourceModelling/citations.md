## Key Citations for Implementation

- **Titze (1984)**: Baseline parameterization that this paper extends (Q_a, Q_s, Q_p parameters)
- **Ishizaka & Flanagan (1972)**: Two-mass model; basis for the aerodynamic equations
- **Beranek (1986)**: End correction formulas for glottal duct
- **Sondhi & Schroeter (1987)**: Discrete-time convolution scheme used in implementation
- **Holmberg et al. (1988)**: Source of measured glottal flow and pressure values
- **Cranen & Schroeter (1995)**: Companion paper on leaky glottis modeling in J. Phonetics
