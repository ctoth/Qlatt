# Citations

1. Perko L., *Differential Equations and Dynamical Systems*. New York: Springer-Verlag, 1991. — Reference for Hopf Bifurcation Theorem (subcritical vs supercritical classification).

2. Titze I. R., "The physics of small-amplitude oscillation of the vocal folds", *J. Acoust. Soc. Am.* **83**, pp. 1536-1552 (1988). — Mucosal wave model of vocal fold oscillation; phonation threshold physics.

3. Awrejcewicz J., "Bifurcation portrait of the human vocal cord oscillations", *J. Sound Vibration* **136**, pp. 151-156 (1990).

4. Lucero J. C., "Dynamics of the two-mass model of the vocal folds: Equilibria, bifurcations, and oscillation region", *J. Acoust. Soc. Am.* **94**, pp. 3104-3111 (1993). — Bifurcation analysis of the Ishizaka-Flanagan two-mass model.

5. Herzel H. and Knudsen C., "Bifurcations in a vocal fold model", *Nonlinear Dynamics* **7**, pp. 53-64 (1995).

6. Lucero J. C., "A theoretical study on the hysteresis phenomenon at vocal fold oscillation onset-offset", *J. Acoust. Soc. Am.*, in press. — Proof of subcritical Hopf bifurcation in the mucosal wave model; derives theoretical onset/offset ratio of 1/2. (Later published as Lucero 1999, JASA 105(1).)

7. Ishizaka K. and Flanagan J. L., "Synthesis of voiced sounds from a two-mass model of the vocal folds", *Bell System Technical Journal* **51**, pp. 1233-1268 (1972). — Two-mass vocal fold model used in this paper.

8. Titze I. R., *Principles of Voice Production*, Englewood Cliffs: Prentice-Hall, 1994. — Two-tube vocal tract approximation and radiation load model.

9. Pelorson X., Hirschberg A., van Hassel R. R., Wijnands A. P. J., and Auregan Y., "Theoretical and experimental study of quasisteady-flow separation within the glottis during phonation. Application to a modified two-mass model", *J. Acoust. Soc. Am.* **96**, pp. 3416-3431 (1994). — Boundary layer glottal aerodynamics model.

10. Behrman A., "Phonation threshold intensity in healthy and dysphonic voices", preprint. — Voice range profiles as a tool for exploring onset-offset bifurcations.
