## Key Citations for Qlatt Project

- **Fant (1960)** — Already in collection. The canonical acoustic theory reference; Story compares area functions with Fant's data showing significant size differences.
- **Ishizaka and Flanagan (1972)** — Already in collection. Two-mass vocal cord model referenced for speech simulation context.
- **Maeda (1982)** — Already in collection. Digital simulation method for vocal tract.
- **Mermelstein (1973)** — Articulatory model; mouth termination boundary technique used in this paper.
- **Baer et al. (1991)** — Important comparison dataset for vocal tract area functions from MRI (two subjects).
- **Yang and Kasuya (1994)** — Another MRI area function comparison dataset.
- **Sondhi and Schroeter (1987)** — Hybrid articulatory synthesizer, relevant to synthesis architecture.
