# Citations

- Nass, C. & Lee, K. M. (2001). Does computer-synthesized speech manifest personality? Experimental tests of recognition, similarity-attraction, and consistency-attraction.
- Lee, K. M., Nass, C., & Brave, S. (2000). Can computer-generated speech have gender? An experimental test of gender stereotype.

## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Lee, Nass & Brave (2000)** - Can computer-generated speech have gender? Foundational CASA study for TTS voice gender perception.
2. **Mullennix et al. (2003)** - Social perception of male and female computer synthesized speech. Direct predecessor comparing gender effects.
3. **Nass & Lee (2001)** - Computer-synthesized speech personality. Similarity-attraction framework for TTS.
4. **Nass & Moon (2000)** - Machines and mindlessness. Core CASA theory paper.
5. **Mitchell et al. (2011)** - Does the accent of a TTS voice matter? Tests accent effects on TTS perception.
