## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Banse & Scherer 1996** — Comprehensive acoustic profiles for 14 emotions with 29 parameters. Already in collection as `Banse_1996_VocalEmotionAcousticProfiles`. Directly complementary — provides finer-grained acoustic targets than this paper's 5 categories.

2. **Scherer 2003** — Review of vocal emotion communication paradigms, including how emotional arousal covaries with acoustic features. Useful for understanding the theoretical framework linking acoustic parameters to perceived emotion.

3. **Grandjean et al. 2005** — Brain responses to angry prosody in meaningless speech (Nat. Neurosci.). Important predecessor showing right mid STG sensitivity even without semantic content.

4. **Ethofer et al. 2006a** — Effects of prosodic emotional intensity on associative auditory cortex. Same research group's prior work showing the intensity dimension matters.

5. **Lattner et al. 2005** — Voice perception: sex, pitch, and the right hemisphere. Relevant for understanding how F0 differences (sex-related or emotion-related) are processed in right hemisphere regions.
