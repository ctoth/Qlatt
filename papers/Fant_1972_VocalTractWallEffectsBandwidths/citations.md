## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **FANT, G. (1960): Acoustic Theory of Speech Production** — the indispensable companion. Supplies the derivations this paper compresses: surface loss treatment (pp. 32-33, 136, 303-311), the normalized friction expressions and Eq. (52) coefficient (p. 308), the resonator-neck alternative forms (pp. 304-305), the radiation bandwidth expression Eq. (54), the semi-closed-system radiation result (Eq. A.36-21), the complex matrix technique (pp. 37-41), and the double Helmholtz model of [u] (pp. 116, 121).
- **FUJIMURA, O. and LINDQVIST, J. (1971): "Sweep-Tone Measurements of Vocal-Tract Characteristics", JASA 49, 541-558** — the closed-glottis bandwidth measurements underpinning Fig. I-C-4 and Tables I-C-II and I-C-III, and the source of the male/female B1 difference. Any reimplementation should validate against this data.
- **FANT, G. (1956): "On the Predictability of Formant Levels and Spectrum Envelopes from Formant Frequencies"** — states the goal that makes bandwidth prediction worth doing: formant levels and spectrum envelopes follow from the F-pattern once bandwidths are known.
- **ICHIKAWA, A. and NAKATA, K. (1971): "Vocal Tract Resonances with Losses"** — demonstrates how radiation loss depends on constriction position, which Fant identifies as the main uncontrolled factor in B3.
- **HOUSE, A.S. and STEVENS, K.N. (1958): "Estimation of Formant Band Widths from Measurements of Transient Response of the Vocal Tract"** — independent measurement method corroborating the 30-70 Hz range below 2000 Hz.
