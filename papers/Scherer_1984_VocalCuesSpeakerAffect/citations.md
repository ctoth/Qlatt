## Key Citations for Qlatt

- **Pierrehumbert (1980)** -- Intonation framework used for categorizing contours (rise vs. fall = high vs. low boundary tone). Already in collection.
- **Lieberman & Michaels (1962)** -- F0 and envelope amplitude related to emotional content. Foundational work on F0-emotion link.
- **Williams & Stevens (1972)** -- Acoustical correlates of emotion in speech. Key reference for emotion synthesis parameters.
- **Laver (1980)** -- Phonetic description of voice quality. Framework for voice quality parameters relevant to affect.
- **Scherer & Oshinsky (1977)** -- Cue utilization in emotion attribution from auditory stimuli using synthesis. Directly relevant to synthesizer affect implementation.
