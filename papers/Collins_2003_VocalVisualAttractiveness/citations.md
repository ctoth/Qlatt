## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Fitch & Giedd 1999** - Vocal tract morphology via MRI; establishes the body size to formant dispersion relationship that this paper confirms. Critical for voice preset parameterization.
- **Abitbol et al. 1999** - Sex hormones and the female voice; documents how oestrogen affects F0 and formant production, relevant for modeling female voice quality changes.
- **Collins 2000** - Male voice attractiveness using same vowel-based paradigm; companion study that shows women prefer deeper male voices (opposite pattern).
- **Fitch 1997** - Formant frequency dispersion correlates with body size in rhesus macaques; foundational for the body-size-to-voice mapping.
- **Hollien et al. 1997** - Effects of three parameters on speaking fundamental frequency; relevant for understanding F0 variation in synthesized speech.
