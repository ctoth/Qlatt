Investigates how six acoustic voice-source measures (F0, jitter, shimmer, SNR, band-limited SNR, H1-H2) vary with speaker sex, consonantal context, and vowel identity in CV syllables from 8 adult speakers. Finds that voiceless consonants systematically increase F0, jitter, shimmer, and spectral tilt while decreasing SNR in the following vowel, and that SNR indexes different noise sources for men (jitter-dominated) versus women (aspiration-dominated). Directly supports consonantal-context-dependent voice source rules and sex-differentiated speaker presets for Klatt synthesis.
