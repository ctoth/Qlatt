## Key Citations

- **Haggard 1970** (ref 4): Primary comparison study; found similar cluster duration tendencies in British English with 8 speakers
- **Peterson & Lehiste 1960** (ref 5): Segmental duration measurement methodology
- **Huggins 1972** (ref 11): JND for consonant duration; validates perceptual relevance of cluster duration rules
- **Klatt 1971** (ref 3): Earlier Klatt study on /s/ duration prediction (QPR 103)
