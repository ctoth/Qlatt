This ASA poster presents a multi-dimensional scaling and linear discriminant analysis study of 50 female voices (ages 18-29) examining which of 26 acoustic parameters best distinguish individual speakers, using perceptual similarity ratings and VoiceSauce measurements. The key finding is that F0, F3, F4, CPP (cepstral peak prominence), SHR (subharmonic-to-harmonic ratio), and energy do most of the work of speaker discrimination, while traditional voice source spectral measures like H1*-H2* are surprisingly unimportant for distinguishing individual voices. For the Qlatt synthesizer's speaker personality system, this work establishes that speaker profile design should prioritize F0, higher formants (F3/F4), and periodicity measures over source spectral tilt parameters to create perceptually distinct voice profiles.
