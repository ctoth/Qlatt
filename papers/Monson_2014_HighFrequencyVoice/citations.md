## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Tabain (1998)** - "Non-sibilant fricatives in English: spectral information above 10 kHz" - Directly relevant for /f/ and /theta/ HFE spectral characterization, rare study extending above 10 kHz
2. **Moore and Tan (2003)** - "Perceived naturalness of spectrally distorted speech and music" - Provides the critical naturalness threshold data at 7-10.9 kHz
3. **Jongman et al. (2000)** - Already in collection; cited for detailed fricative spectral peaks
4. **Dang and Honda (1997)** - Piriform fossa antiresonance at ~5 kHz - explains spectral dip between F5/F6 and F7
5. **Lippmann (1996)** - Consonant identification data with HFE filtering - establishes intelligibility contribution of 8-10 kHz band
