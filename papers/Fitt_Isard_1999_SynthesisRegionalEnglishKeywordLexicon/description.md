Describes the Unisyn keyword-lexicon architecture for English speech synthesis: an accent-independent lexicon transcribed with Wells-style lexical-set "keysymbols," paired with per-accent post-lexical rules that resolve keysymbols into a target accent's phonetic realizations. Illustrates the approach with Scottish and Irish English segmental phonology and shows how diphone recording word-lists are derived per accent from the same base lexicon. Directly relevant to designing accent as a policy layer over a single declarative TTS lexicon.
