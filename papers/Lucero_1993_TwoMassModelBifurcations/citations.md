## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Ishizaka, K., and Flanagan, J. L. (1972).** The base two-mass model whose equations, notation, and typical parameter values (Table I) this paper adopts wholesale. Essential reading before implementing anything here.
- **Titze, I. (1988).** The main comparison target. Introduces the secondary equilibrium position that corresponds to Lucero's position I, and the small-amplitude oscillation physics this paper extends to large amplitude. Also the source of the pressure-recovery assumption (iv).
- **Conrad, W. A., and McQueen, D. M. (1988).** The negative-differential-resistance oscillation theory that this paper disproves, using the fact that NDR requires a coupling ratio outside the oscillation region.
- **Guckenheimer, J., and Holmes, P. (1983).** The bifurcation-theory apparatus (Hopf, saddle-node, transcritical) used throughout; the cited page ranges are the relevant sections.
- **Lucero, J. C. (1993), Ph.D. dissertation.** The author's full-length treatment of vocal fold oscillation dynamics, of which this paper is a condensation.
