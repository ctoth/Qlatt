## Key Citations

- **Klatt (1975)**: Vowel lengthening in connected discourse - directly relevant predecessor work on duration in context
- **Lehiste (1970, 1979)**: Suprasegmental analysis and sentence/paragraph boundary perception
- **Parmenter & Trevino (1935)**: Early benchmark data for segmental durations used as comparison
- **Umeda (1975)**: Vowel duration data for American English used as comparison
- **Goldman-Eisler (1961, 1968)**: Speech rate and articulation rate studies
- **Harris & Umeda (1974)**: Speaking mode effects on vowel duration
