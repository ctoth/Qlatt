## Key Citations for Qlatt

- **Fant 1960** (Ref. 3): The LEA model used for formant frequency computation; area function theory
- **Perkell 1969** (Ref. 10): Cineradiographic data on larynx height ranges
- **Heinz & Stevens 1965** (Ref. 11): Cross-distance to area function conversion methods
- **Sundberg 1970** (Ref. 16): Formant structure differences between spoken and sung vowels (larynx lowering)
- **Fant 1965** (Ref. 13): Formant-cavity affiliations theory
