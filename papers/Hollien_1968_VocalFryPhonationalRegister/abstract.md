# Abstract

The purpose of this research was to test the hypothesis that vocal fry is a separate phonational register: specifically, whether or not the range of fundamental frequencies associated with vocal fry falls below the range of frequencies associated with the modal register. The frequency limits of the modal and falsetto registers, as well as those for vocal fry, were obtained from 12 males and 11 females. The results provide data that demonstrate vocal fry to be a register as specified.

## Interpretation

This paper provides the foundational empirical evidence that vocal fry constitutes a separate phonational register below modal voice. The key data for synthesis are the F0 boundaries: vocal fry occupies roughly 2-78 Hz, modal voice 71-798 Hz, and falsetto 156-1729 Hz, with no overlap between fry and modal in any subject. This confirms that modeling vocal fry in a synthesizer requires a fundamentally different F0 regime (below ~80 Hz) with discrete, widely-spaced glottal pulses rather than continuous voicing. The finding that fry frequency ranges are nearly identical for males and females is notable for speaker modeling.
