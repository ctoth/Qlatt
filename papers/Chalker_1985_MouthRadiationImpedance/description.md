Compares five models for acoustic radiation impedance of the mouth (pulsating sphere, piston-in-infinite-baffle, piston-in-spherical-baffle, Fant's approximation, Flanagan's approximation) at mouth apertures of 0.9 and 5.0 cm^2 up to 5 kHz. Uses the piston-in-spherical-baffle as ground truth. Proposes a new two-term approximation derived from the piston-in-baffle series that improves accuracy over Fant and Flanagan at higher frequencies and larger apertures, with errors under 1.8 acoustic ohms across both apertures. Directly relevant to the radiation load in formant synthesizers.
