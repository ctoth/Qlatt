# Citations

## Full Reference

Hughes, G. W., & Halle, M. (1956). Spectral properties of fricative consonants. *The Journal of the Acoustical Society of America*, 28(2), 303-310. doi:10.1121/1.1908271

## References Cited in This Paper

1. Harris, K. S. (1954). Cues for the identification of the fricatives of American English. *J. Acoust. Soc. Am.*, 26, 952.
2. Tarnoczy, T. (1954). Die akustische Struktur der stimmlosen Engelaute. *Acta linguistica (Budapest)*, 4, 313-349.
3. Jakobson, R., Fant, C. G. M., & Halle, M. (1952). *Preliminaries to Speech Analysis*. MIT Acoustics Lab. TR 13.
4. Denes, P. (1955). Effects of duration on the perception of voicing. *J. Acoust. Soc. Am.*, 27, 761-764.

## Key Citations

- **Harris 1954** — Showed that formant transitions in adjacent vowels contribute little to fricative identification (except [f] vs [th]); primary cues are in the fricative portion itself.
- **Jakobson, Fant & Halle 1952 (Preliminaries)** — Discussed voiced/unvoiced fricative distinction not necessarily requiring low-frequency voicing component.
