## Key Citations for Qlatt

- **Klatt 1976 [2]** — Already in collection as `Klatt_1976_SegmentalDuration`. Foundational duration rules that Hertz's nucleus model extends.
- **Hertz 1991 [4]** — "Streams, Phones, and Transitions" — the most detailed presentation of the nucleus-based timing model. Important lead for understanding the theoretical foundations.
- **Hertz 1992 [5]** — Cornell working paper with full details of the timing model applied to English duration.
- **Hertz 1990 [8]** — The Delta programming language paper — describes the rule formalism in detail.
- **Klatt & Klatt 1990 [10]** — Already in collection as `Klatt_Klatt_1990_VoiceQualityVariations`.
