# Abstract

## Original Text (Verbatim)

This paper investigates the attractiveness of German male voices for female listeners. The primary finding of this study is that deep voices are not necessarily attractive for women. Even voices with a middle or high fundamental frequency can be judged as attractive.

Furthermore, the analysis shows that there is a combination of various parameters, which are responsible for leaving positive or negative impressions to the listener.

---

## Our Interpretation

The paper challenges the common assumption that low-pitched male voices are inherently attractive, finding instead that F0 alone is a poor predictor of attractiveness. What matters is a combination of acoustic features — particularly F0 modulation, absence of nasality, and fluent speech with few hesitations. For our synthesizer, this suggests that voice preset "attractiveness" is multidimensional and that prosodic variation (F0 standard deviation) matters more than absolute pitch level.
