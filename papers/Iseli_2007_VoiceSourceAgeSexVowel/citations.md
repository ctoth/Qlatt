## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Hanson (1995, 1997)** — Already in collection. Directly comparable female speaker data with the same corrected measures.
2. **Hanson & Chuang (1999)** — Already in collection. Male speaker data with same measures.
3. **Holmberg et al. (1995)** — Links H1*-H2* to physiological open quotient measure; essential for validating synthesis parameter mapping.
4. **Lee et al. (1999)** — Developmental changes in F0 and formants for children; extends the age range of speaker modeling data.
5. **Fant (1995)** — LF model frequency-domain analysis; already in collection as Fant_1985_LFModelGlottalFlow.
