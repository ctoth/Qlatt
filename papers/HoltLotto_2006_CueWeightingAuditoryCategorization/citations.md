## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Francis, A. L., et al. (2000)** - "Effects of training on attention to acoustic cues" - Directly relevant: shows how training shifts cue weighting for speech sounds, potential bridge between nonspeech and speech paradigms
2. **Lutfi, R. A. (1993)** - CoRE (Component-Relative-Entropy) model - Theoretical framework for optimal cue weighting based on distributional properties, would provide computational model
3. **Iverson, P., et al. (2003)** - /l/-/r/ perception interference account - Directly tests cue weighting differences between Japanese and English listeners, relevant for L2 synthesis evaluation
4. **Lisker, L. (1986)** - 16 acoustic features for voicing - Comprehensive inventory of the acoustic cues that need to be weighted in stop voicing synthesis
5. **Nittrouer, S. (2004)** - Developmental cue weighting for stop voicing - Shows how cue weighting changes with age, relevant for understanding listener variation in synthetic speech evaluation
