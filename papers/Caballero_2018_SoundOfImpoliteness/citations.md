## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Scherer, K.R., Ladd, D.R., Silverman, K.E., 1984** - "Vocal cues to speaker affect: testing two models" - Foundational paper on covariance vs configuration models for how acoustic cues encode affect. Critical for understanding categorical vs graded prosodic distinctions.

2. **Hellbernd, N., Sammler, D., 2016** - "Prosody conveys speaker's intentions: acoustic cues for speech act perception" - Direct precedent for linking acoustic measures to pragmatic perception; uses similar "elevation index" measure.

3. **Gussenhoven, C., 2004** - "Paralinguistics: Three Biological Codes" - Theoretical framework (frequency code, effort code) explaining why higher pitch signals politeness cross-linguistically.

4. **Banse, R., Scherer, K.R., 1996** - "Acoustic profiles in vocal emotion expression" - Classic paper on acoustic correlates of emotion that this politeness work builds upon.

5. **Jiang, X., Pell, D.M., 2017** - "The sound of confidence and doubt" - Related work from same lab showing how prosody encodes epistemic states; similar methodology applicable to other pragmatic dimensions.
