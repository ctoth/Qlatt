Proposes relational strong/weak metrical trees and a separate grid for linguistic rhythm.
Develops cyclic English lexical stress, morphological exceptions, destressing and extrametricality,
then explains optional phrase-level retraction through grid clashes and local iambic reversal.
Useful for Qlatt's stress representation and provenance, but supplies no acoustic parameter mapping.
Its cycle retains binary stress marks while rebuilding trees, unlike Hayes's later foot-based model.
