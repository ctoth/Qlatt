## Key Citations for Qlatt

- **Pierrehumbert 1980** — Already in collection. RaP's tonal framework builds on and extends Pierrehumbert's autosegmental-metrical theory.
- **Beckman & Ayers-Elam 1997 (ToBI 3.0)** — The primary comparison system. RaP is designed as an alternative/extension to ToBI.
- **Silverman et al. 1992 (ToBI original)** — The original ToBI specification paper.
- **Ladd & Schepman 2003** — Sagging transitions between accent peaks; relevant to inter-accent F0 interpolation.
- **Hayes 1995 (Metrical Stress Theory)** — Foundation for the rhythm tier's beat/nonbeat labeling.
