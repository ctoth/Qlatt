## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Broad and Clermont (1987)** — "A methodology for modeling vowel formant contours in CVC context" (JASA 81:155-165). Directly relevant to Qlatt's formant transition modeling: provides a framework for parameterizing vowel formant trajectories in consonant contexts.

2. **Kaburagi and Honda (2001)** — "Dynamic articulatory model based on multidimensional invariant-feature task representation" (JASA 110:441-452). Foundation for the invariant phonemic task concept; the idea that phoneme targets can be defined in a low-dimensional subspace where context variation is minimal.

3. **Gay (1977)** — "Articulatory movements in VCV sequences" (JASA 62:185-193). Classic study of articulatory coarticulation patterns in VCV contexts; relevant to understanding how formant transitions should be shaped.

4. **Coker (1976)** — "A model of articulatory dynamics and control" (Proc. IEEE 64:452-460). Early articulatory dynamics model that influenced subsequent work on trajectory formation and could inform Qlatt's transition timing.

5. **Saltzman and Munhall (1989)** — Task dynamics model for speech production. Alternative framework for understanding how articulatory gestures overlap and blend, relevant to coarticulation rule design.
