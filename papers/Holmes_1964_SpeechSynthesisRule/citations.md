## Key Citations for Qlatt

- **Delattre et al. 1955**: Concept of acoustic "loci" for consonant transitions - used for external Fn transitions for stops and nasals (boundary value = average of element and adjacent steady-state values)
- **Kelly & Gerstman 1961; Kelly & Lochbaum 1962**: Prior art - first to use a computer programme to synthesize speech by rule, but used a more powerful computer without analogue synthesizer
- **Liberman et al. 1959**: "Minimal rules for synthesizing speech" - predecessor work on synthesis by rule
- **Shearme & Holmes 1961**: Foundation for the centralization observation - formant frequencies in running speech tend toward central values due to articulator inertia
- **Peterson & Lehiste 1960**: Duration of syllable nuclei - source for vowel duration data
- **House & Fairbanks 1953**: Consonant environment effects on vowel formants
