This paper derives analytical spectral formulas for three glottal flow models (LF, R++, KLGLOTT88) and demonstrates that the commonly used spectral measure H1*-H2* cannot uniquely determine the glottal open quotient because it depends jointly on both open quotient and asymmetry coefficient in 5-parameter models, with only the simpler 4-parameter KLGLOTT88 model providing a monotonic Oq-to-H1*-H2* relationship. Experimental measurements on speech and singing signals reveal that many H1*-H2* values fall outside the domain predicted by any glottal flow model, suggesting either model inadequacy or significant source-filter interaction that invalidates inverse filtering. For the Qlatt synthesizer, this paper is a critical reference establishing that voice quality presets must set both Oq and asymmetry coefficient (not just Oq based on H1*-H2* literature values), and that H1*-H2* alone is insufficient for validating synthesized voice source parameters against natural speech.
