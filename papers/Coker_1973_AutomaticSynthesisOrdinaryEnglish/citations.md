## Key Citations (most relevant to Qlatt)

- **[6] Coker & Umeda 1970** — Vowel duration and pitch prominence data
- **[7] Coker & Umeda 1970** — Word boundary acoustic properties (directly relevant to Qlatt boundary rules)
- **[11] Umeda 1972** — Vowel duration in polysyllabic words (data source for duration model)
- **[12] Monsen et al. 1972** — Allophone data for voiceless stops
- **[13] Coker & Umeda 1972** — Allophonic variation significance and vocal cord data
- **[4] Pike 1945** — Intonation model that the pitch control system expands upon
