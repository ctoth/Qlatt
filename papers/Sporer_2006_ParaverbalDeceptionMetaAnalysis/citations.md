## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **DePaulo et al. (2003)** - Most comprehensive meta-analysis of 158 deception cues including both verbal and nonverbal; broader scope than this paper
2. **Rockwell, Buller, & Burgoon (1997)** - Directly compares acoustic measurements vs perceptual ratings of deceptive voices; most relevant for synthesis
3. **Anolli & Ciceri (1997)** - Vocal strategies of naive vs skilled liars; voice quality analysis
4. **Ekman, Friesen, & Scherer (1976)** - Body movement and voice pitch in deceptive interaction; early acoustic data
5. **Streeter et al. (1977)** - Pitch changes during attempted deception; direct F0 measurements
