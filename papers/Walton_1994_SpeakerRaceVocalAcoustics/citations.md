## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Yumoto, Gould, & Baer (1982)** - Foundational method for H/N ratio calculation used in this study; essential for implementing harmonics-to-noise measurement in the synthesizer's diagnostic pipeline.
2. **Kreiman, Gerratt, Kempster, Erman, & Berke (1993)** - Framework for perceptual evaluation of voice quality; relevant for designing perceptual validation of synthesized voice quality parameters.
3. **Mayo (1990)** - Comprehensive F0 and formant data for African-American vs European-American adults; extends the acoustic data in this paper with formant comparisons.
4. **Murry (1988)** - Vocal tract parameters associated with voice quality preference; directly relevant to setting synthesizer parameters for natural-sounding voices.
5. **Imaizumi (1986a)** - Acoustic measures of roughness; provides additional perturbation measures and their perceptual correlates relevant for voice quality synthesis.
