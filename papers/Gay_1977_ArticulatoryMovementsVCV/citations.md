## Key Citations for Qlatt

- **Ohman 1966** (ref 1): The foundational VCV coarticulation model that Gay challenges/refines. Already in collection.
- **Henke 1966** (ref 9): Dynamic articulatory model — scan-ahead mechanism for anticipatory coarticulation.
- **MacNeilage & DeClerk 1969** (ref 13): Motor control of coarticulation in CVC monosyllables. Relevant to articulation modeling.
- **Lindblom & Sundberg 1971** (ref 25): Acoustical consequences of articulator movement. Directly relevant to formant-from-articulation mapping.
- **Perkell 1969** (ref 22): Quantitative cineradiographic study of speech production — foundational articulatory data.
