## Key Citations for Implementation

- **House & Fairbanks 1953** (Ref 1): Consonant environment effects on vowels — foundational for the C-factor
- **Peterson & Lehiste 1960** (Ref 2): Syllable nuclei duration — baseline vowel duration measurements
- **House 1961** (Ref 3): Vowel duration in English — prior duration measurements
- **Kucera & Francis 1967** (Ref 10): Word frequency data used to define function word boundary (top 50 in rank)
- **Harris & Umeda 1974** (Ref 12): Speaking rate effects on vowel duration — companion paper on rate variation
