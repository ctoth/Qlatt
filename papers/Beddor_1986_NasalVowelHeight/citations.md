## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Hawkins & Stevens 1985** — Acoustic and perceptual correlates of nasal-non-nasal distinction (already in collection as Hawkins_Stevens_1985_NasalVowelCorrelates)
- **House & Stevens 1956** — Analog studies of nasalization of vowels (already in collection as House_Stevens_1956_NasalizationVowels)
- **Chistovich & Lublinskaya 1979** — Centre of gravity effect in vowel perception; foundational for understanding how multiple spectral peaks contribute to perceived vowel quality
- **Ladefoged et al. 1978** — Generating vocal tract shapes from formant frequencies; method used in this paper's articulatory synthesis
- **Wright 1980** — Nasalized vowels in perceptual vowel space; directly tests the perceptual space distortions caused by nasalisation
