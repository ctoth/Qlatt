# Puts, Gaulin & Verdolini (2006) - Dominance and Voice Pitch

## Experimental Design

- 111 male speakers recorded in unscripted dating-game scenario
- Each competitive recording manipulated +/- 1 semitone using CoolEdit 2000
- Male raters judged physical and social dominance
- Baseline F0: 112.7 Hz; Competitive F0: 113.2 Hz (mean)
- F0 range of competitive recordings: 85.6-154.6 Hz

## Key Quantitative Findings

### Pitch Manipulation Effects on Dominance
- **Main effect of pitch on dominance:** F(1,110)=128.53, p<.0001
- Lowered pitch -> significantly higher physical AND social dominance ratings
- **Interaction pitch x dominance type:** F(1,110)=30.85, p<.0001
  - Pitch manipulation had GREATER effect on physical dominance than social dominance
- Physical dominance: t(110)=12.40, p<.0001
- Social dominance: t(110)=7.20, p<.0001

### Formant Effects of Pitch Manipulation
CoolEdit shifted formants along with F0:
- Mean F1 shift between raised/lowered: 54.7 Hz
- Mean F2 shift: 92.7 Hz
- Mean F3 shift: 199.9 Hz
- Mean F4 shift: 283.0 Hz
- Formant dispersion change: 76.1 Hz, F(1,11)=16.9, p=.002
- Higher formants shifted more than lower (interaction: F(3,11)=6.42, p=.002)

### Pitch Modulation in Response to Dominance
- No overall F0 change from baseline (112.7) to competition (113.2): t(110)=0.93, p=.352
- BUT: self-rated physical dominance predicted F0 change: rho=-0.27, p=.004
  - **Physically dominant men LOWERED F0**: mean change = -2.08 Hz
  - **Physically subordinate men RAISED F0**: mean change = +1.94 Hz
  - Difference (~4 Hz) significant: t(77)=2.55, p=.006
  - Exceeds JND for male voice pitch range
- Social dominance did NOT predict F0 modulation: rho=0.07, p=.454

### Correlations (Unmanipulated Voices)
- F0 vs physical dominance: r=-0.176, p=.064 (marginal)
- F0 vs social dominance: r=-0.150, p=.106 (n.s.)

### Mating Success
- Lower F0 vs more sexual partners: r=-0.16, p=.112 (trend, n.s.)

## Effect Size Comparison (from Discussion)
- 2-semitone pitch change: effect on physical dominance is ~15x greater than effect on sexual attractiveness (eta-squared)

## Implementation Relevance
- For dominant male voice: lower F0 by 1-2 semitones (and shift formants proportionally)
- Physical dominance signaled more by pitch than social dominance
- Natural F0 modulation range in competitive context: ~4 Hz (from +2 to -2)
- Formant dispersion reduction co-occurs with pitch lowering in natural speech
