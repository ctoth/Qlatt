A textbook chapter from "Noise and Acoustic Fatigue in Aeronautics" covering fundamentals of aerodynamic sound radiation including monopole/dipole/quadrupole source types, radiation impedance, acoustic power, and Lighthill's aeroacoustic analogy. The content is entirely focused on aeroacoustic noise from jets, propellers, and turbulence. Not relevant to speech synthesis or the Qlatt project — likely collected by mistake.
