## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Pierrehumbert (1980)** — The foundational AM analysis of English intonation. The phonological primitives (H\*, L+H\*, etc.) that ToBI adopted come directly from this dissertation. Essential reference for understanding what ToBI labels mean.

2. **Beckman, Hirschberg, and Shattuck-Hufnagel (2005)** — The definitive ToBI specification that Ladd is critiquing. Already in collection as `Beckman_2005_ToBISystemEvolution`.

3. **Bolinger (1961)** — Introduces the concept of "gradience" — meaningful gradient variation within phonological categories. Central to Ladd's argument that F0 variation should not be forced into discrete bins.

4. **Pitrelli, Beckman, and Hirschberg (1994)** — The inter-transcriber reliability evaluation of ToBI. Critical empirical evidence that L+H\* vs. H\* is unreliably distinguished by human annotators, supporting Ladd's gradience argument.

5. **Silverman et al. (1992)** — The original published ToBI standard. Already in collection as `Silverman_1992_ToBILabelingProsody`.
