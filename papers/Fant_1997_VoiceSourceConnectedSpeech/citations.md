## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Fant et al. (1985)** - "A four-parameter model of glottal flow" - The original LF-model paper. Essential foundation for understanding all the parameters discussed.

2. **Fant (1995)** - "The LF-model revisited" - More detailed treatment of transformed parameters and frequency domain analysis. Provides deeper background for Rd parameter.

3. **Klatt & Klatt (1990)** - "Analysis, synthesis and perception of voice quality variations" - Links voice quality perception to synthesis parameters. Defines OQi used in this paper.

4. **Hanson (1997)** - "Glottal characteristics of female speakers" - Female voice modeling. Complements this paper's male-focused analysis.

5. **Gobl (1988)** - "Voice source dynamics in connected speech" - Detailed data on glottal parameter variations across segments. Source for much of the coarticulation data.
