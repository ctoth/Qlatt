Wendahl uses an electrical laryngeal analog (LADIC) to synthesize sawtooth stimuli with controlled cycle-to-cycle frequency perturbation (jitter) at two median F0 values (100 Hz and 200 Hz), with deviations ranging from 0 to +/-10 Hz. 536 listeners judged roughness via paired comparisons, establishing that even 1 Hz jitter around 100 Hz F0 is perceptible as rough, and that roughness scales monotonically with jitter magnitude. The results also demonstrate an F0-dependent interaction: identical absolute jitter at lower F0 produces greater perceived roughness, predicting that male voices will sound harsher than female voices for equal frequency perturbation.
