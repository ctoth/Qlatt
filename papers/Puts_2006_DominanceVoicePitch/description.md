Experimental study of voice pitch manipulation (1 semitone up/down) and its effect on male dominance ratings. Shows that lower pitch increases perceived physical dominance more than social dominance. Also demonstrates that men who believe they are physically dominant lower their F0 when addressing competitors, while subordinate men raise it. Key evidence for pitch as honest signal of fighting ability.
