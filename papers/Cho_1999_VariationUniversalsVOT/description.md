Cross-linguistic study of voice onset time (VOT) across 18 languages from the UCLA endangered languages project, establishing that VOT varies systematically with place of articulation (velar > alveolar > bilabial) due to aerodynamic and articulatory factors. The authors identify four approximate VOT categories for voiceless stops (~10-30 ms unaspirated, ~30-50 ms slightly aspirated, ~60-90 ms aspirated, ~90-160 ms highly aspirated) and show that while place effects are largely universal, the absolute VOT values chosen by each language are language-specific. The paper provides comprehensive VOT data tables and reviews six physiological mechanisms (cavity volume, articulator velocity, contact area, glottal opening, closure-aspiration trade-off) that explain the universal place-of-articulation effect on VOT.
