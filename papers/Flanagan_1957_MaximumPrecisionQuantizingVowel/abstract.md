# Abstract

Results of psychoacoustic experiments are used to estimate the greatest precision necessary in quantizing narrow band-width data specifying the vowel sounds of speech.

## Interpretation

This short letter-to-the-editor synthesizes Flanagan's own DL experiments (formant frequency DL, F0 DL, formant amplitude DL, overall amplitude DL) with known formant/F0 ranges to compute the maximum number of quantization levels and bits needed to encode vowel parameters. The key insight is that differential discrimination on steady-state stimuli provides conservative upper bounds on perceptual precision requirements, since speech perception operates more like absolute judgment and connected speech provides less discriminability than isolated steady states. The resulting ~16-20 bits per vowel frame (~300-400 bits/sec at 20 Hz sampling) became a benchmark for speech compression system design.
