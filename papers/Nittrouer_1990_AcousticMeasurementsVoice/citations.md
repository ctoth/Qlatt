## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Klatt & Klatt (1990)** - Already in collection. The primary reference for voice quality parameters (AH, TL, etc.) in Klatt synthesis; this paper's findings directly extend it.
2. **Gobl (1988)** - Consonantal context effects on excitation strength persisting into the vowel; would inform how long to sustain post-consonantal source modifications in synthesis rules.
3. **Lofqvist & McGowan (1989)** - Peak flow, minimum flow, and open quotient differences after voiced vs. voiceless consonants lasting 20+ pitch periods; critical for coarticulatory duration rules.
4. **Monsen & Engebretson (1977)** - Male/female glottal wave study; complementary data for sex-differentiated voice source modeling.
5. **Milenkovic (1987)** - LMS voice perturbation measures; the measurement methodology underlying this paper's SNR and perturbation analyses.
