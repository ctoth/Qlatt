## Key Citations (Most Relevant to Qlatt)

- **Klatt & Klatt 1990**: H1-H2 and H1-A1 as glottalization diagnostics — directly relevant to Klatt synthesizer voice quality parameters
- **Pierrehumbert & Talkin 1992**: Glottalization of /h/ and glottal stop — lenition/glottalization rules at prosodic boundaries
- **Pierrehumbert & Frisch 1997**: Synthesizing allophonic glottalization — synthesis implementation strategies
- **Dilley, Shattuck-Hufnagel & Ostendorf 1996**: Glottalization of word-initial vowels as function of prosodic structure — rule conditions
- **Pierrehumbert 1995**: Prosodic effects on glottal allophones — prosodic conditioning
- **Berry 2001**: Mechanisms of modal and nonmodal phonation — companion paper in same volume
- **Hanson et al. 2001**: Towards models of phonation — companion paper with acoustic modeling
- **Gerratt & Kreiman 2001**: Taxonomy of nonmodal phonation — companion paper on categorization
- **Stevens 1977, 1994**: Physics of laryngeal behavior, prosodic influences on glottal waveform
- **Hillenbrand & Houde 1996**: f0 drop sufficient for perception of glottal stop
