## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Klatt 1990 [7]** — Already in collection. Voice quality variations among male and female talkers; the primary reference for implementing breathiness/voice quality in the Klatt synthesizer.
- **Henton & Bladon 1985 [6]** — Quantifies breathiness in normal female speech and the trade-off between attractiveness signaling and intelligibility. Directly relevant for setting spectral tilt ranges.
- **Ohala 1984 [10]** — Foundational "frequency code" theory linking F0 to perceived body size across languages. Provides the theoretical basis for size projection in prosody design.
- **Scherer 2003 [11]** — Comprehensive review of vocal emotion communication; useful for understanding how attractiveness cues overlap with emotional expression parameters.
- **Xu, Kelly & Smillie [14]** — Bio-informational dimensions theory that frames size projection, dynamicity, audibility, and association as the core dimensions of vocal emotional expression.
