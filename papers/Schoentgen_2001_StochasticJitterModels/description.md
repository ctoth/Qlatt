Presents five stochastic models of fundamental frequency jitter based on the ribbon model of vocal fold vibration, combining correlation-free disturbances with microtremor (physiological low-frequency oscillation at ~5 Hz). The models range from a simple random walk on one glottal wall to a full coupled left-right, upper-lower margin system with AR(2) microtremor generation. Simulation results show that microtremor dominates normal jitter, decorrelated perturbations are stationary and Gaussian, and the statistical properties (autocorrelation, variogram shape, AR model order) match observed sustained vowels. The framework provides a principled method for synthesizing realistic jitter for both normal and pathological voice qualities.
