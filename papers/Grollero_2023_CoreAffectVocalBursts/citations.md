## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Cowen et al. (2019)** — Source of majority of vocal burst stimuli; establishes 24-emotion taxonomy for nonlinguistic vocalizations. Foundational for any vocal burst synthesis work.
2. **Kuppens et al. (2013)** — Comprehensive review of the V-shaped valence-arousal relationship across multiple modalities and datasets. Essential background for affect modeling.
3. **Banse & Scherer (1996)** — Acoustic profiles for 14 emotions with 29 acoustic parameters. Directly relevant for mapping emotion categories to synthesis parameters.
4. **Holz et al. (2021)** — Paradoxical role of emotional intensity in vocal affect perception. Important for understanding how synthesis amplitude/intensity interacts with perceived emotion.
5. **Laukka et al. (2005)** — Dimensional approach to vocal emotion expression with acoustic correlates. Provides acoustic parameter targets for valence-arousal space.
