## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Titze, I. R. (1988).** "The physics of small-amplitude oscillation of the vocal folds," JASA 83, 1536–1552. Supplies Eq. (9), the mean intraglottal driving pressure in terms of $a_2/a_1$, with the full derivation on p. 1542. Required to implement level-2 interaction.
- **Fletcher, N. H. (1993).** "Autonomous vibration of simple pressure-controlled valves in gas flows," JASA 93, 2172–2180. Eqs. (19)–(20) on p. 2176 are solved directly in this paper to predict oscillation frequency shifts and threshold pressures under reactive load.
- **Titze, I. R. (2006b).** *The Myoelastic-Aerodynamic Theory of Phonation.* Chaps. 2–6 hold the fiber-gel tissue model, the modified Bernoulli with flow separation and jet formulation, the wave-reflection analog, and the tissue-mode nomenclature ($x_{10}$, $x_{11}$) this paper depends on throughout.
- **Titze, I. R., Riede, T., and Popolo, P. (2008).** "Nonlinear source-filter coupling in phonation: Vocal exercises," JASA 123, 1902–1915. The companion human-subject paper supplying the empirical F0-jump magnitudes and the male/female instability asymmetry.
- **Story, B. H., Titze, I. R., and Hoffman, E. A. (1996).** "Vocal tract area functions from magnetic resonance imaging," JASA 100, 537–554. The MRI area functions that make accurate epilarynx geometry, and therefore correct coupling strength, possible.
