**Venue:** Springer, Prosody, Phonology and Phonetics series

## One-Sentence Summary
An edited volume of 17 chapters covering the acoustic correlates, perceptual dimensions, and social consequences of voice attractiveness, likeability, and charisma, organized across five parts: general considerations, voice quality, prosody, databases, and technological applications.

## Problem Addressed
Voice attractiveness research is interdisciplinary but fragmented across phonetics, evolutionary biology, social psychology, and speech technology. This volume consolidates the state of the art across these domains, addressing how vocal features map to social impressions of attractiveness, likeability, and charisma.

## Key Contributions
- Consolidates research on three related but distinct vocal social dimensions: attractiveness, likeability, and charisma
- Covers the full pipeline from production (voice quality, prosody) through perception (listener preferences, ratings) to application (robot voice design, uncanny valley effects)
- Includes both acoustic analysis chapters and perceptual/social evaluation chapters

### Part IV: Databases (Chapters 13-15)

17. **Exploring Verbal Uncanny Valley Effects with Vague Language in Computer Speech** (Clark, Ofemile, Cowan) - p.323 - Uncanny valley in synthetic speech

## Parameters
This is an edited volume; no single parameter table applies. Key acoustic parameters discussed across chapters include:
- F0 (mean, range, variability) as correlates of attractiveness/charisma
- Formant frequencies and formant dispersion as vocal tract length indicators
- Voice quality measures (H1-H2, jitter, shimmer, HNR)
- Speech rate and amplitude modulation
- Spectral tilt

## Relevance to Project
This volume is a reference collection for the speaker personality/voice profile research effort. Several individual chapters overlap with papers already in the collection (Quene_2021, Babel_2014, Kreiman_2007, Burkhardt_2009). The book's primary value is as a comprehensive overview of the voice attractiveness/likeability/charisma literature, connecting acoustic parameters to social perception dimensions. For Qlatt voice preset design, chapters on prosodic correlates (Ch.2, 9, 10) and voice quality (Ch.5, 8) are most directly relevant. The technological applications section (Ch.16-17) addresses synthetic voice perception directly.
