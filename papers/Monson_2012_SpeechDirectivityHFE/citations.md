## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Chu and Warnock (2002)** - Detailed 3D directivity of human talkers (n=40), most comprehensive pre-2012 study with data up to 8 kHz third-octave band
2. **Cabrera et al. (2011)** - Opera singer directivity with simultaneous horizontal recordings, comparable methodology
3. **Flanagan (1960)** - Foundational radiation measurements from the mouth; analog model of head-as-sphere directivity
4. **Monson (2011)** - Doctoral dissertation with extended HFE analysis (basis for this paper and Monson 2014 review)
