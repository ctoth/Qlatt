## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Rosenberg & Hirschberg (Ch.2)** - Prosodic aspects of attractive voice. Julia Hirschberg is a major prosody researcher; this chapter likely contains useful prosodic parameter data.
2. **Kreiman, Auszmann & Gerratt (Ch.5)** - Normal voice definition. Kreiman's voice quality work is already highly relevant to the collection.
3. **Belin (Ch.8)** - Voice averaging and attractiveness. Pascal Belin's voice perception work (temporal voice areas) connects to Staib_2021_CorticalVoiceProcessing in our collection.
4. **Torre & White (Ch.16)** - Trust in robot voice design. Directly relevant to synthetic voice persona design.
5. **Obuchi (Ch.15)** - Computational modeling of voice attractiveness from audio. Most directly implementable chapter.
