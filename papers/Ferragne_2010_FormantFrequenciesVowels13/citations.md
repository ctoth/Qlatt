## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **D'Arcy, Russell, Browning & Tomlinson (2004)** — original ABI corpus description; needed to understand the corpus's recording protocol and regional sampling in full.
- **Ferragne (2008)** PhD dissertation — the fuller phonetic analysis this JIPA article explicitly summarizes; likely has more detail (and possibly female-speaker data) than this article.
- **Hawkins & Midgley (2005)** — apparent-time RP formant data across age groups; directly load-bearing for this paper's drag-chain hypothesis discussion.
- **Wells (1982) Accents of English** — the descriptive baseline cited for virtually every accent in this paper; essential background for interpreting any per-accent claim here.
- **Scobbie, Hewlett & Turk (1999)** — foundational Scottish Vowel Length Rule paper, needed to fully understand the gla/shl/uls duration discussion.
