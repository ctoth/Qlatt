## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Feinberg et al 2005b** — Male voice F0/formant manipulation and attractiveness; directly relevant for designing male voice presets
- **Abitbol et al 1999** — Hormonal basis for F0 variation in female voice; provides physiological grounding for pitch ranges
- **Fitch 1997** — Formant dispersion (Fdisp) metric used here; relevant for vocal tract length modeling in the synthesizer
- **Charpentier & Moulines 1989** — PSOLA algorithm for pitch manipulation; reference for any future speech manipulation tools
- **Zuckerman & Driver 1989** — "What sounds beautiful is good" vocal attractiveness stereotype; broader context for voice quality targets
