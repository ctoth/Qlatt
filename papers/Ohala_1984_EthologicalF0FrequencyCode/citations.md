- **Morton 1977** — motivation-structural rules in bird/mammal sounds; foundational for frequency code across species
- **Bolinger 1964, 1978** — intonation universals across languages; F0 contour patterns for questions/statements
- **Williams & Stevens 1972** — acoustic correlates of emotions in speech (F0, spectral tilt)
- **Scherer et al. 1973** — paralinguistic cues (F0) and perceived confidence
- **Sapir 1929** — phonetic symbolism experiments (size-frequency correspondence)
- **Goldstein 1980** — sexual dimorphism in vocal anatomy growth curves
- **Lieberman 1967** — intonation, perception and language
