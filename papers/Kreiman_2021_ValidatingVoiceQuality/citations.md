## Key Citations for Follow-up

1. **Garellek, Samlan, Gerratt, and Kreiman (2016)** — "Modeling the voice source in terms of spectral slopes," JASA 139, 1404-1410. Derives and validates the four-piece spectral slope parameterization of the harmonic source that this paper uses. Essential companion paper for understanding the source model.

2. **Kreiman, Gerratt, Garellek, Samlan, and Zhang (2014)** — "Toward a unified theory of voice production and perception," Loquens. Original proposal of the psychoacoustic model validated here. Defines the parameter set and theoretical framework.

3. **Kreiman, Gerratt, and Antonanzas-Barroso (2007)** — "Measures of the glottal source spectrum," JSHLR 50, 595-610. PCA analysis showing that voice source measures reduce to four independent factors. Already in collection as Kreiman_2007_GlottalSourceSpectrum.

4. **Signorello, Rhee, Gerratt, and Kreiman (2016)** — Psychoacoustic model of spectral noise in the voice source. Complements the harmonic model with the noise source component.

5. **de Krom (1993)** — Cepstral HNR estimation technique. The method used to separate harmonic and inharmonic components of the voice signal. Standard technique for noise source extraction.
