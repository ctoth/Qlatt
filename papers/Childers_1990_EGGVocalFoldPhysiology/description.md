Childers et al. (1990) validate the relationship between EGG waveform features and vocal fold vibratory events using synchronized ultra-high-speed laryngeal films from 4 normal male subjects and 8 patients with vocal disorders. They confirm that the DEGG negative peak reliably marks glottal closure (within 0.05 ms), while the positive peak less reliably marks glottal opening, and demonstrate that OQ and jitter (RAP) can be estimated from EGG with reasonable accuracy. The paper also presents a mathematical model relating EGG to vocal fold contact area (EGG = k/[A(t) + C]) and documents how glottal area shape varies with F0.
